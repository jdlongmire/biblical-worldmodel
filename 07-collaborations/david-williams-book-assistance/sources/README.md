## New material, 2026-09-16 / 2026-09-23

Six scan packages from David Williams (Genius Scan PDFs), filed with faithful `.md` transcriptions of his handwriting (same basename; transcriptions mark `[illegible]`, `[struck: ...]`, `[marginalia: ...]`, no silent normalization):

- `2026-09-16-oil-and-gas-seep-notes-viii-a.pdf` — "Oil & Gas Seep Notes": David's seepage literature-review notes for outline section VIII-A (Stanley & Lillis, Clark/Leifer/Washburn Coal Oil Point, Quigley & Hornafius, AAPG Hedberg migration mechanisms, *Oil in the Sea* tables, photocopied inserts), with his red-ink TCF-scale extrapolations. Scanned by Victoria Williams, forwarded by David 2026-09-16.
- `2026-09-23-complete-basic-outline-and-thesis.pdf` — David's complete basic outline (I–IX, showing where oil and gas seeps fit) plus his two thesis statements (primary migration unexplained after 150+ years; data fits GFS/RCS better than old-earth uniformitarianism) and oil-window reference notes.
- `2026-09-23-corrected-detail-outline-to-vii-e.pdf` — David's red-ink corrected detail outline through VII-E: a printout of the 2026-09-15 transcription with renumbered sub-lists, corrected sand-lens entries (Powder River–Muddy SS, Delaware–Atoka/Morrow, Michigan shoestring, Niobrara, Silurian reefs), struck text and marginalia.
- `2026-09-23-viii-b-hydrocarbon-microseepage.pdf` — VIII-B "Hydrocarbon Microseepage & Geochemical Exploration": microseepage literature notes (Schumacher, Jedesen rapid seep rates, iodine vs. soil-gas surveys, Dolley/Keota/Leyden fields) plus the 2004 Luca Technologies biogenic-methane press release.
- `2026-09-23-viii-c-diffusion-models-and-rates.pdf` — VIII-C "Diffusion Models & Rates" (David's sheets 1–22; first 10 pages of the scan repeat the VIII-B material, noted structurally not re-transcribed).
- `2026-09-23-viii-c-rest.pdf` — "the rest of VIII-C" via Drive link: VIII-C bibliography (items 1–18), Nelson 1993 CSM thesis notes on caprock diffusion/turnover times, and photocopied thesis tables 1–9 (per-field flux/turnover numbers). Pages 1–10 substantially duplicate the separately emailed VIII-B package.

Double-spaced DOCX review copies of all six transcriptions live in `exports/` (rendered from the `.md` masters; Times New Roman 12, black, double-spaced, page numbers).
