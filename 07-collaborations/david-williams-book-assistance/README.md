## Purpose

Working area for assisting David Williams with development of his hydrocarbon migration and source-rock book. This collaboration is adjacent to BWM geology research but preserves authorship and argument ownership with David Williams.

## Current thesis under development

The working argument is that very low-permeability source rocks such as the Barnett and Bakken require hydrocarbons to have formed in situ, while significant migration out of source rocks must have occurred before compaction and diagenesis reduced permeability. The broader field catalog is intended to test the proposition that trap timing is a major control on productivity, including cases of early migration, diagenetic trapping, isolated systems, and traps barren because they formed too late.

## Proposed manuscript structure

1. Introduction: why timing, not just structure, decides whether a trap holds oil
