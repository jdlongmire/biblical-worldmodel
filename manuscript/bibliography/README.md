# Bibliography Governance

The manuscript bibliography is built from the source-verification ledger, not from unverified inherited citations.

## Requirements

- Harvard-type author-year treatment unless publication requirements later dictate otherwise.
- Primary sources preferred.
- Page/section/location information retained during research even when final citation style does not require it.
- Scripture: ESV, within applicable quotation limits.
- Secondary disagreement is reported where materially relevant.
- Bibliographic records must remain traceable to the manuscript claim they support.
