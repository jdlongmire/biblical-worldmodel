## Types

- exegesis
- observation/evidence
- inference
- model
- philosophy
- worldview/presupposition

## Dispositions

- **Resolved** — demonstrable factual, categorical, exegetical, or logical error.
- **Answered** — legitimate concern adequately addressed by available evidence or argument.
- **Burden Remains** — “This objection does not defeat the argument, but it does identify a genuine explanatory burden that remains.”
- **Unresolved** — no presently satisfactory response; remains evidence against the relevant claim or model.

## Control rule

Author-preferred models receive the same objection treatment as competing models. No objection may be downgraded merely because it challenges BWM, DFM, PFH, CHFM, Methodological Designism, LRT, TRT, or another preferred framework.
