# Two-page entry template and acceptance criteria

**Status:** Retained as the template of record for Spread 001 (BWM-IL-001 / 002). For new production, the single-page plate template (`plate-template.md`) supersedes this spread-first template, per JD's adoption of the plate style guide on 2026-11-13.

## Metadata

Entry ID; title; part; author/editor; revision; claim status; source repositories; illustration status.

## Left page

Title ribbon; 100–150-word orientation; hero plate; numbered callouts; concise structural summary.

## Right page

One or two detailed explanatory panels; 150–250 words of narrative; Scripture references; explanatory legend; small interpretive-status box.

## Claim classes

**T** textual datum; **E** exegetical inference; **O** observation; **M** model assumption/inference; **H** hypothesis; **U** unresolved. Mark confidence and cite primary sources at claim level. An artistic visualization must not silently upgrade H or M to T or O.

## Editorial gates

- Scripture references and exact ESV wording checked against a licensed/authorized source and quotation limits.
- Scientific and historical assertions traced to primary sources or explicitly labeled otherwise.
- Original artwork; accurate diagrams; no hallucinated labels; no unverified maps.
- Text typeset separately from generated art; accessible minimum print type size.
- Printer proof for bleed, gutter, contrast, and image resolution.
- Independent review before final publication.
