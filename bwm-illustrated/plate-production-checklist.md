# Plate production checklist

One checklist per plate, worked in order. The companion record in `illustrated-plates/` is where completion is evidenced; this file is the reusable procedure. Style authority: `plate-style-guide.md`. Format authority: `plate-template.md` and `production-specification.md`.

## 1. Brief

- [ ] Subject, biblical reference range, and plate-series position fixed.
- [ ] Claim ledger drafted: every intended label classified T, E, O, M, or H, with primary sources named.
- [ ] Model content (times, counts, mechanisms) identified as model in the brief itself.

## 2. Artwork

- [ ] Art generated or commissioned to the style guide: ivory ground, engraved linework, muted palette, thin sepia frame, negative space.
- [ ] Art-only master: no baked-in text beyond incidental lettering that will be covered or cropped by typesetting; no plate number; no internal programme codes.
- [ ] Master at production resolution: 3600 x 2700 px minimum at the 12 x 9 in trim (300 ppi), before bleed.
- [ ] Figure safeguards checked: no human likeness of Christ; no depiction of God the Father; pre-Fall figures dignified per the style guide; no implied geocentrism or biblically specified ethnicity.
- [ ] Provenance recorded: generation tool or artist, date, prompts or brief, human edits.

## 3. Typesetting

- [ ] All headings, quotations, labels, callouts, and footnotes typeset in an editable layout, separate from the art.
- [ ] Running header carries series line, subject line, and biblical reference; no plate number.
- [ ] Reconstructed times and estimates carry their model labels on the plate.
- [ ] Interpretive status compartment present and accurate against the claim ledger.

## 4. Verification

- [ ] Every quotation checked word-for-word against an authorised ESV source; attribution and verse range exact. Anything unverified is paraphrased and cited instead.
- [ ] Citations, arithmetic, dates, and counts independently rechecked.
- [ ] Lettering audit: the composed plate read at full size and by magnified crop readback of every text block; defects logged in the companion record, never silently patched.

## 5. Review gates

- [ ] Scripture and exegesis review.
- [ ] Theological review.
- [ ] Scientific and historical review where the plate makes such claims.
- [ ] Visual accuracy check (morphology, anatomy, perspective, clipping).

## 6. Proofs

- [ ] Proof rendered at real print size (12 x 9 in) and read at arm's length.
- [ ] Proof rendered at mobile screen width and read on the sub-site pattern.
- [ ] Bleed, safe areas, and contrast verified against the production specification.

## 7. Filing and publication

- [ ] Art master and companion record filed in `illustrated-plates/`; register row added or updated in the same change.
- [ ] Web copy published to the Illustrated Atlas sub-site with a status note matching the record.
- [ ] Status stated exactly at every step (concept prototype, in review, approved); no print-ready claim before gates 4 to 6 pass.
