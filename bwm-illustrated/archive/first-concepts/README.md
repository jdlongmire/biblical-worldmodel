# Archive - First Concepts

This folder holds the first round of BWM Illustrated concept material, archived on 2026-11-13 at JD's direction when production restarted from the beginning in the ruled format: individual landscape page plates, 12 x 9 in trim, under the adopted plate style guide, with no plate numbers on the artwork.

The files here are the original filed copies, moved unchanged (identical blobs) from `illustrated-plates/`. Their companion records are preserved exactly as filed, including relative references that describe their original location; this index is the map of where they now live. Nothing here is current production, and none of it is print-ready. The BWM-IL numbers 000 to 005 are retired with these items and will not be reused.

## Why these were superseded

- They are portrait concepts; the ruled production format is landscape.
- The day plates print plate numbers in their headers, against the 2026-11-13 ruling.
- The cover is a rendered book mock, not flat cover art.
- Production restarted with a generated landscape candidate for Day 1 (BWM-IL-006), approved by JD as the style benchmark for the new line.

The current register is [Illustrated Plates - Index and Register](../illustrated-plates/README.md). The editorial questions these concepts raised (part names, chapter scheme, cover arc against the provisional contents) lapse with the restart and will be settled afresh in the new production contents.
