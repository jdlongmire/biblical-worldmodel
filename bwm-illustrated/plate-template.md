# Single-page plate template and acceptance criteria

**Status:** The production format from 2026-11-13, per JD's adoption of the plate style guide (`plate-style-guide.md`) with its amendments. This template supersedes the spread-first template for new production; `spread-template.md` is retained as the template of record for Spread 001 (BWM-IL-001 / 002).

## The page

One plate per page. Trim 12 x 9 in landscape (see `production-specification.md`): art master 3600 x 2700 px at 300 ppi before bleed. Composition follows the style guide: editorial page, abundant ivory negative space, thin sepia frame, aligned grid, no ornament competing with the illustration.

## Page anatomy

1. **Running header:** THE BIBLICAL WORLD MODEL and the subject line (for example CREATION: THE SIX DAYS), plus the precise biblical reference. No plate number (JD ruling, 2026-11-13; numbering lives in the companion record, the register, and captions).
2. **Title banner:** restrained engraved banner - subject day or theme in display capitals, subject title, italic subtitle.
3. **Source text:** a brief quotation only if exact wording and attribution are independently verified (ESV preferred); otherwise paraphrase with citation. Never invent a quotation.
4. **Dominant illustration:** one engraved, hand-tinted central image, roughly a third to a half of the page height, composed for the landscape field.
5. **Chronology or process strip:** a horizontal sequence of small illustrated steps (five is the pattern set by the Creation plates). Reconstructed times are labelled on the plate as a proposed model.
6. **Bottom compartments:** two to four small panels chosen to suit the plate - Key Scripture; DFM Perspective; Model Estimates; Key Insights / Interpretive Status. Do not force every panel into every plate; preserve hierarchy, legibility, and visual calm.
7. **Footer (optional):** a concise motto or verified quotation with attribution, and adequate whitespace below it.

## Companion record (metadata)

Every plate is filed with a companion record in `illustrated-plates/` carrying the front matter and notes the register procedure specifies: record ID, title, plate series position, day or subject, Scripture, source asset with dimensions, bytes, and SHA-256, provenance, print status, claim status (section 4 of the style guide), QA findings, and review gates. The plate's claims are classified there at claim level: T, E, O, M, H, U.

## Acceptance criteria

A plate advances only when the production checklist (`plate-production-checklist.md`) is complete for its stage: art-only master separated from typesetting; scripture verified; lettering audit passed by magnified crop readback; reviews recorded; proofs rendered at print size and at mobile screen width. Statuses are stated exactly - concept prototype, in review, approved - and never smoothed over.
