# Creative direction, v0.1

## Format

Ruled by JD on 2026-11-13: **12 x 9 in landscape hardcover**; premium matte stock. The production unit is the individual landscape page plate (see `plate-style-guide.md` and `plate-template.md`); two-page spreads were the first published iteration (Spread 001) and remain part of the book's published record. Pagination, production economics, and printer are undecided.

## Visual vocabulary

Victorian scientific encyclopedia meets Renaissance inventor's folio: ivory parchment, finely engraved sepia linework, restrained hand-coloring (ultramarine, ochre, forest green, rust), ornamental title cartouches, small schematic insets, numbered leader lines, sectional diagrams, and classic serif typography. Steampunk is a **visual metaphor**, not an assertion about historical technology. Refined by the adopted plate style guide (2026-11-13): antique ivory natural-history register, restrained chroma, thin sepia framing, and abundant negative space. Where this draft and `plate-style-guide.md` differ, the style guide governs.

## Composition

For new production, composition follows the single-page plate template (`plate-template.md`): one landscape page carrying header, banner, dominant illustration, chronology strip, and bottom compartments. The two-page composition below is retained as the record of the Spread 001 approach.

Left page: principal illustration, introductory text, structural diagram. Right page: mechanisms or sequence, detail views, evidence/interpretation labels, Scripture and synthesis. Keep critical labels out of the gutter. Use consistent callout numbering, scale and legible type.

## Representational safeguards

No human depiction of Christ. Distinguish literal history, artist's reconstruction, conceptual diagrams, and speculative mechanisms in captions. Avoid treating metaphorical machinery as actual mechanisms. Do not reproduce the photographed reference book's layouts, illustrations, or text. Commission or generate original artwork.

## Production workflow

1. Approve a claim-level editorial brief and source ledger.
2. Produce clean artwork without embedded long-form AI text.
3. Typeset all headings, quotations, labels, and footnotes in an editable layout.
4. Perform exegetical, historical, technical, and copy-edit checks.
5. Proof print at final physical size and verify readability, bleed, and gutter.
