First plate of the Earth Systems topical line of the atlas, supplied
by the author and filed exactly as supplied.

- The plate treats the earth's heat budget (radiogenic, primordial,
  and core heat) and what dynamic, high-heat processes imply for
  radiometric age extrapolations.

  directly stated in Scripture (a created, ordered, and productive

  standard published values (class E); the hydrotectonics framing is
  the model (class M/H), labelled as such on the plate.
