# BWM-IL-019 - Design vs. "Timergence" (landscape plate)

Second plate of the Life Systems topical line of the atlas, supplied
by the author and filed exactly as supplied.

- The plate frames the origins question as a contest of explanations:
  did life's complex, purposeful features come from a Mind (design),
  or from blind processes given enough time ("timergence")?
- Its centrepiece is the bacterial flagellum, labelled a natural
  molecular motor, with a magnified view of the motor assembly.
- Its conclusion panel, The Inference, states the design inference in
  terms of uniform experience: when we see complex, functional,
  information-rich systems, the reasonable inference is design by a
  mind, not mindless processes plus time.
- The plate cites no Scripture and follows a topical comparison
  structure rather than the day-plate template; both are recorded in
  its filing audit.
