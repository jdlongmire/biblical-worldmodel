# BWM-IL-020 - The Fall and the Lapsarian Covenant (landscape plate)

First plate of the Fall and Covenant topical line of the atlas,
supplied by the author and filed exactly as supplied.

- The plate sets out the author's covenant reading of the Fall: the
  eternal Covenant of Grace, purposed in eternity, enters time and
  is instituted at the Fall (Gen. 3:15) as the Lapsarian Covenant,
  assuring redemption for a people in Adam, fulfilled in Christ, the
  last Adam.
- Its right panels carry the Protoevangelium, Genesis 3:15 quoted in
  the ESV, with a definition and the fourfold account of the breach.
- Its Covenantal Timeline runs Creation, the Fall, the Lapsarian
  Covenant, and Fulfillment in Christ; its Parallel Covenants panel
  sets the Covenant of Works (pre-Fall) beside the Lapsarian
  Covenant of Grace (post-Fall).
- Its filing audit records one defect in the supplied artwork: the
  Key Features panel reads "foogiveness" for "forgiveness". The
  plate is filed verbatim with the defect standing, at the
  author's decision (2026-11-20).
