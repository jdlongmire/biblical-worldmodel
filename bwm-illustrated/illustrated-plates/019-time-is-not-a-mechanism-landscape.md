# BWM-IL-019 - Time Is Not a Mechanism (landscape plate)

Second plate of the Life Systems topical line of the atlas, supplied
by the author and filed exactly as supplied. It replaces the Design
vs. "Timergence" plate originally filed under this number, at the
author's direction before that filing merged.

- The plate defines the author's coined term: timergence is the
  critique of appeals to sufficient time, chance, trials, complexity,
  emergence, or multiple universes that assume an origin event is
  plausible without demonstrating causally sufficient mechanisms.
- Its centre panels draw the distinction the title states: time is a
  dimension of sequence and duration and by itself creates nothing;
  a causal mechanism is a specific, operative process that must be
  shown sufficient to its outcome.
- Its Key Questions for Origins strip puts the same question to five
  domains: cosmos, origin of life, biological innovation,
  consciousness, and moral normativity.
- Its evidence test runs in three steps (identify the mechanism;
  demonstrate causal sufficiency; test conditions, time and
  probability), and its key takeaway is the plate's thesis in one
  line: possibility is not demonstration.
- Its filing audit records one defect in the supplied artwork: the
  Cosmos panel reads "life-permiiting" for "life-permitting". The
  plate is filed verbatim with the defect standing, pending the
  author's direction on a repair.
