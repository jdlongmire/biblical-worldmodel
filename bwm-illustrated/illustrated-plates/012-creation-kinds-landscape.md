# BWM-IL-012 - Creation Kinds (landscape plate)

First plate of the Life Systems topical line of the atlas, supplied
by the author and filed exactly as supplied.

- The plate sets out the biblical "kind" (Hebrew min): a created,
  reproductive design group, broader than the modern species and
  often mapping near the family level, with built-in potential for
  variation.
- Post-creation and post-Flood diversification within kinds produces
  the species and varieties observed today; the plate's examples are
  the canid, equid, and finch kinds, with the genetic and biological
  evidence panel alongside.
- Its interpretive summary keeps the series distinction: creation
  "according to their kinds" is what Scripture states (Gen. 1:11-25;
  6:19-20); the kind as a design grouping at about the family level,
  with inherent diversification potential inside a biblical time
  framework, is the interpretive model, labelled as such on the
  plate.
