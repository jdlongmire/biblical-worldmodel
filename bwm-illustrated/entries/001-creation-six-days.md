# Entry 001: The Creation of the World

**Subtitle:** The Six Days of Genesis 1

**Status:** Editorial prototype, not canonical interpretation.

## Opening narrative

Genesis begins with God creating the heavens and the earth (Genesis 1:1). The narrative unfolds through a sequence of divine commands, distinctions, and provisions. Light is introduced before the sun and moon are appointed as governors of the day and night. Land and vegetation precede the appearance of terrestrial creatures and humanity. The account culminates in God's rest on the seventh day (Genesis 2:1–3). The illustrations should make the narrative sequence visible while distinguishing what the text states from proposed explanations of how the world functioned during its earliest days.
