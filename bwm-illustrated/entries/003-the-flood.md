# Entry 003: The Flood: Narrative and Earth-History Models

**Status:** Draft editorial prototype. **Primary text:** Genesis 6–9.

## Spread thesis

The biblical Flood account supplies a historical narrative with construction, judgment, preservation, recession, and covenantal aftermath. Geological mechanisms proposed to explain the event belong to a distinct, testable model layer.

## Left plate: Ark and narrative sequence

Create an annotated wooden-vessel cutaway, clearly labeled **conceptual reconstruction**. Use only dimensions explicitly attributed to Genesis 6:15; do not invent compartment plans, machinery, or animal capacities as biblical facts. Small timeline panels: instruction, entry, rising waters, recession, exit.

## Right plate: Water and sediment

Pair a simplified hydrological schematic with a separate hypothetical stratigraphic cross-section. Mark the latter **model illustration**, not observed universal Flood stratigraphy. A legend distinguishes scriptural sequence, physical observations, and hydrotectonic hypotheses. Do not assert that a proposed model has been empirically established.
