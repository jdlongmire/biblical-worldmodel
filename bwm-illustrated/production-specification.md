# Production specification, v0.2

v0.2 (2026-11-13): trim ruled by JD - 12 x 9 in landscape. Draft 0.1 explored 14 x 11 in landscape and 12 x 12 in square; both are superseded by this ruling.

## Page system

Trim: 12 x 9 inches landscape. At 300 pixels/inch, a 12 x 9 page is 3600 x 2700 pixels before bleed. Two-page spread trim is 24 x 9 inches (7200 x 2700 pixels), for spread material. Printer-specific bleed and safe area are **TBD**; provisional bleed 0.125 inch (a full-bleed page file is then 3675 x 2775 pixels), provisional outside safe area 0.375 inch, and larger inner safe area 0.625 inch at a spread gutter. On single plates, keep critical content at least 0.375 inch from every edge. Do not place continuous critical text across the binding.

## Type hierarchy

Display heading 24–36 pt, subheads 12–16 pt, body 10.5–12 pt, callouts 9–10 pt minimum for print proof. Text must remain vector/editable; artwork is separate from typography. Footnotes and citations must be legible at actual trim size.

## Layers

1. Background paper and ornamental furniture.
2. Main illustration and diagram insets.
3. Numbered leaders and callout anchors.
4. Editable headings, paragraphs, legends, references, and status labels.
5. Production marks and optional printer bleed.

## Artwork and color

Generate art-only masters at final target dimensions or redraw as vector/print-resolution raster. Archive original source files, generation provenance, human edits, and usage rights. Use CMYK conversion and ICC proofing only after a printer is selected. Do not assume RGB concept PNGs are press-ready.

## Editorial quality assurance

Every plate receives (a) source ledger, (b) theological/exegetical review, (c) scientific/historical review when applicable, (d) visual accuracy check, (e) typography and physical proof. No claim of independent review before one occurs.
