# Scriptural Authority and Corrigible Apprehension

Biblical WorldModel treats Scripture as an authoritative explanatory source because its authority derives from its divine Giver rather than from the interpreter, institution, or model that appeals to it.

The Holy Spirit is the Giver and continuing Steward of the written Word. The canonical deposit is fixed. The Spirit's continuing ministry leads Christ's church in the apprehension, discernment, teaching, and application of that truth without enlarging the deposit.

This establishes an epistemic distinction required by BWM:

- **Revelation is complete.**
- **Scripture is objective.**
- **Human interpretation is corrigible.**
- **Apprehension can mature.**
- **Scientific and historical models are human constructions subject to testing and revision.**

Accordingly, an apparent conflict between Scripture and a scientific or historical model requires examination of both the model and our interpretation of Scripture. Neither human construction is granted the epistemic status of the revealed deposit.

BWM therefore operates under the order:

**Scripture -> authoritative deposit -> fallible apprehension -> nature as genuine evidence -> corrigible models -> testing and refinement.**
