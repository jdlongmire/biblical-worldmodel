## Interpretive boundary

Tour's own position must not be strengthened beyond what he claims. On his official site he states that his chemical tools do not enable him to scientifically prove intelligent design. Therefore:

- Tour's chemistry objections may challenge proposed naturalistic pathways.
- Failure of a naturalistic pathway does not by itself establish intelligent design.
- BWM may separately develop a positive Designist inference, but it must not attribute that inference to Tour unless Tour explicitly makes it.
- Conversely, a proposed naturalistic workaround does not answer Tour merely by existing.

## Tour challenge translated into BWM systems language

Tour's strongest chemistry critique can be represented as a continuity requirement:

```text
S0 --R1--> S1 --R2--> S2 ... --Rn--> living/evolvable system
```

For every transition Ri, record:

- actual inputs;
- actual products and byproducts;
- yield;
- selectivity;
- purification;
- concentration;
- environmental conditions;
- energy/activation;
- ordering/timing;
- intermediate lifetime;
- transport;
- investigator intervention;
- compatibility with Ri+1.

The pathway burden is not discharged by showing each Ri in mutually incompatible experiments.

## Auxiliary Hypothesis Non-Credit application

When Tour identifies a difficulty and the literature proposes mechanism A:

```text
Tour objection -> proposed A
```

the disposition is not "answered" until A's evidential state is assessed:

- E0 speculative
- E1 modeled
- E2 demonstrated process
- E3 historically corroborated
- E4 discriminatively corroborated explanation

A naturalistic auxiliary that merely restores possibility remains explanatory debt.
