# Abiogenesis Architecture Burden Model

Status: systems-level synthesis from Tour audit and primary origin-of-life literature.

## Objective

Prevent progress on isolated chemistry from being silently promoted into progress of equal magnitude on the complete historical origin-of-life problem.

## Architecture levels

### A0 — Feedstock
Geochemically available precursors, elements, simple molecules and energy sources.

### A1 — Building blocks
Amino acids, nucleotide precursors/nucleotides, amphiphiles, sugars and related species.

### A2 — Functional polymers / networks
Catalytic or templating polymers and reaction networks with persistence.

### A3 — Compartment
A bounded chemical system with adequate permeability and retention.

### A4 — Coupled autonomous system
Replication, metabolism/energy use and compartment dynamics functionally coupled.

### A5 — Heritable evolvable system
Replication supports inherited variation and sustained Darwinian selection.

### A6 — Coded translation
A mapping/adaptor/translation architecture connects nucleotide sequence to peptide sequence with useful fidelity.

### A7 — Open-ended biological evolution
System can sustain expanding adaptive evolution without investigator maintenance.

## Promotion rule

Evidence at level An does not automatically establish An+1.

```text
A1 evidence != A4 evidence
A3 evidence != A5 evidence
RNA replication evidence != coded translation
component synthesis != autonomous integration
```

## Current high-level status

The origin-of-life literature demonstrates substantial chemistry at A0-A3 and partial experimental systems relevant to A4/A5. The literature itself continues to identify full functional integration and open-ended Darwinian evolution as major challenges. The origin of coded translation remains a distinct major unresolved transition.

Therefore BWM shall not describe abiogenesis as "chemistry has done nothing," nor shall it describe the existence of plausible submechanisms as an established historical pathway.

## Design inference boundary

Failure or incompleteness of a naturalistic pathway is negative evidence against that pathway to the degree the failed proposition was required by it. Positive Designist inference requires its own argument, predictions and comparative explanatory warrant.

This boundary preserves both Tour's stated position and BWM's Auxiliary Hypothesis Non-Credit Principle.
