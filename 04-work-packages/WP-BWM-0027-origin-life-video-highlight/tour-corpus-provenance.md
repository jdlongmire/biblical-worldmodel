# Tour Corpus Provenance and Source Discipline

## Primary authority hierarchy

For "What does James Tour argue?":
1. Tour-authored technical/critical essay.
2. Tour first-party lecture/course/site.
3. Tour-authored interview/transcript.
4. Secondary characterization only when primary is inaccessible.

For "Is Tour's chemistry claim correct?":
1. Original experimental paper/dataset at issue.
2. Methods/supplementary information.
3. Independent replication or directly relevant primary experimental work.
4. Technical reviews.
5. Tour's critique and substantive technical responses as competing analyses.

Tour is therefore primary for his own argument but generally secondary/critical commentary for experiments performed by other laboratories.

## Attribution guard

Tour's official statement explicitly says he does not know how to use science to prove intelligent design and that his chemical tools do not permit that assessment.

BWM shall not convert:

```text
Tour: proposed abiogenesis chemistry is inadequate
```

into:

```text
Tour: chemistry scientifically proves intelligent design
```

without a separate explicit Tour source.

Likewise, critics' labels applied to Tour do not substitute for Tour's stated position.

## Next-source acquisition

For each TOUR-* claim, build a source packet containing:
- Tour passage/timestamp;
- experiment/paper Tour is criticizing;
- exact methods relevant to objection;
- authors' stated limitation;
- strongest technical response;
- subsequent work, if any;
- BWM evidential-state disposition.

This is the required basis for promotion from objection ledger to canonical scientific claim.
