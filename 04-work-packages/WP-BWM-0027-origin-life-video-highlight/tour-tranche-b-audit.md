## TOUR-011 — Vesicles are not functional cell membranes

**Tour objection:** spontaneous amphiphile vesicles/micelles are not equivalent to functional biological membranes with controlled transport, gradients, homeostasis and coupled internal chemistry.

**Primary comparison:** Szostak and colleagues explicitly propose simpler fatty-acid protocell membranes rather than modern phospholipid/protein membranes. Such vesicles can form compartments, grow/divide under some conditions, and permit transport of small solutes/nucleotides through intrinsic permeability.

**Disposition:** RETAIN WITH ARCHITECTURAL DISTINCTION.

Fatty-acid vesicle work materially answers the claim that no simple compartment with nutrient permeability is chemically possible. It does not demonstrate the historical emergence of modern selective active-transport membranes, nor does a protocell hypothesis require a modern membrane at the outset.

Correct burden:
1. demonstrate a prebiotically plausible compartment;
2. demonstrate compatible nutrient/substrate transport;
3. maintain useful internal gradients/chemistry;
4. couple compartment growth/division to internal replication/metabolism;
5. provide a pathway toward progressively controlled transport.

**Credit:** E2 for several local vesicle capabilities. Historical integrated protocell remains below E4.

## TOUR-012 — Four biomolecule classes do not equal life

**Tour objection:** obtaining lipids, nucleotides, amino acids/peptides and carbohydrates separately does not explain their coordinated assembly into a living system.

**Primary comparison:** Adamski et al. explicitly state that de novo life requires functional integration of replication, metabolism and compartmentalization into an out-of-equilibrium system capable of open-ended Darwinian evolution. Other systems-chemistry reviews make the same integration problem explicit.

**Disposition:** RETAIN STRONGLY.

This is not merely Tour rhetoric. It corresponds to a recognized systems-level research frontier.

## TOUR-013 — Modern biochemical pathways cannot simply be projected backward

**Tour objection:** extant enzymatic/biological pathways cannot be assumed in a prebiotic environment.

**Primary comparison:** origin-of-life research itself commonly distinguishes prebiotic chemistry from extant metabolism and proposes precursor or alternative chemistries. Reviews discuss transitions/takeovers between primitive systems and extant biology.

**Disposition:** RETAIN AS METHODOLOGICAL CONTROL.

A proposed precursor pathway must be demonstrated independently rather than borrowing modern machinery. Conversely, Tour may not reject a precursor merely because it differs from extant biology.

## TOUR-014 — Investigator intervention can hide causal burden

**Tour objection:** selected reagents, transfers, purification, replenishment, timing and cycling may perform functions attributed rhetorically to unguided chemistry.

**Primary comparison:** laboratory intervention is ubiquitous and unavoidable as an experimental method. It becomes evidentially relevant only when the historical model lacks an independently specified natural analogue for the intervention.

**Disposition:** RETAIN, PRECISELY FORMULATED.

Investigator intervention is a **burden marker**, not evidence of design by itself.

For each intervention classify:
- analytical only;
- boundary-condition emulation;
- target-directed selection/purification;
- reagent replenishment;
- temporal sequencing;
- transfer between incompatible environments.

Then ask whether the historical model supplies an independently warranted analogue.

## TOUR-015 — Local reaction success does not establish continuous pathway

**Tour objection:** successful isolated reactions do not establish an end-to-end geochemical route to life.

**Primary comparison:** systems-chemistry literature explicitly recognizes the need to integrate subsystems.

**Disposition:** RETAIN STRONGLY; CANONICAL METHODOLOGY CANDIDATE.

This is the Arrow Problem:

```text
demonstrated nodes != demonstrated arrows != demonstrated end-to-end history
```

## TOUR-016 — Purified components/polymers do not explain living-cell assembly

**Tour objection:** even possession of biomolecular components or polymers does not explain autonomous functional organization.

**Primary comparison:** autonomous-system literature distinguishes component availability from self-maintaining organization, coupled replication/metabolism/compartmentalization and open-ended evolution.

**Disposition:** RETAIN STRONGLY.

Avoid requiring a modern cell to appear in one step. The valid burden is a continuous path through successively viable autonomous systems.

## TOUR-017 — Public communication can overstate experimental accomplishment

**Tour objection:** origin-of-life results are sometimes described publicly as though larger transitions have been solved.

**Disposition:** RETAIN CASE-BY-CASE.

This is not a blanket scientific objection. Each case requires comparison of:
- methods/results;
- paper's own claims/limitations;
- press release;
- popular article/video;
- later citation rhetoric.

No field-wide conclusion should be inferred without a corpus study.

## TOUR-018 — Origin of life remains unsolved

**Primary comparison:** Adamski et al. call chemistry-to-biology one of the biggest mysteries and identify major remaining challenges. Recent systems-chemistry literature still describes synthetic integration of metabolic and replicative functions in compartments far from equilibrium as a monumental endeavour.

**Disposition:** RETAIN WITH PRECISION.

"Unsolved" means no empirically established, continuous historical mechanism from plausible prebiotic geochemistry to autonomous open-ended Darwinian life. It does not mean no important constituent chemistry has been demonstrated.

## TOUR-019 — Sequence/information/code origin is distinct from monomer synthesis

**Tour objection:** producing nucleotides or polymers does not explain coded translation/information-processing architecture.

**Primary comparison:** origin-of-translation literature explicitly treats emergence of translation and genetic-code assignment as a distinct major problem. Wolf & Koonin call translation's origin arguably the central and hardest problem in origin-of-life research and describe a fidelity/machinery catch-22. Modern reviews still list unresolved questions in tRNA evolution, codon assignment and coded peptide formation.

Experimental stereochemical associations and RNA-directed peptide-bond formation provide partial clues but do not establish the full historical origin of coding.

**Disposition:** RETAIN STRONGLY; REFER ontology to TRT/LRT.

Chemistry questions stay in WP-BWM-0027:
- amino-acid activation;
- RNA-amino-acid association;
- aminoacylation;
- adaptor emergence;
- codon assignment;
- templated peptide formation;
- fidelity;
- transition to ribosomal translation.

Semantic/representational claims route to TRT/LRT.

**Credit:** E1-E2 for multiple proposed/experimental submechanisms; no E4 assignment for full code/translation origin.

## TOUR-020 — Tour's chemistry does not scientifically prove intelligent design

**Tour position:** his chemistry critique does not itself supply a scientific proof of intelligent design.

**Disposition:** RETAIN AS ATTRIBUTION CONTROL.

BWM's positive Designist argument, if made, must stand on its own positive warrant and must not be smuggled into Tour's scientific objections.

## Tranche-B synthesis

The strongest surviving Tour challenge is architectural:

```text
precursors
 -> polymers
 -> replicators
 -> compartments
 -> coupled metabolism
 -> heredity
 -> code/translation
 -> autonomous reproduction
 -> open-ended Darwinian evolution
```

The literature contains E2 demonstrations at multiple nodes and some arrows. It does not follow that the complete directed path has been historically demonstrated.

### Integration burden

For a candidate pathway P:

```text
P_valid =
  all required transitions physically demonstrated
  + boundary conditions mutually compatible
  + material/energy flows closed enough for persistence
  + interventions mapped to natural mechanisms
  + subsystem coupling demonstrated
  + historical traces/circumstantial evidence
  + discriminators against viable alternatives
```

This is a research criterion, not a claim that abiogenesis is impossible.
