## Findings

### F-001: BWM is already structurally a gateway

The BWM README and accepted component architecture already define BWM as an integration/publication layer and require externally authoritative programmes to be consumed through interfaces rather than silently forked. WP-BWM-0026 is therefore a refinement and generalization of existing architecture.

### F-002: TRT and LRT require two simultaneous truths

LRT is an independently authoritative programme with its own formalization and publications. TRT's authoritative repository states that TRT is the broader ontology within which LRT is situated and that LRT formalizes TRT's L3 constituent. BWM must preserve both facts rather than reducing LRT to an internal BWM or TRT document set.

### F-003: Semantic Actualism is a research programme

SA is not merely a BWM foundation label. Its repository explicitly identifies it as a foundational-ontology research programme in philosophy of mind with a Lakatosian hard core, protective belt, discriminating consequences, traceability, open gating problem, and defeat conditions.

Its canonical source states that SA and TRT share the constrained-information-in-action form but neither is derived from the other in the SA repository. It also describes DFM as downstream only if SA's agency-of-actualization gating problem resolves positively. BWM must not convert that conditional relationship into an established dependency.

### F-004: DFM has outgrown the old BWM classification

The accepted BWM component architecture currently lists DFM as a Historical Framework. The DFM repository now operates as a distinct research programme with programme-level methodology and governance. BWM should retain a creation/initialization historical interval or framework where useful, but DFM source authority belongs to the DFM repository.

### F-005: PFH naming should become epistemically explicit

The canonical historical category is the pre-Fall interval. PFH should mean Pre-Fall Hypothesis and own proposed reconstructions within that underdetermined interval. This prevents hypothesis content from acquiring the linguistic status of established history.

### F-006: DTE and UTE are comparison constructs

DTE and UTE remain comparative interpretive/model families. They are not Longmire research programmes and shall not appear in a research-programme registry as peers of DFM.

### F-007: Additional associated cosmology programmes exist

The repository audit surfaced FCD and CAC in `creation-cosmology-programmes`. That repository explicitly governs two Lakatosian research programmes in one tree. FCD is the more general programme; CAC is its chronology-specific specialization. Their relationship to DFM and BWM needs an explicit interface decision rather than accidental duplication.

## Taxonomic rule

A construct's BWM category does not determine its source authority.

For example, BWM may use an output of DFM in a historical-framework context and an output of TRT in Foundations, while DFM and TRT remain independently governed research programmes.
