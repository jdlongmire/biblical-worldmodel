# Corpus Disposition Recommendations

Status: initial architecture, subject to claim-level verification and JD canonicalization approval.

## 1. Canonical BWM methodology candidates

Promote only after source review:

- Historical reconstruction as an inverse problem.
- Explicit separation of observation from model-conditioned reconstruction.
- Required declaration of initial conditions, boundary conditions, governing equations, dynamical histories, transport/alteration/preservation mechanisms, uncertainty, and sensitivity.
- Symmetrical audit rule: conventional and BWM/DFM reconstructions receive the same burden treatment.
- Underconstrained reconstruction, rather than "historical science is storytelling," as the methodological critique.

Candidate formalism:

```text
O + M + I + B + D -> H
```

A reconstructed history H is conditional upon O, M, I, B, and D. Multiple admissible tuples may map to the same observed state O; model discrimination therefore requires independent constraints or discriminating observations.

## 2. DFM candidates

Refer for deeper work:

- initialization-state constraints;
- distinction between retrodictive/model age and historical elapsed time;
- molecular preservation as a state-history constraint;
- thermal history and deep-slab state constraints;
- planetary/cosmological formation burdens where initialization materially changes the inverse solution.

Do not promote any simple "young clock" merely because it conflicts with conventional chronology.

## 3. Earth History / Flood-model candidates

- cold/deep slab morphology and thermal contrast;
- catastrophic sedimentation/fossilization observations;
- high-energy geological processes;
- fault-fluid pressure and rapid deformation claims after source verification;
- preservation-state constraints.

Flood interpretation must remain downstream of the observation ledger and must generate its own quantitative expectations.

## 4. WP-BWM-0021 referrals

- residual C-14 in coal/diamond/fossil material;
- helium retention;
- radiohalos;
- radiometric discordance;
- open-system isotope behavior;
- radiogenic heat claims;
- RATE-related material.

WP-0029 should not duplicate WP-0021's detailed geochronology audit.

## 5. WP-BWM-0022 referrals

All language asserting "age," "clock," "date," or "timescale" from model inversion should be checked against the retrodictive-age terminology standard.

## 6. WP-BWM-0027 referrals

The origin-of-life section should be substantially harvested, but as a systems-integration burden rather than a catalogue of categorical impossibility claims.

Recommended new artifact in WP-0027:

**Abiogenesis Integrated Systems Compatibility Matrix**

Minimum dimensions:
- precursor availability;
- concentration;
- solvent/water activity;
- pH;
- temperature;
- activation chemistry;
- catalysts/minerals;
- chirality;
- regioselectivity;
- competing reactions;
- degradation;
- yield;
- purification/separation;
- transport between environments;
- compartment compatibility;
- energy coupling;
- replication;
- translation/code;
- error tolerance.

## 7. TRT/LRT referrals

Route these as conceptual/ontological questions:
- order versus information;
- syntactic versus semantic information;
- representation;
- prescription;
- symbol grounding;
- code/mapping relations;
- physical instantiation versus semantic actualization.

Do not let an information-theory ambiguity function as evidence for a biological historical conclusion.

## 8. Research quarantine

Hold without public/canonical use until quantitatively audited:
- whole-Earth radiogenic heat "melting" claims;
- tidal heat integration claims;
- waiting-time numerical claims copied from the source;
- "genetic entropy" chronology;
- specific CISS requirements asserted for abiogenesis;
- precise combinatorial search-space figures;
- claims that particular prebiotic yields make origin impossible;
- planetary angular-momentum claims stated as conservation violations.

## 9. Rejected-argument register

Exclude from canonical/public BWM in current form:
- present lunar recession as a constant hard-age clock;
- modern geomagnetic decline as a free exponential clock;
- telomere chronology;
- simple human population-growth chronology;
- thousands-year mtDNA/Y MRCA claims without a defensible model;
- "vacuum accretion" characterization;
- "no star formation observed";
- "living fossil = zero evolution";
- historical science = storytelling;
- modern methodological actualism = "nothing ever changes";
- blanket "deep time violates thermodynamics" conclusions absent a complete thermal model.

## 10. Public communication recommendation

The source's strongest public thesis can eventually be recast as:

> Historical ages are reconstructed from present traces through models. The relevant scientific question is therefore not whether reconstruction is legitimate, but whether the model's initial conditions, boundary conditions, rate history, transport terms, and independent constraints are adequate to distinguish the proposed history from viable alternatives.

This wording should not be published as a canonical BWM statement until the methodological literature review is complete.
