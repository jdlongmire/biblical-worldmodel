# Initial Atomic Claim Ledger

Status: research intake, non-canonical.

This ledger decomposes the submitted "Evolution Story-Telling Is Not Real Science" text into researchable claim families. It does not adopt the source's rhetoric or conclusions.

## Governing correction

Historical science is not rejected as science merely because past events are unrepeatable. Historical hypotheses can be evaluated through surviving traces, independently testable regularities, consilience, model comparison, and discriminating observations. WP-BWM-0029 therefore targets **underconstrained historical reconstruction**, hidden initialization/boundary assumptions, invalid rate extrapolation, and failures of model discrimination.
