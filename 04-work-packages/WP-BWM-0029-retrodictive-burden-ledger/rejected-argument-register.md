# Rejected-Argument Register

Purpose: prevent arguments that fail technical scrutiny from being reintroduced into canonical or public BWM material without materially new evidence or a corrected model.

| ID | Argument | Reason for rejection in current form | Reopen condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| RA-001 | Historical science is only storytelling because the past is unrepeatable. | Historical hypotheses can be tested using traces, regularities, model comparison and discriminating predictions. | A narrower identifiable methodological claim. |
| RA-002 | Modern uniformitarianism assumes nothing ever changes and all rates remain constant. | Misstates modern actualistic/model-based geology. | Critique a specific model's stated rate/boundary assumptions. |
| RA-003 | Integrated radiogenic energy over deep time by itself proves Earth must melt. | Generated energy is not retained energy; complete transport/loss balance is required. | Full reproducible thermo-mechanical model. |
| RA-004 | Present lunar recession supplies a fixed ~1.4 Ga maximum age. | Tidal dissipation and orbital evolution are time-dependent. | Dynamical model with independently constrained dissipation history. |
| RA-005 | Modern geomagnetic dipole decline is a ~1.4 ka exponential half-life clock. | Geodynamo is non-monotonic/dynamical; recent slope is not a free-decay law. | Physically justified dynamical model fitting paleomagnetic data. |
| RA-006 | Human population growth can be exponentially extrapolated backward to date humanity. | Growth rate and carrying capacity vary strongly through history. | Independently constrained demographic model. |
| RA-007 | Telomere shortening dates humanity. | Cellular aging process is not a species chronology. | None presently identified. |
| RA-008 | Somatic mutation accumulation dates humanity. | Individual-cell/organism process does not integrate as a species clock. | None presently identified. |
| RA-009 | mtDNA/Y lineages are empirically shown to converge only thousands of years ago. | Claim is not supported by conventional primary coalescent literature as stated; dates are model-conditioned. | Reproducible alternative coalescent analysis with explicit mutation/demographic model. |
| RA-010 | Haldane's cost of selection generally disproves macroevolution. | Result is model-dependent and later population-genetic work alters the applicable load under other regimes. | Transition-specific demographic/load calculation. |
| RA-011 | More than 99.9% of all beneficial mutations are lost, universally. | Fixation/loss probability depends on s, dominance, Ne, initial frequency and population model. | State parameterized claim. |
| RA-012 | A fixed number of de novo mutations per child proves inevitable genomic collapse. | Population fitness depends on DFE, selection, dominance, recombination, epistasis and demography. | Reproducible population-level mutation-load model. |
| RA-013 | Protoplanetary accretion occurs in empty vacuum. | Protoplanetary disks contain gas and dust; gas dynamics are central to current models. | Critique a specific disk/accretion mechanism. |
| RA-014 | No star formation has been observed. | Protostars and star-forming regions are directly observed at multiple stages, though complete individual histories are reconstructed. | Critique a specific inferred transition/mechanism. |
| RA-015 | Living fossil means zero evolution. | Morphological stasis does not entail genomic, physiological or ecological identity. | Define a measurable stasis proposition. |
| RA-016 | Exact prespecified nucleotide-string waiting time is automatically the waiting time for any new biological function. | Functional target size and accessible genotype network may differ greatly from one exact sequence. | Demonstrate that the biological function actually requires the specified target or equivalently narrow target set. |

Rejected does not mean the surrounding research domain is uninteresting. It means this formulation should not be used as evidence.
