# Auxiliary Hypothesis Ledger and Evidential-State Standard

Status: governing research method for WP-BWM-0029.

## Purpose

Prevent the existence of a proposed mechanism from being mistaken for an explanation of historical reality.

This rule applies symmetrically to conventional naturalistic models, BWM, DFM, Flood/Earth-History models, PFH, and associated research programmes.

## Prohibited inference

```text
A proposed mechanism exists
        !=
the historical anomaly is explained
```

Likewise:

```text
a simulation can reproduce O
        !=
the simulated history occurred
```

and:

```text
a mechanism operates under laboratory conditions
        !=
the mechanism operated historically for the required duration,
at the required scale, under the required boundary conditions
```

## Evidential-state vocabulary

Every proposed explanatory mechanism shall be assigned one of these states.

### E0 — Speculative
Conceptually proposed but lacking a demonstrated physical mechanism or sufficiently specified model.

### E1 — Modeled
Represented mathematically or computationally and capable of producing some relevant outcome under stipulated parameters.

A modeled mechanism has demonstrated internal/model capability, not historical occurrence.

### E2 — Experimentally / observationally demonstrated process
The physical process itself has been observed or experimentally demonstrated under defined conditions.

This establishes capability within those conditions. It does not establish historical deployment.

### E3 — Historically corroborated mechanism
Independent evidence indicates that the mechanism operated in the historical system being reconstructed.

Required evidence should be independent of merely fitting the anomaly the mechanism was introduced to explain.

### E4 — Discriminatively corroborated explanation
The mechanism successfully predicts or explains evidence that was not simply used to construct/tune it, and competing explanations perform materially worse under comparable constraints.

E4 is the strongest state in this ledger. It remains defeasible.

## Auxiliary-hypothesis classes

### A-P — Progressive auxiliary
Adds independently corroborated empirical content, makes risky/discriminating predictions, or unifies previously independent observations without merely parameterizing away the original anomaly.

### A-N — Neutral/provisional auxiliary
Makes the parent model viable or supplies a plausible mechanism but lacks sufficient independent historical corroboration.

### A-D — Degenerative auxiliary
Primarily accommodates known anomalies through added flexibility, unconstrained parameters, or repeated rescue modifications without commensurate independent evidential gain.

Classification is provisional and can change with new evidence.

## Initial entries

### AH-COS-001 — Inflation

- Parent: standard hot Big Bang cosmology.
- Motivating burdens: horizon, flatness and monopole problems among others.
- Auxiliary: early accelerated expansion driven by an inflationary sector/field.
- State: **E1-E2 depending on proposition**. Inflationary dynamics are extensively modeled and some broad classes generate spectra compatible with observations; the inflaton and the historical inflationary episode are not directly observed.
- Initial Lakatos status: **A-N**, pending model-specific assessment of genuinely novel predictions and independent corroboration.
- Prohibition: CMB compatibility may not be represented as direct observation of inflation.
- Destination: WP-BWM-0011.

### AH-PLAN-001 — Streaming instability

- Parent: protoplanetary/planetesimal formation models.
- Motivating burden: growth/drift/bouncing/fragmentation barriers in simple incremental coagulation.
- Auxiliary: aerodynamic concentration of solids and gravitational collapse under suitable disk conditions.
- State: **E1**, with underlying gas-solid processes having experimental/observational support but historical planetesimal formation by this route remaining model-mediated.
- Initial Lakatos status: **A-N**.
- Required promotion: independently constrained disk conditions plus observational signatures discriminating this pathway from alternatives.
- Destination: DFM/cosmology.

### AH-FOSS-001 — Iron/crosslink preservation

- Parent: conventional long-duration fossil preservation.
- Motivating burden: survival of endogenous molecular/tissue remnants.
- Auxiliary: iron-mediated chemistry, crosslinking, mineral association and related stabilization.
- State: **E2 for relevant chemical capability; historical sufficiency remains below E4**.
- Initial Lakatos status: **A-N**.
- Required promotion: quantitative kinetics under reconstructed fossil conditions and predicted molecular products/distributions matching specimens.
- Destination: Earth History/DFM.

### AH-GEN-001 — Broad functional target / alternative mutational paths

- Parent: evolutionary waiting-time response.
- Motivating burden: long waiting times for prespecified multi-site targets.
- Auxiliary: larger functional target spaces, multiple accessible paths, standing variation, recombination.
- State: variable; these processes exist, but their relevance must be established transition by transition.
- Initial Lakatos status: **A-N** unless target size/pathway is independently constrained.
- Prohibition: an unspecified large target may not be used as a free rescue.
- Destination: population-genetics lane.

### AH-BWM-001 — Designed functional initialization

- Parent: DFM.
- Motivating burden: mature/functionally coherent created state and mismatch between retrodictive age and historical elapsed time.
- Auxiliary/hard-core interface: initialized functional state.
- State: theological/canonical commitment at the BWM worldview level; scientific initialization parameters remain model propositions requiring empirical constraint.
- Lakatos status for scientific use: cannot be automatically classed progressive.
- Required promotion: independent initialization principles, derived expectations, discriminating predictions, and limits preventing arbitrary S0 selection.
- Prohibition: "created that way" may not function as an unconstrained scientific escape term.
- Destination: DFM.

### AH-BWM-002 — Flood-altered boundary conditions

- Parent: Flood/Earth-History model.
- Motivating burden: catastrophic geological reconstruction.
- Auxiliary: changed hydraulic, tectonic, thermal, erosional, depositional or chemical boundary conditions during the Flood.
- State: **E0-E2 depending on the specific proposed process**.
- Initial Lakatos status: **A-N** until quantitatively specified.
- Required promotion: define changed variables, mechanism, magnitude, duration and predicted traces.
- Prohibition: "catastrophe changes rates" is not itself an explanation.
- Destination: Earth History/Flood model.

## Governance rule

No WP-BWM-0029 claim may use verbs such as **explains, resolves, accounts for, demonstrates the history, establishes the mechanism,** or equivalent unless its evidential state justifies that wording.

Preferred vocabulary:
- E0: proposes / conjectures
- E1: models / can reproduce under stipulated conditions
- E2: demonstrates physical capability under specified conditions
- E3: is independently supported as having operated historically
- E4: is discriminatively corroborated as an explanation

## Lakatosian audit rule

The timing of an auxiliary's introduction is relevant but not dispositive. A post-hoc auxiliary can become progressive if it later earns independent corroboration or successful novel prediction. A pre-existing mechanism can remain evidentially weak if merely invoked without showing that it operated in the historical case.

The audit therefore asks:

```text
Anomaly
  -> Auxiliary
  -> Independent constraint
  -> Novel/discriminating expectation
  -> Test
  -> Evidential-state update
```

This ledger is designed to prevent both naturalistic and Designist research programmes from receiving explanatory credit they have not earned.
