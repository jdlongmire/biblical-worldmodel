# Audit Addendum — Mechanism Credit and Auxiliary-Hypothesis Control

This addendum governs interpretation of all quantitative audits in WP-BWM-0029.

## Correction

Where an audit identifies a proposed conventional mechanism, that identification does **not** mean WP-BWM-0029 accepts the mechanism as the historical explanation.

Examples:

- Iron-mediated/crosslink preservation demonstrates candidate chemical capability; it does not by itself demonstrate multi-million-year preservation in the fossil's actual history.
- Streaming instability is a specified planetesimal-formation model; its existence does not establish that Solar-System planetesimals historically formed by that mechanism.
- Functional redundancy, standing variation and alternative mutational paths are real population-genetic considerations; they may not be invoked without transition-specific constraints to erase a calculated waiting-time burden.
- Inflation is a modeled cosmological mechanism/family; observational compatibility is not direct observation of the historical inflationary episode.
- Contamination/background mechanisms in AMS are experimentally real; their existence does not prove that every residual C-14 measurement is background. The measured excess must be quantitatively allocated.

## Required standard

Each audit shall distinguish:

```text
physical possibility
model capability
experimental demonstration
historical occurrence
historical sufficiency
discriminating corroboration
```

These are separate propositions.

A conventional auxiliary and a BWM auxiliary receive the same burden:
1. specify the mechanism;
2. constrain its parameters independently where possible;
3. show it can operate under relevant boundary conditions;
4. identify traces expected if it historically operated;
5. compare those traces with observation;
6. test against viable alternatives.

See `auxiliary-hypothesis-ledger.md` for the governing evidential-state vocabulary.
