# WP-BWM-0029 — Historical Reconstruction, Boundary Conditions, and Retrodictive Burden Ledger

## Purpose

Harvest the submitted critique of evolutionary/deep-time narratives without importing its rhetoric or conclusions wholesale.

The package asks two questions:

1. Which observations and methodological criticisms survive independent technical scrutiny?
2. Where should surviving material live in the BWM ecosystem?

The governing principle is symmetrical: a present-day rate does not by itself license either uniform backward extrapolation or catastrophic backward extrapolation.

## Core reconstruction model

Historical conclusions are represented as:

```text
O + M + I + B + D -> H
```

- **O** = observed present state
- **M** = governing model
- **I** = initial conditions
- **B** = boundary conditions
- **D** = dynamical/rate history, including transport, preservation, alteration, and disturbance where relevant
- **H** = reconstructed history

Therefore an inferred age or history is a model-conditioned reconstruction, not a raw observation.

This extends WP-BWM-0022's retrodictive-age terminology and must remain consistent with it.

## Claim-level disposition

Every atomic proposition receives one of five dispositions:

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RETAIN** | Evidence and formulation are sufficiently strong for continued BWM/programme use |
| **QUALIFY** | Core issue is legitimate but wording, inference, or scope must be corrected |
| **REJECT** | Argument is technically invalid, misleading, or unsupported as stated |
| **UNRESOLVED** | Evidence is insufficient or competing models remain materially underdetermined |
| **REFER** | Valid research topic already belongs to another WP or programme |

Rejected material remains in the provenance ledger so that weak arguments are not accidentally reintroduced later.

## Initial research lanes

### RL-1 — Historical-science methodology
Formalize the boundary-condition and extrapolation critique. Require explicit initialization, closure/open-system assumptions, transport terms, rate histories, uncertainty, and model sensitivity.

### RL-2 — Geochronology and radiocarbon
Audit residual C-14, open-system behavior, radiogenic inventories, decay assumptions, contamination/background, and any proposed chronological inference. Coordinate with WP-BWM-0021 and WP-BWM-0022.

### RL-3 — Earth thermal and tectonic ledger
Evaluate radiogenic heat, mantle heat flow, thermal diffusion/advection, cold slab morphology, CMB observations, and catastrophic versus conventional tectonic histories. Do not infer chronology from an integrated energy total without a full energy/transport model.

### RL-4 — Earth-Moon and geomagnetic claims
Rebuild or reject lunar-recession and geomagnetic arguments using dynamical models. Present recession rates and recent dipole trends may not be treated as invariant exponential/linear clocks without independent justification.

### RL-5 — Fossil molecular preservation
Inventory endogenous soft-tissue, protein, pigment, DNA/biomolecular preservation claims and preservation mechanisms. Separate verified molecular survival from chronological conclusions.

### RL-6 — Origin-of-life systems integration
Harvest the strongest chemistry issues: compatible environments, concentration, activation, chirality, regioselectivity, polymerization, degradation, compartmentalization, transport, energy coupling, replication, translation/code, and error control.

Primary systems question:

> Can individually demonstrated chemical subsystems be joined into a physically continuous, unguided trajectory under mutually compatible boundary conditions?

Detailed origin-of-life content is referred to WP-BWM-0027. WP-0029 owns the cross-domain methodology and disposition.

### RL-7 — Population genetics and waiting time
Audit mutation load, waiting-time, fixation, drift, selection, effective population size, coalescent inference, bottlenecks, mtDNA/Y claims, heterozygosity, and alleged human genetic clocks. Reject demographic or molecular clocks that cannot independently constrain their historical assumptions.

### RL-8 — Cosmology and planetary formation
Audit inflation, horizon/flatness/monopole/lithium issues, baryon asymmetry, planetesimal formation barriers, angular-momentum transport, migration, and other candidate anomalies. Distinguish genuine open research problems from claims that an existing proposed mechanism is merely an ad hoc rescue.

### RL-9 — Information and representation
Extract distinctions among order, complexity, information, representation, prescription, code, and semantic mapping. Route ontology/semantics questions to TRT/LRT through WP-BWM-0026 jurisdiction rather than embedding them as biological conclusions inside DFM.

### Recommended destinations

- **BWM Foundations / methodology:** historical reconstruction, epistemic hierarchy, model-conditioned retrodiction.
- **DFM:** initialization, functional maturity, retrodictive age, cosmological/physical state constraints.
- **Earth History / Flood model:** catastrophic sedimentation, tectonics, stratigraphy, slab dynamics, geological boundary conditions.
- **WP-BWM-0021:** Snelling/RATE, geochronology, radiocarbon, helium/radiohalo claims where overlapping.
- **WP-BWM-0022:** terminology governing calculated/retrodictive age.
- **WP-BWM-0027:** origin-of-life chemistry and integrated abiogenesis analysis.
- **TRT/LRT:** information, representation, semantic prescription, logic, symbol grounding, reducibility questions.
- **Research quarantine:** promising claims lacking primary verification or adequate quantitative models.
- **Rejected-argument register:** technically failed clocks and arguments that should not be reused in canonical or public BWM material.

## Initial disposition hypotheses

These are research starting points, not canonical conclusions.

**Retain for investigation**
- historical reconstruction/boundary-condition methodology;
- molecular preservation constraints;
- cold/deep slab thermal constraints;
- origin-of-life system integration;
- homochirality, linkage selectivity, compartmentalization, reaction compatibility;
- genetic-code/information questions;
- waiting-time questions under explicit population-genetic models;
- planetesimal-formation barriers;
- horizon, flatness, monopole, lithium and related cosmological model burdens.

**Qualify before use**
- inflation critiques;
- radiogenic thermal ledgers;
- human mutation-load arguments;
- radiocarbon in conventionally ancient carbon;
- living fossils and stasis;
- claims about angular-momentum transport and planetary migration.

**Reject as presently formulated**
- constant present lunar-recession extrapolation as a clock;
- exponential extrapolation of the recent geomagnetic decline;
- telomere shortening as a human/Earth chronology;
- simple human population growth as an age clock;
- "no star formation has been observed" as a decisive objection;
- characterization of protoplanetary accretion as occurring in empty vacuum;
- the blanket claim that every deep-time thermal calculation melts Earth.

Rejected arguments may be reopened only with materially new evidence or a corrected physical model.

## Promotion gates

A proposition may move from this WP into canonical corpus only when:

1. the empirical observation is source-provenanced;
2. observation and historical interpretation are explicitly separated;
3. the strongest technically relevant alternative explanation is represented;
4. required initial and boundary conditions are stated;
5. quantitative calculations are reproducible where the claim is quantitative;
6. uncertainty and sensitivity are reported;
7. a discriminator or falsifier is identified where feasible;
8. destination corpus jurisdiction is confirmed under WP-BWM-0026;
9. rhetoric is removed from scientific propositions;
10. JD approves canonical promotion.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every harvested proposition has a disposition and destination.
- Weak short-chronology arguments are explicitly prevented from contaminating the canonical corpus.
- Conventional explanations are evaluated by the same boundary-condition standard applied to DFM/Flood hypotheses.
- No unexplained initialization choice functions as a universal escape for either model family.
- No unresolved research problem is represented as proof of an alternative model.
- Cross-programme material follows WP-BWM-0026 jurisdiction.
- Canonical promotion remains subject to Principal Operator review.
