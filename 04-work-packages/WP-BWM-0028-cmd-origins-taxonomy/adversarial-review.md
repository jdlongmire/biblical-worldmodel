# Adversarial Review and Reconciliation

Status: completed candidate review, 2026-09-26

## Review set

The four WP-BWM-0028 candidate artifacts were tested against:

- canonical BWM epistemic hierarchy;
- accepted BWM component architecture;
- DFM BWM interface;
- cross-domain invariants;
- WP-BWM-0015 observation/history/purpose method;
- WP-BWM-0022 retrodictive-age terminology;
- the September 2026 CMD/DFM/DTE/UTE source working paper.

## Findings

### F1. CMD must not become a second epistemology

**Risk:** The CMD candidate could duplicate or compete with the canonical L0-L6 hierarchy.

**Disposition:** PASS WITH CONSTRAINT.

CMD is retained as methodological governance. The canonical epistemic hierarchy owns claim levels, dependency propagation, symmetry, precommitment, and canonicalization. CMD owns the posture connecting scriptural authority, real natural evidence, reason, operational regularity, boundary discipline, and Lakatosian programme governance.

### F2. DFM/DTE/UTE must not become peer BWM programmes

**Risk:** The source paper repeatedly calls all three "programmes," while accepted BWM architecture recognizes DFM as a Historical Framework.

**Disposition:** REVISE SOURCE TERMINOLOGY; CANDIDATE PASSES.

The canonical candidate uses **origin mode** for the triad. DFM is instantiated as a BWM Historical Framework. DTE and UTE remain comparison classes.

### F3. Initialization taxonomy could conflict with existing functional/chronicle distinction

**Risk:** A new ladder could silently replace the existing canonical-candidate distinction in the observation/history/purpose method.

**Disposition:** PASS WITH NORMALIZATION.

The I0-I4 taxonomy is subordinate to the binary governance distinction:
- functional/systemic initialization;
- chronicle-maturity requiring additional warrant.

I0-I3 describe types of initialized order. I4 flags the higher-burden archive case. The ladder does not confer warrant.

### F4. "Every dynamical reconstruction begins from conditions..." can be overclaimed

**Risk:** The statement is useful but may be read as a theorem about every possible cosmological or mathematical model.

**Disposition:** NARROW.

Canonical wording should refer to an explanatory model's specified domain: dynamics operate on some admitted state, law, boundary condition, or structure. The comparative issue is whether and how that admitted structure is explained, not whether it can be rhetorically renamed DFM.

### F5. Model + Interpretation -> Expectations is underspecified

**Risk:** As a canonical formula it omits auxiliaries, boundary conditions, test context, and observation.

**Disposition:** PASS AS PUBLIC SHORTHAND ONLY.

Canonical analysis retains:
- `H = R(O,M,I,B)` for reconstruction;
- expectation generation with explicit auxiliaries and context;
- epistemic-level dependency for test consequences.

### F6. DFM discontinuity language could imply arbitrary intervention

**Risk:** "Discontinuity allowed at initialization" can function as a universal escape clause.

**Disposition:** CONSTRAIN.

A DFM discontinuity must identify the boundary, initialized state, functional or revelational warrant, physical integration burden, and discriminating consequence. Discontinuity is admissible within the mode; it is not evidential success.

### F7. UTE definition risks conflating methodological and metaphysical naturalism

**Risk:** Some scientific reconstructions exclude purposive agency methodologically without asserting metaphysical naturalism.

**Disposition:** REVISE.

UTE is defined at the causal-reconstruction level: no directing purposive agency is included in the explanatory account under comparison. This does not by itself classify the researcher's total worldview.

### F8. "Scripture first" needs jurisdictional precision

**Risk:** A broad slogan could be read as Scripture supplying measurements or technical mechanisms it does not address.

**Disposition:** PASS WITH EXISTING CANONICAL WORDING.

Use: Scripture is primary for biblical-historical claims; nature is real evidence; domain models remain responsible for empirical and mechanistic claims.

### F9. Chronicle-maturity burden is necessary but not sufficient

**Risk:** Treating I4 merely as "highest burden" could still permit arbitrary fabricated histories if enough rhetoric is supplied.

**Disposition:** STRENGTHEN.

An I4 proposal must identify why the specific record-like information is required or independently warranted, what alternative initialized states were possible, and what risky expectation distinguishes the proposal. Absent such warrant, the claim remains an unresolved anomaly rather than a default DFM explanation.

### F10. Source paper historical claims require source audit

**Risk:** Claims about Kepler, Boyle, Newton, Faraday, Maxwell, Huxley, medieval natural philosophy, and the historical consolidation of methodological naturalism are substantive historical claims.

**Disposition:** HOLD FOR RESEARCH.

They are not needed to canonicalize the methodological distinctions. No public historical narrative should be promoted from this WP until primary-source verification is completed.

## Reconciliation result

No blocking contradiction was found between the candidate architecture and the accepted BWM component architecture.

Promotion is warranted for:
1. CMD methodological governance, with F1/F8 constraints;
2. origins-mode taxonomy, with F4/F6/F7 revisions;
3. initialization burden taxonomy, with F3/F9 constraints;
4. expectation-method integration, with F5 constraint.

Historical exemplars and historical-development claims remain research/provenance material pending source verification.
