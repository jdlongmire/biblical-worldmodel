# Candidate: Christian Methodological Designism Governance

Status: candidate under WP-BWM-0028  
Target owner: `02-systems-baseline/2.2-architecture/foundations/`

## Purpose

Christian Methodological Designism (CMD) names the BWM methodological posture for inquiry within an intelligible created order. It does not replace the canonical BWM epistemic hierarchy, hermeneutics, ontology, or domain evidence. It governs how those authorities and tools are brought into disciplined relation.

## Governing commitments

1. **Normative authority.** Scripture is primary for biblical-historical claims. Christ the Logos grounds truth and intelligibility within the Christian WorldModel.
2. **Real created order.** Nature is real evidence. It is interpreted evidence rather than a self-interpreting epistemic authority.
3. **Reason as instrument.** Logic, categorization, mathematics, model construction, and probabilistic reasoning are instruments of inquiry operating under the governing worldview.
4. **Operational regularity.** Stable secondary causes and repeatable operations warrant empirical investigation of the running created order.
5. **Boundary discipline.** Successful operational regularity does not by itself establish that the same dynamics generated an initialization state or were historically traversed across a boundary event.
6. **Research-programme discipline.** Historical reconstructions must expose hard-core commitments, revisable auxiliaries, predictions, accommodations, failure conditions, and degeneration risk.
7. **Symmetry.** Competing reconstructions are evaluated under the same evidential and auxiliary-hypothesis standards.
8. **Revisability below authority.** Particular scientific and historical reconstructions remain revisable where Scripture does not require them.

## Jurisdiction of exemplars

The source working paper integrates contributors by jurisdiction rather than claiming that they independently entail one origins reconstruction.

- Christ and apostolic Scripture: normative authority.
- Van Til: presuppositional exposure and denial of worldview-neutral adjudication.
- Aristotle and Socrates: categorical, causal, teleological, and dialectical tools.
- Aquinas: public metaphysical argument and faith/reason coherence, subordinated to Scripture within BWM.
- Reid and Plantinga: cognitive reliability and warranted belief.
- Gödel: formal-system limits, without treating incompleteness as a theistic proof.
- Lakatos: research-programme governance and progressive/degenerating problemshift.
- Bayes: structured updating under uncertainty within an identified model and prior structure.

These are methodological jurisdictions. Their inclusion is not a genealogical claim and does not imply agreement among the exemplars on BWM's theology or origins history.

## Methodological naturalism distinction

CMD distinguishes:

- **regularism**: nature is sufficiently stable for disciplined investigation;
- **secondary-cause inquiry**: ordinary mechanisms should be investigated as genuine causes within the created order;
- **methodological naturalism as an admissibility rule**: purposive or revelational explanation is excluded from scientific explanation by methodological definition.

BWM accepts the first two. It does not grant the third rule worldview-neutral authority over origins reconstruction.

Historical claims about the development of these categories require primary-source validation before public canonical publication.

## Integration with canonical epistemic governance

CMD consumes the canonical L0-L6 epistemic hierarchy. It adds no parallel hierarchy.

For every substantive claim, BWM should identify:

`authority/observation -> model + assumptions + interpretation -> expectation/test -> disposition`

Consequences of failure propagate only through demonstrated dependency.

## Anti-degeneration rules

CMD shall not permit:

- functional maturity as an unconstrained explanation for any inconvenient datum;
- emergence as an unconstrained explanation for any unexplained organization;
- retrospective auxiliary invention without increased constraint;
- demotion of a failed indispensable claim after testing;
- asymmetric evidential pricing of competing programmes;
- conflation of measurement with historical reconstruction;
- teleological permission to substitute for physical or historical explanation.

## Candidate maxim

> Model + assumptions + interpretation generate expectations; observations test those expectations at the level from which they follow.

This maxim is subordinate to the fuller BWM reconstruction and epistemic-hierarchy machinery.
