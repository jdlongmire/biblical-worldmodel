# Candidate: Origins-Mode Taxonomy

Status: candidate under WP-BWM-0028  
Target owner: Integration, with DFM instantiated under Historical Frameworks

## Purpose

Classify explanatory treatments of a beginning without treating the classification itself as a set of BWM research programmes.

The taxonomy asks a narrow question:

> What explanatory role do agency, initialized state, elapsed process, and undirected dynamics play in the production of the system under examination?

## DFM: Designed Functional Maturity

A purposive agent initializes a system in a state sufficient for its commissioned operation. Genuine elapsed history follows initialization. The ordinary dynamics observed after deployment need not be the process that generated the initialized state.

Core mode commitments:

- purposive agency;
- specified initialization;
- operationally sufficient state at the relevant boundary;
- distinction between post-initialization dynamics and generation of the initial state.

DFM does not imply that every record-like feature is initialized. Particular chronicle-shaped initialization claims incur an additional explanatory burden.

Within BWM, DFM is instantiated as the creation/initialization Historical Framework.

## DTE: Designed Time and Emergence

A purposive agent intends an outcome and uses genuine elapsed processes and intermediate stages as means by which the relevant function or form develops.

Core mode commitments:

- purposive agency;
- real developmental history;
- intermediate stages constitutive of the making of the target system.

Auxiliaries may differ over front-loaded law, providential concurrence, or discrete intervention.

Within BWM, DTE is a comparative origin mode. It is not an active BWM research programme.

## UTE: Undirected Time and Emergence

The causal reconstruction contains no directing purposive agency. The target system is explained through impersonal initial/boundary conditions plus lawful dynamics, stochastic processes, selection effects, and other non-purposive mechanisms admitted by the model.

Core mode commitments for comparison:

- no directing purposive agency in the causal reconstruction;
- developmental history is constitutive where the target state is derived;
- explanatory sufficiency is sought within non-purposive dynamics and boundary conditions.

Within BWM, UTE is a comparative origin mode. It is not an active BWM research programme.

## Initialization symmetry

The taxonomy shall not claim that UTE literally "borrows DFM." The stronger comparative point is:

> Every dynamical reconstruction begins from conditions that are not generated by the subsequent dynamics merely by running those dynamics backward without limit.

The discriminating question is therefore not whether a model has an initial or boundary state. It is what that state contains, what the model claims was historically traversed, and what explanatory work is assigned to agency, dynamics, and elapsed time.

## Hybrid use

Real explanatory systems may combine modes by domain or interval. A model may posit designed initialization and later genuine development, or designed law with extensive process, without collapsing the taxonomy.

Therefore each comparison shall name:

- target system/domain;
- boundary under examination;
- mode applied at that boundary;
- subsequent historical mode;
- auxiliary hypotheses.

## Comparative matrix

| Axis | DFM | DTE | UTE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginning | designed deployment/initialization | designed commencement of developmental process | boundary/initial state plus non-purposive dynamics |
| Agency | constitutive | constitutive | excluded from causal reconstruction |
| Intermediate stages | optional for production of initialized target | constitutive | constitutive where target is dynamically derived |
| Elapsed process | genuine after initialization | means of making | means of making |
| Discontinuity | admissible at initialization | minimized or localized according to model | not purposive by explanatory rule |
| Primary burden | justify initialized content, especially record-like content | justify directed history and mode of guidance | derive organization from admitted conditions and dynamics |

## Discrimination

Comparisons must seek observations for which the modes generate materially different expectations. Evidence compatible with all compared modes may constrain all of them while possessing low discriminatory force.
