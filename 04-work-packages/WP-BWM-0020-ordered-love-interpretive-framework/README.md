## Core rubric

**Ordered covenant communion -> creaturely autonomy -> rebellion -> Curse and death -> external law confronting fallen humanity -> judgment, restraint, and accommodation -> promise and preservation -> the Seed -> regeneration and the Bride -> consummated covenant communion.**

The paper treats natural creaturely self-love as good within proper communion. Godward and neighborward love are commanded; self-love is presupposed. Autonomy makes the self the governing reference point of good, disordering natural self-love against God and neighbor. External divine law exists before the Fall, while Mosaic law is its expanded covenantal articulation to fallen humanity. The framework distinguishes creation ordinance, moral command, fallen-world accommodation/regulation, judicial action, ceremonial/typological law, and redemptive preservation.

National judgment and slavery/servitude are treated as explicit challenge cases. The framework is a canonical rubric, not a substitute for passage-specific exegesis.
