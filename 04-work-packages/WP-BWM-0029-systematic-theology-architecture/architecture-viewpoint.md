## 9. Epistemic and governance overlay

Every doctrinal claim should carry a status:

```text
EXPLICIT
   │
CANONICAL SYNTHESIS
   │
LOGICAL ENTAILMENT
   │
THEOLOGICAL INFERENCE
   │
ACTIVE HYPOTHESIS
   │
OPEN PROBLEM
```

This is not a confidence ladder in which every lower item is necessarily weaker than every higher item. It is a claim-type/status discrimination.

Example:

- God's creation of humanity in his image: Explicit.
- Covenant-communion architecture: Canonical synthesis.
- Culpability requires truthful agent attribution: Logical/moral entailment within accepted premises.
- Freely embraced impeccability: Theological inference under active testing.
- Non-imputation of Adam's personal guilt: Active hypothesis / OPEN.
- Exact metaphysics of first sinful assent: Open problem.

## 11. Architectural invariants

1. Scripture remains the primary epistemic authority.
2. Covenant and communion are organizing theological syntheses, not authorities above Scripture.
3. Creator and creature remain categorically distinct.
4. The Father decrees and ordains; personal mission language must preserve one divine purpose and inseparable operation.
5. Genuine creaturely agency cannot be erased to solve providence.
6. Divine ordination and creaturely sinful intention are not identical predicates.
7. Inherited noetic/affective corruption and individual culpability remain distinct unless canonical exegesis establishes otherwise.
8. Temptation and conceptual consideration are not identical to sin.
9. Sin may exist inwardly before external enactment through culpable assent/willing.
10. Redemption is accomplished by the Son and applied by the Spirit according to the Father's purpose.
11. Glorification perfects rather than abolishes redeemed personhood and agency.
12. Historical/domain models may expose tension but may not silently rewrite doctrine.
13. Open hypotheses remain visibly open.
14. Canonical promotion preserves provenance.

## 12. Viewpoint summary

The proposed BWM architecture treats Systematic Theology as the doctrinal bridge between foundational authority/ontology and historical or empirical modeling.

Its central relational grammar is:

> **The triune God creates and covenantally orders communion; genuine creatures can culpably rupture that communion; God judges rebellion, sovereignly redeems a people through the Son, restores them through the Spirit, and brings them to freely embraced, perfected covenant communion in the New Creation, to the glory of God.**
