## Recommended hierarchy placement

### L6 — Worldview / hard-core commitment

Candidate principle:

> **Scripture is truthful divine revelation and therefore provides reliable testimony concerning reality wherever it speaks. Because the same God is the author of Scripture and Creator of the natural world, genuine revelation and genuine observation cannot ultimately conflict.**

Recommended name: **Scriptural Testimony Principle (STP).**

This belongs at L6 because it is a worldview-level epistemic commitment governing what BWM recognizes as authoritative testimony.

### L5 — Biblical WorldModel methodological consequence

Candidate consequence:

> **Scriptural testimony is admissible as historical information concerning initial conditions and events inaccessible to direct present observation. BWM models must reconcile that testimony with genuine observations of God's world.**

This is the WorldModel-level methodological consequence of STP.

### L4 and below — corrigible research

DFM, PFH, CHFM, radiometric interpretations, Flood mechanisms, cosmological mechanisms, and other scientific/historical reconstructions remain protective-belt content as appropriate.

## Dependency map

L6 STP → L5 Scripture as historical constraint → L4 research programmes → L3 models → L2 auxiliaries → L1 predictions/tests → L0 observations.

## Hard-core / support distinction

**Hard-core candidate:** STP itself; Word/world non-contradiction at the level of genuine revelation and genuine observation; Scripture's legitimacy as testimony about reality.

**Supporting warrant layer:** historical evidence, textual transmission, canon chronology, anthropology/flourishing, testimony epistemology, naturalism distinction, and historical-inference formalization. These remain research/support artifacts and can be revised without changing STP.

**Theological hypothesis layer:** Providential Non-Preservation Hypothesis. PNPH remains explicitly noncanonical unless separately dispositioned.
