# WP-BWM-0011 — Chronological Contamination

Status: Active / proposed for canonicalization
Owner: JD Longmire
Working branch: `wp/bwm-chronological-contamination`

## Objective

Formalize the Biblical WorldModel treatment of dating outputs that conflict with the programme's post-Fall **human mortality chronology**, and establish a disciplined research path for identifying and testing mechanisms that generate excess retrodictive age.

## Scope

This work package covers:

- the human mortality chronology constraint for genuinely human bodily remains and burial events;
- the formal definition of **chronological contamination**;
- separation of human existence chronology from human mortality chronology;
- separation of observation, operational measurement, retrodictive age, actual elapsed age, and inherited/system-state age;
- rules for evaluating convergence among dating methods for shared assumptions;
- a protective-belt research discipline requiring candidate contamination mechanisms to remain testable and defeasible;
- Sima de los Huesos as the first worked case study;
- later extension to Neanderthal, Denisovan, and other proposed human-lineage remains.

It does not establish the total elapsed duration of human existence, rule out an extended pre-Fall human interval, authorize altering primary-source scientific claims, suppress contrary evidence, or declare a specific contamination mechanism established without evidence.

## Key programme statements

> Human death enters the biblical historical order through the Fall. Accordingly, genuinely human bodily remains resulting from death, and burial events involving those remains, belong to post-Fall history.

> Chronological contamination is any inherited, introduced, altered, mixed, or mis-modelled state variable that causes a dating procedure to return a retrodictive age older than the actual elapsed duration of the object, event, or deposit being dated.

## Relationship to WP-BWM-0012

`WP-BWM-0012-extended-prefall-human-development` investigates total human antiquity and the possibility of an extended, biblically unmeasured pre-Fall interval.

The packages are intentionally separated:

- **WP-BWM-0012 governs when humans may have lived.**
- **WP-BWM-0011 governs when humans may have died.**

Formally:

`T_human = T_preFall + T_postFall`

while:

`T_human_death is a subset of T_postFall`

This permits investigation of pre-Fall human tools, structures, symbolic artifacts, settlement traces, migration, and other activity without placing human death before the Fall.

## Initial research task: Sima de los Huesos

Decompose the approximately 430 ka conventional age into its constituent evidentiary chains. For each dating system:

1. identify the directly measured quantity;
2. identify the event actually being dated: activity, deposition, death, burial, or surrounding substrate;
3. document the conversion model from measurement to elapsed time;
4. identify boundary conditions and closure/reset assumptions;
5. identify plausible old-bias mechanisms;
6. determine which mechanisms can be experimentally or observationally tested;
7. identify common-mode assumptions across supposedly independent methods;
8. compare those results with the BWM post-Fall human mortality chronology constraint.

Primary-source literature is required for the evidentiary ledger. Secondary sources may be used for discovery and orientation but shall not substitute for accessible primaries.

## Acceptance criteria

This work package is ready for human acceptance when:

- the terminology is approved by JD;
- the hard-core versus protective-belt distinction is explicit;
- human existence chronology and human mortality chronology cannot be conflated;
- the relationship with WP-BWM-0012 is explicit and non-conflicting;
- chronological contamination cannot be confused with ordinary laboratory contamination alone;
- unresolved cases are labelled unresolved rather than filled with speculative mechanisms;
- the Sima case has a source-grounded dating-method decomposition;
- every proposed mechanism is classified by evidentiary status and testability;
- the work register points to this package.
