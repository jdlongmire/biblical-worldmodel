# Decree and Agency Synthesis

Status: Research draft
Work package: WP-BWM-0028
Date: 2026-09-25

## Purpose

Formalize the relationship among the Father's sovereign decree and ordination, God's inherently logical nature, genuine creaturely moral agency, sin, guilt, judgment, election, redemption, pneumatological application, and consummation. This artifact distinguishes definitions, axioms, derived propositions, active hypotheses, and pressure points.

## Vocabulary

G = the one true triune God. F = Father. S = Son. H = Holy Spirit of Truth. D = Father's sovereign decree. O = Father's ordination of the means and history by which the decreed end obtains. P = created image-bearing person. W(P) = P's will. A(P) = voluntary act genuinely attributable to P. I(P,A) = P's intention in A. C = rightful communion with God, neighbor, self, and creation. R = creaturely rebellion. K = post-Fall cursed/disordered condition. Q(P) = personal guilt. J = Son's righteous judgment. E = Father's election. X = Son's accomplished redemption. Gl = glorification/perfected communion.

Modal operators:

- □L phi: logically necessary, or denial entails contradiction.
- □D phi: decretively certain given the Father's free and immutable decree.
- □K phi: dispositionally necessary given fallen condition K.

These operators are not interchangeable.

## Definitions

**D1 Divine logicality.** God is inherently logical. Logical coherence is not an independent authority external to God. Contradiction is not an actualizable state because God does not deny his own nature.

**D2 Sovereign decree.** The Father's decree is his eternal, free, certain, comprehensive, and unfailing determination of the history and end that shall obtain.

**D3 Ordination.** The Father's sovereign ordering of the means, events, boundaries, relations, and consequences through which the decreed history certainly obtains.

**D4 Created person.** A genuinely distinct, derivative, image-bearing self capable of knowing, willing, deliberating, intending, acting, loving, and pursuing perceived good.

**D5 Moral agency.** Voluntary acts genuinely proceed from and belong to the person's own willing, intention, and loves such that praise, blame, judgment, forgiveness, or condemnation may truthfully predicate of that person.

**D6 Creaturely freedom.** Voluntary action proceeding according to the agent's own nature, knowledge, loves, intentions, judgments, and will. Freedom does not require metaphysical indeterminacy between contrary possibilities.

**D7 Natural self-love.** The personal self's orientation toward perceived preservation, good, happiness, flourishing, security, fulfillment, and life. It is a created good when ordered under supreme love for God and genuine love for neighbor.

**D8 Ordered communion.** Supreme love for God, rightly ordered self-love, genuine love for neighbor, and faithful stewardship of creation.

**D9 Sin.** Culpable creaturely rebellion against God, expressed through lawlessness, unbelief, disordered love, and rupture of rightful communion with God, neighbor, self, and creation.

**D10 Guilt.** Personal moral liability belonging to a person because of that person's culpable rebellion.

**D11 Curse.** The divinely imposed post-Fall condition and judgment under which humanity and creation exist after Adam's rebellion.

**D12 Election.** The Father's sovereign and merciful choosing of a particular people from guilty humanity and giving them to the Son, without grounding the choice in superior creaturely merit.

**D13 Effectual redemption.** The Son's accomplishment of salvation for all whom the Father gives him.

**D14 Pneumatological application.** The Spirit's work of bringing the elect to faith and repentance, regenerating, indwelling, sanctifying, preserving, and sealing them in application of the Son's accomplished redemption.

## Axioms and controlling commitments

**A1 One God, three persons.** F, S, and H are personally distinct and are the one true God G. God's external works are undivided.

**A2 Personal missions.** The Father decrees, ordains, sends, gives, and elects. The Son creates, sustains, judges, redeems, reconciles, and restores. The Spirit reveals, testifies, convicts, regenerates, sanctifies, preserves, and seals. These are distinct personal missions within one divine purpose.

**A3 Divine logicality.** God cannot actualize a contradiction in the same respect at the same time.

**A4 Comprehensive decree.** Every event belonging to the moral history that actually obtains falls within D and O. No autonomous historical event exists outside divine sovereignty.

**A5 Decretive certainty.** If the Father decrees phi, then □D phi. This does not imply □L phi. A freely decreed contingent creation does not become metaphysically necessary merely because its occurrence is certain.

**A6 Genuine creaturely distinction.** For created person P, P != G. W(P) is genuinely P's will although P exists and continues only by divine creation and sustenance.

**A7 Ownership condition for culpability.** If Q(P) rests on voluntary sinful act A, then A and its sinful intention must genuinely be attributable to P.

**A8 No transfer of sinful intention.** The Father's ordination of event e does not entail identity between God's intention concerning e and the creature's sinful intention in e.

**A9 Moral purity of God.** No sinful defect, disordered love, rebellion, or culpable intention is attributable to God.

**A10 Real judgment.** The Son judges real persons for real culpable acts, intentions, loves, harms, knowledge, persistence, and violated relationships.

**A11 Sovereign mercy.** Mercy and grace are not debts owed to the guilty.

**A12 Effectual economy.** The Father gives the elect to the Son; the Son effectually redeems those given; the Spirit effectually applies that redemption and preserves the redeemed to final inheritance.

## Derived propositions

**P1 Ordination and culpability are not contradictory predicates.** The Father may ordain the certain occurrence of an event while the sinful intention constituting rebellion remains genuinely attributable to the creature. Divine ordination establishes the certainty of the event without transferring the creature's sinful intention to God.

**P2 Sovereignty cannot negate ordained agency.** If God ordains P as a genuine moral agent, and culpability requires genuine ownership of willing, then a model in which P is culpable while P is not genuinely the relevant agent is internally contradictory.

> God's sovereignty cannot require the negation of the creaturely agency God sovereignly ordained.

**P3 Contrary choice is not constitutive of freedom.** God is perfectly free and cannot sin. Glorified persons remain genuine persons without final apostasy. Therefore ability to choose evil cannot be necessary to genuine freedom.

**P4 Moral possibility does not entail an actual Fall.** Genuine derivative agency may include the possibility of disordered willing without making actual rebellion logically necessary. The actuality and certainty of the Fall belong to decree and ordination.

**P5 Sin is not a positive created substance.** Personhood, agency, self-love, stewardship, authority, and communion are goods. Sin is culpable disorder, privation, corruption, and misdirection of created goods.

**P6 Judgment requires individualized moral truth.** Righteous judgment accounts for the agent, act, intention, knowledge, deliberation, persistence, harm, and violated relationship.

**P7 Mercy does not negate justice.** If condemnation rests on actual culpable rebellion and mercy is not owed, granting mercy to the elect does not create injustice toward those receiving righteous judgment. This remains downstream of the guilt model.

## Adam, curse, and inherited condition

### H1 Proposed non-imputation thesis

1. Adam is personally guilty for Adam's rebellion.
2. Adam's failed stewardship brings the entrusted order under curse.
3. Adam's descendants enter cursed/disordered condition K.
4. Adam's descendants do not inherit Adam's personal guilt merely by descent.
5. Each descendant becomes personally guilty through actual voluntary culpable rebellion.

H1 is an active theological hypothesis, not yet a canonical BWM conclusion.

### H2 Inherited corruption

K includes mortality, alienation from God, fractured human communion, disordered desire and self-love, inability apart from grace to love God supremely, and a created order experienced under frustration and corruption.

### H3 Universal personal sin

For every ordinary post-Fall human moral agent P in K, the model proposes □K(P sins voluntarily). This is dispositional necessity, not external coercion.

### H4 Personal guilt

K(P) != Q(P) merely by inheritance.

R(P) -> Q(P).

The unresolved joint is why K makes universal actual sin dispositionally necessary without making inherited corruption itself identical to personal guilt.

## Ordered love

Proper moral order:

God -> self <-> neighbor -> creation.

Supreme love for God orders natural self-love, love for neighbor, and faithful stewardship. Sin severs self-love from supreme love for God and genuine love for neighbor, making self-reference governing and fracturing communion. This extends WP-BWM-0020.

## Judgment

Gravity of offense may include act, harm, knowledge, intention, voluntary deliberation, persistence, relationship violated, and rightful claims of the offended party.

Sin against God concerns the creature's total relation to Creator, sustainer, rightful Lord, highest good, and giver of life and agency.

The model shall not rely on an unsupported shortcut of infinite divine status -> mechanically infinite offense -> mechanically infinite punishment. Judgment must be defended as righteous, truthful, individualized, and commensurate with actual guilt.

## Election and redemption

Given universal actual guilt: no guilty person can demand acquittal, mercy, or grace. The Father freely elects according to sovereign mercy and gives the elect to the Son. The Son effectually redeems those given. The Spirit effectually applies redemption, preserves, and seals the elect for final redemption.

The redeemed receive mercy. The condemned receive righteous judgment for their own culpable rebellion. "No one receives injustice" remains downstream of successful defense of the guilt and judgment premises.

## Trinitarian mission

> The Father decrees and ordains.
> The Son creates, judges, and redeems.
> The Holy Spirit testifies, convicts, regenerates, sanctifies, preserves, and seals.

This describes personal missions within one divine purpose and must not be read as three independent divine operations or wills.

## Telos

The Father decrees the final end and ordains the means. The Son's judgment manifests holiness, righteousness, truth, authority, justice, and lordship. The Son's redemption manifests mercy, grace, love, faithfulness, power, and restoration. The Spirit's ministry manifests truth, conviction, witness, regenerating power, sanctification, preservation, and certainty of final inheritance.

Evil is not ultimate, autonomous, or outside the Father's decree. Its occurrence does not make sinful willing morally attributable to God.

The infallibly decreed end is the manifestation of the glory of the triune God, particularly the glory of the Son as Creator, Lord, righteous Judge, and gracious Redeemer, to whom the Father gives a redeemed people and whom the Spirit glorifies by bringing that people into everlasting communion with God.

## Strongest pressure points

**PR1 Divine authorship of sin.** Distinguish ordination, sustaining causality, agent ownership, intention, moral predicate, and purpose without making creaturely action independent of God.

**PR2 Romans 5 and imputation.** Determine whether inherited curse without inherited personal guilt preserves Paul's Adam-Christ argument rather than merely fitting selected verses.

**PR3 Universal sin from K.** Formalize condition, disposition, voluntary willing, act, and guilt so inherited corruption is not silently equated with inherited culpability.

**PR4 Freedom.** Show why decretive certainty does not erase genuine agent-level willing, tested against divine freedom and glorified impeccability.

**PR5 Trinitarian decree.** Establish biblical warrant for the Father-oriented economic formula while preserving one divine nature, will, purpose, and inseparable operation.
