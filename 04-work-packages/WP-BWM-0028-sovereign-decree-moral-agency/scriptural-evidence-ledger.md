## Method

Scripture is primary. Entries distinguish what a text states directly from theological synthesis and unresolved inference. The ledger is designed to pressure-test WP-BWM-0028, not merely collect supportive proof texts.

Confidence labels:
- HIGH: direct canonical warrant for the stated proposition.
- MEDIUM: synthesis strongly supported by multiple texts but requiring inferential linkage.
- OPEN: material pressure point requiring dedicated exegesis.

## Ledger

### SE-01: Image-bearing personhood and delegated stewardship
**Texts:** Genesis 1:26-28; 2:15-17; 3:1-24.
**Direct warrant:** Humanity is made in God's image, receives dominion/stewardship, receives divine command, acts, and is held accountable.
**Supports:** D4-D6, A6-A7; real derivative agency and delegated authority.
**Limit:** "Image of God" alone does not formally define every faculty listed in D4.
**Confidence:** HIGH for personhood/stewardship/accountability; MEDIUM for the full philosophical agency model.

### SE-02: Distinct intentions in one providential event
**Text:** Genesis 50:20.
**Direct warrant:** Joseph predicates evil intention of his brothers and good intention of God concerning the same historical action/outcome.
**Supports:** A8, P1.
**Pressure:** The text supports dual intention strongly; it does not by itself provide a complete metaphysics of divine causality.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-03: Definite divine plan and culpable human action
**Text:** Acts 2:23.
**Direct warrant:** Jesus is delivered up according to God's definite plan and foreknowledge, while those who crucify him are described as culpable/lawless actors.
**Supports:** A4-A9, P1-P2.
**Pressure:** Any account that preserves only divine plan or only creaturely culpability fails to preserve the conjunction stated by the text.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-04: Predestination and named creaturely agents
**Text:** Acts 4:27-28.
**Direct warrant:** Herod, Pilate, Gentiles, and Israel act against Jesus while their acts fall within what God's hand and plan predestined to occur.
**Supports:** comprehensive ordination plus real creaturely action.
**Pressure:** The passage is a central test against models that reduce decree to mere foreknowledge.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-05: Degrees of culpability under derivative authority
**Text:** John 19:11.
**Direct warrant:** Pilate's authority is given "from above," while Jesus simultaneously speaks of "greater sin."
**Supports:** derivative authority, culpability, differentiated gravity of offense.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-06: Personal responsibility
**Text:** Ezekiel 18:20.
**Direct warrant:** The son does not bear the father's iniquity, nor the father the son's; wickedness is predicated of the wicked person.
**Supports:** H1's emphasis on personal guilt.
**Pressure:** Ezekiel addresses responsibility in its covenantal/prophetic context and cannot simply be asserted to settle Paul's Adamic argument in Romans 5.
**Confidence:** HIGH for its own claim; OPEN for its exact relation to original guilt.

### SE-07: Universal actual sin and culpability
**Texts:** Romans 1:18-32; 2:1-16; 3:9-23.
**Direct warrant:** Humanity suppresses known truth, acts unrighteously, and is brought under judgment; "all have sinned."
**Supports:** universal actual rebellion and personal culpability.
**Pressure:** These chapters establish actual universal sin but do not alone settle whether Adamic guilt is also imputed.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-08: Adam introduces sin and death
**Text:** Romans 5:12-14.
**Direct warrant:** Sin enters through one man, death through sin, death spreads to all, and death reigns from Adam to Moses.
**Supports:** Adamic historical headship, curse/death propagation.
**Pressure:** "because all sinned" and Paul's treatment of those whose sin differs from Adam require grammatical and contextual analysis.
**Confidence:** HIGH for Adamic causal significance; OPEN for H1.

### SE-09: One trespass, condemnation, and Adam-Christ parallel
**Text:** Romans 5:15-19.
**Direct warrant:** Paul's argument repeatedly connects one man's trespass/disobedience with death, condemnation, and the many being "made sinners," and contrasts this with Christ's obedience, righteousness, justification, and life.
**Supports:** strong Adamic solidarity and Christological representative structure.
**Pressure:** This is the strongest canonical pressure against H1. The non-imputation thesis must explain verses 16, 18, and 19 without weakening the parallel or importing its conclusion.
**Confidence:** HIGH that the pressure is real; OPEN on final disposition.

### SE-10: The Father's giving and the Son's effectual preservation
**Text:** John 6:37-40.
**Direct warrant:** Those the Father gives come to the Son; the Son receives them and declares the Father's will that he lose none but raise them at the last day.
**Supports:** D12-D14, A12; election/giving, effectual reception, perseverance, resurrection.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-11: Spirit of Truth and glorification of the Son
**Texts:** John 15:26; 16:7-15.
**Direct warrant:** The Spirit testifies about Christ, convicts the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment, guides into truth, declares what he hears, and glorifies the Son by taking what is the Son's and declaring it.
**Supports:** A2, D14, Trinitarian mission model.
**Pressure:** Personal mission language must not be converted into ontological inferiority.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-12: The Son's glory precedes creation
**Text:** John 17:1-5.
**Direct warrant:** Jesus speaks of glory with the Father before the world existed while also seeking glorification in the completion of his mission.
**Supports:** distinction between intrinsic/pre-creational glory and historical manifestation of glory.
**Pressure:** The model must never imply that God needed sin or redemption to supply a deficiency in the Son's glory.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-13: Election, purpose, redemption, and sealing
**Text:** Ephesians 1:3-14.
**Direct warrant:** The Father chooses in Christ before the foundation of the world, predestines according to his will, redemption is in the Beloved through his blood, God's purpose is set forth in Christ, inheritance is predestined according to divine counsel, and believers are sealed with the promised Spirit as guarantee of inheritance.
**Supports:** D12-D14, A11-A12, election-redemption-Spirit architecture.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-14: Divine purpose and preservation
**Text:** Romans 8:28-39.
**Direct warrant:** Calling, foreknowledge, predestination, justification, glorification, Christ's intercession, and inseparability from God's love form a preservation sequence.
**Supports:** decretive certainty of redemptive purpose and perseverance.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-15: Son as Creator, sustainer, reconciler, and telos
**Texts:** Colossians 1:15-20; Hebrews 1:1-4; John 1:1-3.
**Direct warrant:** Creation is through the Son; created things hold together in him; he sustains, reconciles, and is preeminent.
**Supports:** expanding the Son's mission beyond Judge/Redeemer to Creator, Sustainer, Lord, and Redeemer.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-16: Judgment according to actual deeds and knowledge
**Texts:** Romans 2:5-16; Luke 12:47-48; Matthew 11:20-24; Revelation 20:11-15.
**Direct warrant:** Judgment is according to works; greater knowledge/privilege can entail greater accountability; final judgment concerns persons and deeds.
**Supports:** A10, P6, individualized judgment and degrees of culpability.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-17: Mercy is sovereign rather than owed
**Texts:** Romans 9:14-24; Ephesians 2:1-10; Titus 3:3-7.
**Direct warrant:** Salvation is grounded in divine mercy/grace rather than human willing, running, or works.
**Supports:** A11, D12-D14.
**Pressure:** Romans 9 must be handled in its full argumentative context rather than isolated as a slogan.
**Confidence:** HIGH for grace/mercy not being earned; MEDIUM for every downstream election formulation until full exegesis.

### SE-18: Regeneration, indwelling, sanctification, and sealing
**Texts:** John 3:5-8; Romans 8:9-17; 1 Corinthians 6:11; 12:13; Ephesians 1:13-14; 4:30; Titus 3:5-7.
**Direct warrant:** New birth, indwelling, washing/sanctification, incorporation, sealing, and renewal are attributed to the Spirit.
**Supports:** D14 and A12.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

### SE-19: Glorified agency without apostasy
**Texts:** Revelation 21:1-27; 22:1-5; 1 John 3:2; 1 Corinthians 15:42-57.
**Direct warrant:** The final state is incorruptible, purified, and permanently ordered to God.
**Supports:** P3 as a theological inference: genuine redeemed personhood persists in a state from which corruption is absent.
**Pressure:** The exact modal claim "incapable of sin" is a theological inference from the consummated state rather than a phrase directly stated in these texts.
**Confidence:** MEDIUM.

### SE-20: Consummated judgment and restored communion
**Texts:** Revelation 20:11-15; 21:1-7; 22:1-5.
**Direct warrant:** Final judgment is followed by new creation, God's dwelling with his people, removal of death/curse, and worshipful communion.
**Supports:** terminal sequence of judgment, restoration, and consummation.
**Confidence:** HIGH.

## Synthesis ledger

### Strongly established conjunctions

1. Divine plan/predestination and culpable creaturely action are jointly affirmed in the crucifixion texts.
2. Creaturely evil intention and divine good intention can concern the same providential history without identity of moral intention.
3. The Father chooses/gives/purposes, the Son accomplishes redemption and preserves those given, and the Spirit testifies/applies/seals.
4. Actual human sin and personal judgment are pervasive canonical categories.
5. Adam's act has consequences extending beyond Adam.
6. The Son's glory precedes creation; redemptive history manifests divine glory rather than supplying a divine deficiency.

### Claims requiring further derivation

1. The exact philosophical definition of moral agency.
2. The three-modal formalism: logical, decretive, dispositional necessity.
3. The claim that universal post-Fall sin is dispositionally necessary.
4. The precise relation between inherited corruption and culpability.
5. The modal inference from glorification to impeccability.

### Principal unresolved issue

**H1: inherited curse/corruption without inherited Adamic personal guilt.**

Romans 5:12-19 supplies the primary canonical pressure. Ezekiel 18:20 and Romans 1-3 establish important personal-responsibility data, but they cannot be used to bypass Paul's Adam-Christ argument.

The next research artifact shall therefore be `romans-5-pressure-test.md`.
