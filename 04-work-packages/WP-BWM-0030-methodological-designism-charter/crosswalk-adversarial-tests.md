## Initial adversarial tests

### A. DFM and radiogenic mechanisms
A failed accelerated-decay mechanism bears on that mechanism and any model materially dependent on it. DFM may survive if it was not precommitted to accelerated decay and can supply a different admissible account. Repeated replacement mechanisms without added constraint or evidence would count toward degeneration.

**Result:** framework constrains DFM rather than immunizing it.

### B. DFM and functional initialization
DFM may posit initialized functional state, but "designed that way" is insufficient by itself. A useful DFM proposal must identify which observations follow from the initialized state, which are underdetermined, and which would count against a particular initialization model.

**Result:** design remains admissible while acquiring evidential obligations.

### C. PFH duration and demographic scenarios
PFH as a Historical Framework is broader than any particular long-duration scenario. Failure of a 50–60 kyr demographic reconstruction does not automatically reject PFH. If PFH becomes materially dependent on that duration through prior commitments, post-hoc demotion is prohibited.

**Result:** existing BWM architecture fits dependency governance.

### D. CHFM mechanism
BWM may consume CHFM's catastrophic Flood framework while individual tectonic, pore-pressure, thermal, or geochemical mechanisms remain testable. "The Flood was catastrophic" cannot be used to report an unresolved heat or transport mechanism as solved.

**Result:** interface conformance can preserve external source authority while enforcing BWM epistemic discipline.

### E. Conventional cosmology
BWM cannot reject a conventional cosmological model merely because its hard core excludes design as a scientific explanation. Specific empirical, logical, explanatory, or dependency arguments must be made.

Conversely, methodological naturalism cannot empirically falsify DFM merely by declaring design inadmissible.

**Result:** framework-disagreement rule is symmetric.

### F. Abiogenesis
Failure of a particular abiogenesis pathway bears first on that pathway. It does not automatically falsify every naturalistic origin-of-life programme. Repeated failed pathways can create cumulative programme pressure when dependencies, alternatives, and rescue behavior are documented.

BWM likewise cannot treat current mechanistic gaps as direct proof of a specific design mechanism.

**Result:** hierarchy plus degeneration rule avoids mirror-image gap arguments.

### G. Hermeneutical pressure
If an empirical model conflicts with a BWM interpretation, BWM may reexamine the interpretation. A reading invented only to remove the conflict fails BWM-MD-005 unless it is independently exegetically admissible.

**Result:** the protective belt cannot expand through unlimited textual elasticity.

### H. Inner certainty
Strong inward certainty does not raise an interpretation or model above testing. Claimed counsel that conflicts with Scripture is rejected as a failed attribution.

**Result:** internal counsel cannot become an epistemic escape hatch.
