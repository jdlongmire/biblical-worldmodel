## Adversarial review targets

1. Hindsight bias: do not make the present framework appear inevitable.
2. False binary: Ham/Ross are representative poles in the author's experience, not exhaustive taxonomy.
3. Self-exoneration: harmonization must be described as a serious good-faith project.
4. Category leakage: distinguish methodological naturalism from metaphysical naturalism.
5. Initialization overreach: operational-state analogies establish a modeling caution, not by themselves a creation chronology.
6. Authority/evidence confusion: biblical authority does not make a particular geological or cosmological auxiliary true.
7. Exemplar proof-texting: intellectual exemplars supply tools and critiques, not borrowed authority for BWM conclusions.
8. Documentary compression: dense 2024–2026 records must not be mistaken for the beginning or full duration of a roughly thirty-five-year journey.
9. Failed-auxiliary sanitization: preserve the fact that some recent mechanisms were criticized, revised, downgraded, or abandoned rather than narrating every step as inevitable progress.
