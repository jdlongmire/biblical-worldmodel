# WP-BWM-0036 — Intellectual Journey and Biographical Narrative

## Purpose

Develop a first-person intellectual biography explaining how the Biblical World Model (BWM), Christian Methodological Designism, regulative supernaturalism, and related research programmes emerged from the author's theological formation, professional systems practice, and iterative engagement with competing explanatory frameworks.

The narrative is autobiographical before it is argumentative. It records the route by which the present methodology was reached, including positions later revised, rather than retroactively presenting the current framework as though it had been present from the beginning.

## Governing narrative question

> How did I move from inherited young-creation biblical confidence, through serious old-earth and ANE harmonization, to asking whether there was a third way whose rules of inquiry themselves needed to be reconsidered?

## Core narrative arc

1. Fundamentalist formation: strong material confidence in biblical authority; young creation received as the straightforward reading; gratitude for that inheritance while recognizing that the form received supplied convictions more fully than tools for interrogating sophisticated competing presuppositions.
2. Professional formation in systems: network technician through IT and enterprise architecture and systems engineering, with exposure to defense-system modeling and simulation; architecture, requirements, interfaces, boundary conditions, initialization, model fidelity, traceability, verification, validation, and the distinction between a system and its model.
3. Permission to question: the PCA creation-study process as a major milestone opening legitimate Reformed space to investigate old-earth and alternative Genesis interpretations without treating investigation itself as abandonment of biblical authority. Historical details require primary-source verification before publication.
4. Harmonization: serious engagement with old-earth reconstruction, natural science, and ANE/literary frameworks. Treat this period sympathetically rather than as an obvious error.
5. Presuppositional capitulation: first recognized within the author's own systematic theology. Working definition: the progressive, often individually plausible, accommodation of one's systematic commitments to the governing assumptions of another worldview until those assumptions acquire functional authority over the original system. This is first a self-diagnosis, not a label for old-earth Christians generally.
6. Third-way question: resistance to an apparent choice between conventional Ken Ham/YEC and Hugh Ross/OEC packages. The emerging programme changes the methodological axis rather than seeking a midpoint.
7. Regulative supernaturalism: if Scripture warrants supernatural divine action in the history of creation, explanations of the resulting world must permit that action to function as a genuine historical cause. Supernatural causation remains accountable to ordered explanation and testing.
8. Iterative exemplars: Christ the Logos; apostolic Scripture; Van Til, Aristotle, Socrates, Reid, Plantinga, Aquinas, Gödel, Lakatos, and Bayes. Their contributions were encountered iteratively and remain subordinate to Scripture.
9. Coming back differently: present creation conclusions resemble important parts of the inherited position, while the epistemic architecture has changed substantially. BWM is an architecture for disciplined inquiry under biblical authority with defeasible models exposed to empirical, logical, historical, and exegetical pressure.

## Narrative constraints

Write in first person. Preserve gratitude toward fundamentalist formation while naming its limitations in the author's experience. Do not equate old-earth interpretation with presuppositional capitulation. Present presuppositional capitulation first as self-critique. Do not caricature Ken Ham, Hugh Ross, the PCA, ANE scholarship, or methodological naturalism. Distinguish observation, inference, model, hypothesis, and historical reconstruction. Do not imply that supernaturalism removes explanatory burden. Avoid retrospective inevitability. Preserve openness to falsification and explicit unresolved burdens. Keep Christ the Logos and Scripture's authority structurally governing without using the exemplars as substitute authorities.
