## Content thesis

The video examines why many Christians feel pressured to accept a naturalistic account of origins. It distinguishes operational science from historical reconstruction: scientists observe present states, calculate ages, and infer histories. Converging evidence deserves serious attention, but consilience does not by itself establish that a naturalistic reconstruction exhausts the possible history of the system.

The Christian claim adds a second evidentiary source. God's Word and God's world share an Author. If God has given trustworthy testimony concerning creation, that testimony bears on origins even though methodological naturalism, by design, cannot admit revelation as a scientific cause.

The governing public formulation is:

> We observe present states. We calculate ages. We reconstruct histories.

Calculated age may correspond to actual elapsed history, and convergence can strengthen that correspondence, but calculated age does not necessitate actual age.
