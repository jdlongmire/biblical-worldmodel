# Tranche 1 Source Notes

## Kepler

A 26 March 1598 letter to Herwart von Hohenburg is consistently identified as the source for Kepler's description of astronomers as priests of God with respect to the book of nature. Publication should wait for direct verification against the critical edition, *Gesammelte Werke*, vol. XIII, p. 193, or a reliable critical translation.

## Boyle

Boyle's *The Christian Virtuoso* (1690) directly argues that commitment to experimental philosophy is compatible with and may assist Christian commitment. The Robert Boyle Project also confirms the relevant Boyle corpus, including work on final causes and the study of nature.

## Faraday

Specialist historical scholarship attributes to Faraday's 1844 memorandum on matter explicit statements connecting material creation with laws and the Creator. Until the manuscript or critical primary edition is inspected, BWM should cite the primary source as cited in the secondary scholarship.

## Maxwell

Primary published material supports a narrow claim that Maxwell connected molecular uniformity, creation, and theological meaning. This can support a claim about Maxwell. It should not be generalized to Victorian physics without additional evidence.

## Huxley and Victorian uniformity

Matthew Stanley's specialist scholarship supports a qualified historiographic conclusion that theistic and naturalistic scientists could share commitment to uniform natural law and substantial scientific practice while grounding uniformity differently. Huxley's own primary texts remain to be extracted for claim-level verification.
