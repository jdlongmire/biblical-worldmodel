## Intent

Establish the public BWM objection-response register promised by the existing Objections page and use it to retain substantive objections at their strongest form, their current response, disposition, and any remaining explanatory burden.

## Authority and boundaries

BWM owns the public integration register. Canonical programme repositories retain authority for programme-specific technical claims. A BWM response may summarize, route, or classify an objection, but may not silently settle an open technical burden owned by DFM, CHFM, PFH, Post-Flood Diversity, or another canonical programme.

This package does not authorize merge or production publication without Principal Operator acceptance.

## Initial capture

The first detailed entry records the recurring "trickster God / Last Thursdayism" objection to Designed Functional Maturity, including the stronger distant-supernova form. It preserves the distinction between functional maturity and fabricated chronicle while retaining the physical modeling burden associated with information-bearing astronomical states across the creation boundary.

The package also records **aesthetic-revelatory function** as a legitimate BWM dimension of created function. Scripture presents creation as observable, intelligible, abundant, and wonder-producing, not merely mechanically sufficient. This category may help explain observational richness beyond minimal life-support requirements, but it may not be used to justify fabricated chronicle or replace a causal account.

## Acceptance criteria

- Public register exists and is linked from the Objections page and site navigation.
- Objections are steelmanned rather than weakened.
- Each entry identifies target, response, disposition, and remaining burden.
- The register distinguishes an answered philosophical/category objection from unresolved or partially resolved technical mechanisms.
- The manuscript objection ledger cross-references the specific trickster/supernova form.
- No entry treats initialization as a universal escape from contrary evidence.
- Aesthetic-revelatory function is defined without treating beauty as proof of design or as permission for fictional event records.
- The working taxonomy distinguishes functional maturity, causal maturity, aesthetic-revelatory function, and chronicle maturity.
