## BWM analytical connection

The most important prospective BWM harvest is methodological. Historical geology repeatedly turns present observations into claims about unobserved antecedent states. The historical record surrounding uniformitarianism and catastrophism can therefore function as a case study for the BWM distinction between operational regularity and retrodictive entailment. Historical research must establish what Lyell and later geologists actually claimed before BWM draws that inference.
