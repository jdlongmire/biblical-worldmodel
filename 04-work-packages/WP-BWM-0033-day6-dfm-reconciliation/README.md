## Reconciled architecture

Day 6 contains three analytically distinguishable transitions:

1. **Human creation / deployment.** Adam and Eve are actualized as human creatures within the cumulative sixth creation phase.
2. **Human commissioning.** Humanity receives vocation, moral boundary, marriage, blessing, reproduction, stewardship, and dominion. The human office is inaugurated.
3. **Ordinary exercise / runtime.** The commissioned human vocation is subsequently exercised in history. BWM's PFH investigates the pre-Fall portion of that runtime without assigning a duration Scripture does not state.

In DFM notation, Day 6 is the final creation-week transition:

```text
S_5 --G_6--> S_6
```

where `G_6` includes the divine acts by which terrestrial animal life is populated and humanity is created and commissioned. `S_6` is the completed, evaluated "very good" creation state at the close of Day 6.

The transition into subsequent ordinary history is conceptually distinct:

```text
S_6 --F_L^t--> S_6(t)
```

where `F_L^t` denotes ordinary creaturely operation under the stable created order for elapsed history `t`. This notation does not imply that every human action is reducible to physical law. It marks the provenance/operation boundary only.

## Genesis 2 relationship

Genesis 2:7–25 supplies higher-resolution human detail within the Day 6 phase represented compactly in Genesis 1:26–31. It therefore expands the internal structure of `G_6`; it does not constitute a second creation sequence in the model.

The Day 6 internal sequence retained from WP-BWM-0018 is:

1. Adam is formed and receives life.
2. Adam is placed in Eden and receives vocation.
3. Adam receives the prohibition/boundary.
4. God declares Adam's solitude not good.
5. Animals are brought to Adam and named.
6. Adam experiences the absence of a corresponding helper.
7. Eve is built and brought to Adam.
8. Adam recognizes her as his corresponding human counterpart.
9. Marriage is constituted/stated.
10. Humanity jointly receives blessing, multiplication, dominion, and the commission to fill and subdue.
11. God evaluates the completed creation as very good.

The exact clock schedule inside the day remains a reconstruction variable. WP-BWM-0018's quantitative naming model remains a feasibility test rather than a historical timetable.

## Designed Functional Maturity implication

Day 6 provides unusually direct textual evidence for functional maturity at the human level. Adam is presented as immediately capable of:

- understanding divine communication;
- receiving vocation and prohibition;
- acting intentionally in a task;
- recognizing and differentiating creaturely categories;
- assigning names;
- apprehending relational correspondence and absence;
- recognizing Eve as a human counterpart;
- entering the commissioned human vocation.

This constrains an admissible DFM model of human initialization. Human functional maturity must include whatever cognitive, linguistic, moral, relational, and embodied capacities are required by these textual actions.

The text does not specify the implementation mechanism, the complete contents of Adam's initialized knowledge, or the boundary between initialized knowledge and learning during Day 6. Those remain open research questions.

## Epistemic classification

- Adam is formed and receives life from God.
- Adam receives vocation and prohibition.
- Animals are brought to Adam and named.
- No corresponding helper is found among them.
- Eve is made and brought to Adam.
- Humanity receives blessing, multiplication, dominion, and food provision.
- God evaluates the completed creation as very good.

### Strong textual/canonical inferences
- Genesis 2 provides increased resolution on the human events associated with Day 6.
- The joint commission follows the existence of male and female because Genesis 1:28 addresses them plurally.
- Day 6 establishes the starting conditions for subsequent human history.

### Analytical abstractions
- `S_5 --G_6--> S_6`.
- "deployment," "commissioning boundary," and "runtime" as systems-language descriptions.
- Treating Genesis 2 as an internal decomposition of `G_6`.

### Open model questions
- Exact intra-day timing.
- Exact number of named created kinds.
- Exact scope of initialized human knowledge.
- Exact duration of pre-Fall history.
- Physical details of supernatural initialization not supplied by the text.
