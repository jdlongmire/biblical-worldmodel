# WP-BWM-0032 — Book Corpus Harvest and Manuscript Foundation

## Parent

**WP-BWM-0031 — God's Word, God's World: Making Sense of All the Noise**

## Purpose

Build the evidentiary, editorial, and manuscript foundation required to draft *God's Word, God's World* as a coherent book rather than a compilation of existing programme material.

This package executes the pre-drafting phase authorized by WP-BWM-0031.

## Governing principle

> **Harvest before drafting.**

The existing corpus contains substantial argumentation, examples, distinctions, sources, and formulations relevant to the book. Drafting should begin only after that material is inventoried, dispositioned, and mapped to the manuscript architecture.

The book remains reader-facing. Research programmes supply intellectual infrastructure; they do not determine the manuscript's organization.

## Scope

### 1. Corpus harvest ledger

Perform a structured sweep beginning with BWM and extending to associated repositories and artifacts where relevant, including:

- Biblical World Model
- DFM
- Methodological Designism
- LRT
- TRT
- MXM / mind and consciousness material
- thinx-tank
- relevant papers, work packages, public articles, and debate material containing unusually clear lay formulations

For every candidate artifact record:

- repository
- path / canonical source
- title or identifier
- concept / argument
- candidate chapter(s)
- epistemic status
- accessibility level
- source/citation status
- disposition:
  - include
  - adapt/rewrite
  - sidebar/example
  - cite/reference
  - technical appendix
  - underlying corpus only
  - exclude
- superseded or conflicting formulations
- notes

Do not assume all corpus material belongs in the book.

### 2. Chapter claim sheets

Create a claim sheet for Chapters 1–14 and the epilogue.

Each sheet shall identify:

- governing question
- chapter purpose
- 3–6 principal claims
- reader outcome
- required concepts
- dependencies on earlier chapters
- concepts intentionally deferred
- primary worked examples
- corpus sources
- external sources requiring research
- epistemic-status concerns
- anticipated objections
- transition into the next chapter
- provisional word-count range from WP-BWM-0031

The claim sheets become the immediate control documents for drafting.

### 3. Source and verification ledger

Build the source ledger concurrently with harvesting.

Source discipline:

- search for primary sources first
- never fabricate citations
- never present a secondary source as primary
- when primary material is accessible only through a secondary source, use “as cited in”
- state when only secondary evidence is available
- report material divergence among sources rather than silently harmonizing
- distinguish source claims from author inference
- verify quotations before manuscript use
- record page/section/location data where available

Confidence notation:

- **HIGH** — verified primary source
- **MEDIUM** — reliable secondary source with specific location/page support
- **LOW** — paraphrase, tertiary treatment, or incomplete sourcing
- **UNCERTAIN** — cannot presently assess

The final manuscript need not expose these internal labels everywhere; the ledger uses them to govern drafting reliability.

### 4. Recommended-voices inventory

Develop the Epilogue recommended-voices inventory early so it can also serve as an intellectual cross-check and bibliography-development mechanism.

Candidate domains:

- biblical interpretation and theology
- philosophy and apologetics
- philosophy of science
- origins and creation
- biology and origin-of-life chemistry
- information
- mind and consciousness
- AI and computation
- serious skeptical/naturalistic interlocutors worth reading directly

For each voice record:

**Voice → Specific contribution → Where I differ / caveat → Where to begin → Relevant chapters**

Inclusion is granular and must never imply wholesale endorsement.

### 5. Book Constitution

Create a concise editorial constitution governing all manuscript development.

Initial rules:

1. **Define before arguing.**
2. **Concrete before abstract.**
3. **Scripture interprets Scripture first.**
4. **Distinguish revelation, observation, inference, model, hypothesis, and speculation.**
5. **Apply methodological scrutiny symmetrically.**
6. **State uncertainty rather than conceal it.**
7. **Introduce programme machinery only when the reader needs it.**
8. **Primary sources before secondary sources.**
9. **Never protect one of our hypotheses from evidence.**
10. **Never confuse our worldmodel with God's world.**
11. **Christ the Logos is the ultimate point of coherence, never a component inside one of our models.**
12. **Word-count ranges govern proportion and pacing, not content.**
13. **Do not pad to a target or cut necessary argument merely to satisfy a target.**
14. **A reader should not need to master BWM, DFM, Methodological Designism, LRT, TRT, PFH, CHFM, or their acronyms to understand the book.**
15. **The manuscript must model the epistemic conduct it advocates.**

This constitution may be refined during harvesting but changes should preserve the governing commitments of WP-BWM-0031.

### 7. Explicit exclusion ledger

Maintain `EXCLUDED.md` for material reviewed and intentionally omitted or deferred.

Record:

- artifact
- reason for exclusion
- whether it remains useful in the technical corpus
- whether reconsideration is warranted for a later edition or appendix

This is a scope-control mechanism and prevents repeatedly reconsidering the same material.

### 8. Pilot drafting gate

Do not begin full sequential manuscript drafting immediately after the harvest.

Draft two pilot chapters first:

#### Pilot A — Chapter 1: Drowning in the Noise

Tests:

- public-facing voice
- accessibility
- narrative opening
- theological register
- audience assumptions
- balance between concrete examples and epistemic framing

#### Pilot B — Chapter 6: The Map Is Not the Territory

Tests:

- translation of technical epistemology into accessible prose
- observation/inference/model distinctions
- retrodiction and model assumptions
- methodological symmetry
- ability to use BWM/Designism insights without requiring programme mastery

The two pilots jointly test the book's principal registers.

Full manuscript drafting should begin only after both pilots are reviewed against the Book Constitution and the chapter claim sheets.

## Execution sequence

### Phase 1 — Foundation
- create Book Constitution
- establish manuscript workspace
- establish ledger templates
- establish chapter claim-sheet template

### Phase 2 — BWM harvest
- sweep canonical BWM corpus
- map artifacts to manuscript chapters
- identify superseded/conflicting material
- populate source gaps

### Phase 3 — Associated-programme harvest
- DFM
- Methodological Designism
- LRT
- TRT
- mind/MXM material
- other relevant corpus sources

### Phase 4 — External-source verification
- verify existing sources
- locate primary sources
- identify research gaps
- populate recommended voices

### Phase 5 — Chapter architecture
- complete all claim sheets
- resolve duplication
- confirm intentional deferrals
- review manuscript proportions

### Phase 6 — Pilot drafting
- draft Chapter 1
- draft Chapter 6
- evaluate against Constitution
- revise architecture if necessary

### Phase 7 — Drafting authorization
Produce a readiness assessment recommending whether to proceed into full manuscript drafting and identifying unresolved source, architecture, or epistemic issues.

## Completion criteria

WP-BWM-0032 is complete when:

- the relevant corpus has been systematically reviewed rather than selectively recalled;
- every chapter has a defined argumentative purpose and claims;
- major manuscript claims have traceable source paths or explicit research gaps;
- superseded corpus formulations are identified;
- excluded material is dispositioned;
- the recommended-voices inventory includes contribution-specific caveats;
- the manuscript workspace is ready for sustained drafting;
- Chapters 1 and 6 demonstrate a viable reader-facing register;
- programme jargon remains subordinate to reader comprehension;
- no identified hypothesis is insulated from evidentiary or exegetical challenge;
- the project is ready for deliberate full-manuscript drafting.

## Initial disposition

**Authorized for execution.**

WP-BWM-0031 remains the governing book-concept package. WP-BWM-0032 is the pre-drafting execution package and may not alter the book's foundational theological or epistemic commitments without Principal Operator disposition.
