## Objective

Systematically harvest relevant work by Dr. Hugh Ross and Reasons to Believe (RTB) for potential use within Designed Functional Maturity (DFM) and the broader Biblical WorldModel, while preserving a strict distinction between useful evidence, primary-source trails, arguments, and Ross/RTB's broader old-earth/progressive-creation framework.

The purpose is evidence discovery and comparative analysis. Ross/RTB is not a canonical authority for DFM.

## Core Harvest Rule

For each candidate item, record the chain:

`Ross/RTB claim -> underlying primary source -> observed fact -> Ross interpretation -> DFM relevance -> DFM divergence -> evidential status`

Where Ross/RTB cites scientific literature, follow the citation to the original primary source whenever accessible. Do not substitute an RTB summary for the primary literature when the original source can be obtained.

## Priority Harvest Domains

1. Cosmological fine-tuning and life-permitting parameter space.
2. Early-universe mass density, expansion conditions, entropy, and initial conditions.
3. Stellar nucleosynthesis and elemental abundance constraints.
4. Galactic, stellar-system, planetary, and terrestrial habitability.
5. Earth-Moon-Sun configuration and biosphere requirements.
6. RTB design-compendium material as an evidence-discovery index.
7. Ross/RTB model-testing and falsifiability methodology.
8. Arguments and explanatory structures that translate technical astrophysics into design-relevant reasoning.
9. Areas where Ross accepts conventional deep-time historical reconstruction and DFM asks whether the evidence entails that history or only the observed mature state.
10. High-value video/audio resources suitable for the Biblical WorldModel Video Highlights collection.

## Required Classification

Each harvested item should distinguish at least:

- source type and provenance;
- primary source located: yes/no;
- observed or measured fact;
- model-dependent inference;
- Ross/RTB interpretation;
- DFM compatibility;
- DFM divergence;
- potential DFM evidential-ledger relevance;
- confidence/status;
- unresolved technical questions;
- public-site suitability;
- video-highlight suitability where applicable.

## Alignment Discipline

Ross/RTB material must not be presented as though Ross's complete WorldModel is aligned with DFM. Relevant fine-tuning, design, astrophysical, or methodological material may be compatible with or useful to DFM while Ross's chronology, progressive-creation framework, Genesis interpretation, and acceptance of conventional deep-time cosmological reconstruction can differ materially from DFM.

Public-facing harvested media shall use the site's external-resource alignment disclosure and an item-specific alignment note.

## Research Questions

The harvest should repeatedly test:

1. What is directly observed or measured?
2. What historical reconstruction is inferred from those observations?
3. Which inference depends on uniform backward extrapolation of normalized physical regularities?
4. Does the evidence require the proposed historical trajectory, or is it also compatible with a functionally mature initialized state?
5. Does the item increase, decrease, or leave unchanged the comparative explanatory burden for DFM versus specified rivals?
6. Does Ross/RTB identify a primary scientific source, and does that source support the strength of the public claim?
