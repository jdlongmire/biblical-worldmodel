# Auxiliary Hypothesis Non-Credit Principle

**Status:** Principal Operator approved methodology candidate, 2026-09-28  
**Owner:** WP-BWM-0009 — Canonical Epistemic Hierarchy and Claim-Level Evaluation  
**Applies to:** L2 auxiliary hypotheses and any higher-level claim whose warrant depends upon them.

## Principle

> **The availability of an auxiliary hypothesis, mathematical model, simulation, or physically possible mechanism does not constitute evidential support for its historical instantiation. Explanatory credit increases only to the extent that the mechanism is independently constrained, shown applicable under the relevant boundary conditions, produces expected historical traces, and survives discriminating comparison with viable alternatives.**

Compact form:

```text
proposed
  != possible
  != modeled
  != demonstrated process
  != historically instantiated
  != historically sufficient
  != discriminatively corroborated
```

The existence of literature about a proposed mechanism is not itself an evidential state.

## Evidential states

- **E0 — Speculative:** proposed without a sufficiently demonstrated physical mechanism or specified model.
- **E1 — Modeled:** mathematical/computational model produces a relevant outcome under stipulated parameters.
- **E2 — Demonstrated process:** the physical process is observed or experimentally demonstrated under defined conditions.
- **E3 — Historically corroborated mechanism:** independent evidence supports operation of the mechanism in the target historical system.
- **E4 — Discriminatively corroborated explanation:** the mechanism survives comparison with viable alternatives and successfully accounts for independent or novel evidence not merely used to tune it.

No wording shall silently promote a proposition between these states.

## Mechanism-credit rule

A mechanism proposed in response to anomaly O receives no historical explanatory credit merely because it can be written as:

```text
M(theta) -> O
```

The relevant burden is:

```text
M(theta_independent)
 + applicable boundary conditions
 + expected historical traces
 + successful discriminator
 -> increased warrant
```

where `theta_independent` means parameters independently measured or constrained where feasible rather than freely fitted to O.

## Simulation rule

> A simulation reproducing an observation demonstrates capability of the modeled system under its encoded assumptions. It does not demonstrate that the simulated history occurred.

Simulation evidence must identify:
- governing equations;
- initialization;
- boundary conditions;
- fitted versus independently constrained parameters;
- omitted processes;
- sensitivity;
- predicted traces;
- observational discriminator.

## Experimental rule

> Demonstrating a mechanism in the laboratory establishes physical capability under the demonstrated conditions. Historical application additionally requires evidence that materially relevant conditions obtained in the historical system.

Scaling in duration, energy, concentration, geometry, environment, or system size must be justified rather than assumed.

## Auxiliary classification

For Lakatosian programme appraisal:

- **A-P Progressive:** auxiliary generates independently corroborated empirical content, risky/discriminating predictions, or genuine unification beyond accommodation of the motivating anomaly.
- **A-N Neutral/provisional:** auxiliary provides a plausible/model-capable response but lacks adequate independent historical corroboration.
- **A-D Degenerative:** auxiliary principally accommodates known difficulties through flexibility, unconstrained parameters, or serial rescue without commensurate independent evidential gain.

A post-hoc auxiliary is not automatically degenerative. It may become progressive through later independent corroboration. A pre-existing mechanism is not automatically progressive merely because it predates the anomaly.

## Symmetry rule

This principle applies identically to:
- conventional naturalistic auxiliaries;
- DFM initialization hypotheses;
- Flood/CHFM boundary-condition mechanisms;
- PFH reconstructions;
- cosmological auxiliaries;
- evolutionary mechanisms;
- abiogenesis pathways;
- associated TRT/LRT applications where historical mechanism claims are made.

Examples:

- Inflation does not receive historical credit merely because inflationary models can fit cosmological observables.
- Streaming instability does not receive historical credit merely because simulations produce planetesimal collapse.
- Iron-mediated stabilization does not establish million-year fossil preservation merely because the chemistry can retard degradation.
- A broad unspecified functional target does not erase an evolutionary waiting-time result unless target size/pathways are independently grounded.
- "Designed functional initialization" does not scientifically explain arbitrary observations unless DFM constrains admissible initialization and derives testable consequences.
- "Flood-altered boundary conditions" does not explain an anomaly until the changed conditions, mechanism, magnitude, duration and predicted traces are specified.

## Anti-rescue dependency rule

WP-BWM-0009's existing dependency/precommitment rule remains controlling:

> A claim cannot be treated after failure as dispensable if the parent model or programme was previously committed to, or materially dependent upon, that claim.

The Non-Credit Principle adds the reciprocal protection:

> A newly proposed auxiliary cannot be treated as though it has repaired the parent model merely by restoring logical or numerical compatibility with the anomaly.

Compatibility restored is not explanatory debt discharged.

## Auxiliary explanatory-debt ledger

For every material L2 auxiliary record:

```text
Anomaly/problem
-> auxiliary
-> evidential state E0-E4
-> independently constrained parameters
-> historical trace
-> novel/discriminating prediction
-> test result
-> A-P / A-N / A-D
-> effect on parent model/programme
```

Unpaid explanatory debt remains visible in the parent model's appraisal.

## Language control

Use verbs according to warranted state:

- **E0:** proposes, conjectures
- **E1:** models, simulates, can reproduce under stipulated assumptions
- **E2:** demonstrates physical capability under specified conditions
- **E3:** is independently supported as historically operative
- **E4:** is discriminatively corroborated as an explanation

Avoid **explains, resolves, establishes, demonstrates the history, accounts for historically** below the corresponding evidential burden.

## AI/research-agent control

Research agents working under BWM shall not convert:
- "a paper proposes X" into "X explains O";
- "a simulation reproduces O" into "O occurred by X";
- "X is physically possible" into "X is historically probable";
- "the literature has a response" into "the anomaly is resolved."

When presenting an auxiliary, agents must report its evidential state and outstanding explanatory debt when material to the conclusion.
