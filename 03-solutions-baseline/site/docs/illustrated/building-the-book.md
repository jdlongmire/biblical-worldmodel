# Building the Book

*The Biblical World Model: An Illustrated Atlas of Creation, History, and Reality* is conceived as an oversized, illustrated coffee-table book. Its first iteration is this site. That order is deliberate: spreads are composed, reviewed, and published here first, where they can be corrected cheaply; the printed book is then built from finished spreads, not discovered at the printer.

## The options

### Option A - Print-on-demand hardcover proof

A small number of hardcover copies produced one at a time by a print-on-demand service.

- **Best for:** the first physical object - checking trim, colour, legibility, and gutter behaviour in the hand before any large commitment.
- **Requires:** print-ready interior and cover files at the chosen trim, which print-on-demand services accept as standard PDFs.
- **Limits:** unit cost is high, trim sizes and papers are restricted to the service's list, and finish quality sits below a true coffee-table edition. A proof route, not the final form.

### Option B - Premium offset hardcover

A conventional print run of the oversized hardcover the concept describes: premium matte stock, generous format, sewn binding.

- **Best for:** the book as conceived, once the plate set is complete or substantial.
- **Requires:** everything Option A requires, at offset standard - CMYK-proofed colour, printer-selected bleed and safe areas, a chosen printer, and the economics of a print run (quantity, storage, fulfilment).
- **Limits:** the largest cost and the least forgiving of late corrections. Every gate below must pass first.

### Option C - The staged path (recommended)

Publish serially on this site; freeze each spread only when its review gates pass; rebuild print masters from the frozen spreads; run Option A to hold a proof copy and correct against it; then decide Option B with the proof in hand.

- **Best for:** keeping every option open while the book grows, and spending print money only on verified material.
- **Cost:** time. Print waits until the masters exist.

## The gates before any print option

These apply whichever route is chosen, and they are stated here so the book's status is never oversold:

1. **Review gates per spread** - Scripture quotations and references checked, theological review, scientific and historical review where the plate makes such claims, and a visual accuracy check. On the record today, both filed plates are at gate zero: concept prototypes, unreviewed.
2. **Print masters** - artwork separated from lettering: art-only images plus editable typesetting, at 300 ppi at the ruled trim (3600 x 2700 px per page before bleed). The plates on this site are web-resolution copies with the lettering baked in, and the cover as supplied is a rendered book mock rather than flat cover art. None of it is a print master yet.
3. **Production decisions** - trim and orientation are ruled: **12 x 9 in landscape** (13 November 2026), with individual landscape page plates as the production format under the book's adopted plate style guide; the portrait plates published so far are concepts to be recomposed, not cropped. Still open: the part structure of the book, and printer selection, from which bleed, safe areas, and colour proofing follow.
