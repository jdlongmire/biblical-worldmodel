# Earth Systems

Topical plates of the atlas: the created earth's systems, their
evidence, and their implications for Earth's history, treated under
the same discipline as the day plates (what Scripture states, what
the data show, and what the model infers, kept distinct on the plate
itself).

## Radiogenic Heat Budget and Radiometric Decay

![Radiogenic Heat Budget and Radiometric Decay: a cross-section of the earth (crust, mantle, core) with the sources of its heat, an isotope table, the present-day heat budget, and the mechanisms by which geoheat and hydrotectonics can influence age extrapolation](assets/011-radiogenic-heat-budget-landscape.jpg)

*Plate (record [BWM-IL-011](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel/blob/main/bwm-illustrated/illustrated-plates/011-radiogenic-heat-budget-landscape.md)) - Genesis 1:11-13; 1:31.*

The first Earth Systems plate, supplied by the author and filed as
supplied. It sets out the earth's heat budget (radiogenic heat from
the decay of uranium, thorium, and potassium; primordial heat; core
heat), the basics of radioactive decay with the standard half-lives,
and the ways heating, fluids, and rapid large-scale activity can
reset or bias radiometric systems. Its interpretive summary states
the distinction plainly: a created, ordered, and productive earth is
what Scripture states; an earth endowed with significant radiogenic
and primordial heat, whose dynamic processes can influence
radiometric age extrapolations, is the interpretive model.
