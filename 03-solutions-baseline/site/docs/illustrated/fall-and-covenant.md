# Fall and Covenant

Topical plates of the atlas: the Fall, the breach of the Edenic
covenant, and the covenant of grace instituted at the Fall and
carried through redemptive history, treated under the same
discipline as the day plates (what Scripture states, what the data
show, and what the model infers, kept distinct on the plate
itself).

## The Fall and the Lapsarian Covenant

![The Fall and the Lapsarian Covenant: Adam and Eve after the Fall beneath the serpent in the tree, with the lamb and the cross in sunrise light; panels on the Lapsarian Covenant, the Protoevangelium, the breach, a covenantal timeline, parallel covenants, and theological implications](assets/020-the-fall-and-the-lapsarian-covenant-landscape.jpg)

*Plate (record [BWM-IL-020](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel/blob/main/bwm-illustrated/illustrated-plates/020-the-fall-and-the-lapsarian-covenant-landscape.md)) - Genesis 3:15; Romans 5:12-21.*

The first Fall and Covenant plate, supplied by the author and filed
as supplied. It sets out the author's covenant reading of the Fall:
the eternal Covenant of Grace, purposed in eternity (Eph. 1:4-6),
enters time and is instituted at the Fall as the Lapsarian Covenant
(Gen. 3:15), assuring redemption for a people in Adam, fulfilled in
Christ, the last Adam (Rom. 5:12-21). Its panels carry the
Protoevangelium (Gen. 3:15, quoted), the fourfold account of the
breach, a four-tile covenantal timeline from Creation to
Fulfillment, the parallel covenants (works before the Fall, grace
after it), and the theological implications. Its key takeaway: the
Fall brought judgment, but also the Lapsarian Covenant, God's
eternal Covenant of Grace entering time.
