# Start your journey

You do not need to know the programme names to begin. Start with a question you care about, and follow the evidence and the argument at your own pace.

## I am trying to reconcile Scripture and what I observe

Start with [what Scripture claims](scripture.md), then distinguish [observations from historical interpretations](methodology.md). The aim is careful understanding, with room for difficult questions.

## I want to examine the evidence

Visit [evidence](evidence.md) and [compare frameworks](compare.md). Ask what was observed, what assumptions connect it to an explanation, and what would discriminate between alternatives.

## I want the full framework

Explore [the WorldModel](worldmodel.md), then follow its [research programmes](research-programmes.md) to their canonical sources.

## I want to challenge it

Begin with [objections](objections.md) and [open problems](open-problems.md). Agreement is not required to participate. Specific criticisms and primary sources make the strongest contribution.
