# Start your journey

You do not need to know the programme names to begin. Start with a question you care about, and follow the evidence and the argument at your own pace. The four paths below are starting points, not fences; most readers end up crossing between them.

## I am trying to reconcile Scripture and what I observe

Start with [what Scripture claims](scripture.md), then distinguish [observations from historical interpretations](methodology.md). Many apparent conflicts trade on that distinction being collapsed rather than examined. The aim here is careful understanding, with room for difficult questions and no requirement to resolve every tension on a first pass.

## I want to examine the evidence

Visit [evidence](evidence.md) and [compare frameworks](compare.md). Ask what was actually observed, what assumptions connect it to an explanation, and what would discriminate between alternatives. A [working glossary](sources.md) is available if a term is unfamiliar or used in an unfamiliar sense.

## I want the full framework

Explore [the WorldModel](worldmodel.md), then follow its [research programmes](research-programmes.md) to their canonical sources. The programme repositories, not this site, are the technical authority on any claim made within them; this site exists to explain and route, not to substitute for them.

## I want to challenge it

Begin with [objections](objections.md) and [open problems](open-problems.md). Agreement is not required to participate. Specific criticisms grounded in primary sources make the strongest contribution, and there is a [public route to raise them](community.md).
