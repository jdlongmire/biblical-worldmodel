# Responses

This section contains focused responses to published arguments, critiques, and evidential claims relevant to the Biblical WorldModel and its research programmes.

Responses are intended to engage the strongest form of an argument, distinguish observation from historical inference, expose the assumptions used by competing reconstructions, and identify evidence that could discriminate among them. A response may identify a genuine problem for a Biblical WorldModel hypothesis. Where it does, that problem is retained as a research constraint rather than dismissed.
