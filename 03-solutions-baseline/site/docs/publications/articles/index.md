# Articles

These articles develop Biblical WorldModel analysis from primary research, public lectures, interviews, and other source material.

An article's use of an external source does **not** imply that the source's author or speaker endorses the Biblical WorldModel, Designed Functional Maturity, or the article's theological conclusions. Each article should distinguish the source's empirical claims, the source author's interpretation, and the WorldModel's own analysis.
