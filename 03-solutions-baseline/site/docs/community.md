# Join the conversation

Substantive disagreement, skeptical questions, source-based criticism, and corrections are welcome. Moderation should concern conduct and relevance, not agreement.

## Public participation

A GitHub account is required to submit an issue. Issues are public; do not include private or sensitive information.

- [Ask a question](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel/issues/new?title=Question%3A%20)
- [Submit an objection](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel/issues/new?title=Objection%3A%20)
- [Report an error](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel/issues/new?title=Correction%3A%20)
- [Propose research collaboration](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel/issues/new?title=Research%20collaboration%3A%20)
- [Read existing discussions in the issue tracker](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel/issues)

## Private contact

A dedicated private-contact route is not active yet. Please do not put private messages into public issues. A form and any embedded discussion service will be introduced through the separate community/contact work package.

## Participation standards

Be specific, cite sources when available, and address arguments rather than people. Spam, harassment, impersonation, malicious links, and disclosure of private information are not welcome. A serious challenge should retain a traceable link to any resulting correction or canonical review.
