# Cosmology

How should observations of the universe be connected to an account of its history? This section will distinguish measurements, explanatory assumptions, initial-condition proposals, and remaining tests.

The cosmology programme summaries and their current dispositions are still being prepared for this site. Begin with [methodology](methodology.md) and the [research guide](research-programmes.md); no unsettled proposal is promoted here as a demonstrated explanation.
