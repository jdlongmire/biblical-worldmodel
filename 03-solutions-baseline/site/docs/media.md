# Narrated explainers

Narrated introductions and guided visual explanations are planned. No overview video has been published on this site yet, and no publication date has been set.

## What is planned

Future videos will derive from approved pages and graphics, with source revisions, captions, and transcripts retained. A media artifact must preserve the uncertainty and status of its source content, applying the same distinctions this site makes in text: observation from interpretation, tested result from open problem.

## Visual identity

When narrated content lands, it will carry the same visual identity used across this site rather than introducing a separate look for video.

In the meantime, [explore the WorldModel](worldmodel.md) or [begin a reading journey](start-here.md).
