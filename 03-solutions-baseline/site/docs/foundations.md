# Foundations of reality

Questions about logic, information, action, and actuality sit beneath many of the explanations we use.

[Triadic Reality Theory](https://github.com/jdlongmire/triadic-reality-theory) and [Logic Realism Theory](https://github.com/jdlongmire/logic-realism-theory) are canonical sources for the foundational programmes represented here. Their own definitions, arguments, formal results, and limitations control their technical claims.

This site will provide introductory explanations without making an analogy stand in for a proof.
