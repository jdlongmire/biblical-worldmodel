# Sources and glossary

## Source discipline

Prefer primary sources. Distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis, and demonstrated result. Cite the source that actually supports a claim, and preserve its limits.

Programme repositories are listed in the [research guide](research-programmes.md). They retain authority over technical claims; this site translates and routes them for readers.

## Working glossary

**Observation:** a recorded feature or measurement of the world.

**Inference:** a conclusion drawn from evidence using stated reasoning and assumptions.

**Historical reconstruction:** a proposed account of past events connecting a model to present observations.

**Canonical programme source:** the repository that owns a programme's technical claims and current research status.

**Auxiliary hypothesis:** a supporting proposal that can be revised or rejected without silently changing every foundational commitment.

**Falsifier:** a result that contradicts a specified claim under its stated testing conditions. Whether a particular result meets those conditions must be argued, not assumed.
