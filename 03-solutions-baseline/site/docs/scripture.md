# What does Scripture claim?

Scripture is the theological starting point of this project. That commitment does not make every proposed scientific mechanism a biblical claim.

As this section develops, it will distinguish the text, its interpretation, and the explanatory models proposed to connect biblical history with observations. Those layers should be examined separately.

## Questions to bring

- What does the passage actually say in context?
- Which conclusions are textual, and which depend on an interpretive framework?
- Which physical mechanisms remain hypotheses?
