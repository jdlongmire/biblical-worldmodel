# Explore the Biblical WorldModel

This site brings several questions into one reader-facing framework: what Scripture claims, what we observe, how we reconstruct history, and what foundations our explanations require.

*An orientation graphic, not a physical mechanism or proof. Technical claims remain with the canonical research programmes.*

## Explore a layer

- [Scripture](scripture.md): theological commitments and interpretation.
- [Biology and human origins](biology.md): life, humanity, and explanatory questions.
- [Earth history](earth-history.md): geological history and catastrophic reconstruction.
- [Cosmology](cosmology.md): the universe and its history.
- [Foundations](foundations.md): logic, ontology, and the structure of explanation.
