# Earth history and the Flood

This section will examine geological observations and proposed catastrophic historical reconstructions, distinguishing biblical claims from specific geophysical hypotheses.

The canonical source for the programme linked here is [Catastrophic Hydrotectonic Flood Model](https://github.com/jdlongmire/catastrophic-hydrotectonic-flood-model). Its mechanisms, calculations, tests, and unresolved problems must be evaluated from that source.

A full public overview is in preparation. This page does not present an unverified mechanism as an established result.
