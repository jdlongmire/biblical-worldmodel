# Objection / response register

This register retains substantive objections in their strongest useful form. An objection is not removed merely because BWM has a response. Where the response leaves a technical, evidential, or exegetical burden, that burden remains visible and should be routed to the canonical programme that owns it.

## Dispositions

**Answered** means the objection, as stated, rests on a distinction, premise, or inference that BWM can presently answer. It does not imply that every associated technical question is solved.

**Burden remains** means the objection does not presently defeat the target claim, but it identifies a genuine explanatory burden that still requires research.

**Unresolved** means BWM does not presently have a satisfactory response and the objection remains evidence against the relevant claim or model.

## BWM-OBJ-003 — "Trickster God," Last Thursdayism, and distant supernovae

### Strongest form

If God can initialize a young cosmos with states that conventionally imply a long prior history, there appears to be no principled difference between creation thousands of years ago and creation five minutes ago with memories and records preloaded. The objection becomes sharper for distant astronomical transients because a supernova is not merely a static mature object. Its radiation can encode a temporally ordered causal process: stellar collapse, explosive nucleosynthesis, expanding ejecta, changing spectra, neutrino emission, and subsequent remnant development. If those stages never occurred, photons that encode them would constitute fabricated chronicle. If they did occur, DFM must explain how information from a real event conventionally assigned a long propagation history is present within a young chronology. An unconstrained appeal to initialization, or observation-specific changes to physical constants, would undermine empirical trust and explanatory power.

### Response

DFM does not grant an unrestricted right to populate the creation boundary with arbitrary pseudo-history. It distinguishes **functional maturity** from **fabricated chronicle**.

A mature Adam requires the anatomy and cognition necessary to function as an adult. He does not require false childhood memories, scars from accidents that never occurred, or documentary evidence of nonexistent parents. Likewise, a mature created system may require initialized structure without thereby requiring invented records of particular events that never happened.

The virtual-world analogy exposes the inference at issue. A developer can initialize a functioning virtual world with mature mountains, forests, rivers, or stars without deceiving its inhabitants. A scientist inside that world might correctly determine how long an observed tree would take to grow under runtime conditions while incorrectly inferring that this runtime developmental history produced the initialized tree. The measurement can be sound while the historical reconstruction fails because its assumed initial condition is wrong.

The supernova case therefore requires a refinement. Unlike Adam's mature anatomy, a supernova observation contains information about a **real temporal causal sequence**. DFM should not answer this by treating the signal as merely another static mature state. If the received information represents a supernova, the event and the represented causal sequence must be treated as real unless an independent argument establishes otherwise.

The key distinction is between **event duration and propagation duration**. A supernova's collapse, explosion, nucleosynthesis, neutrino emission, spectral evolution, and early ejecta dynamics do not themselves require the full source-observer light-travel time conventionally assigned to the observation. Much of the long inferred interval is the signal's propagation history between source and observer.

DFM therefore accepts the reality of the event sequence while questioning a further inference: that the present source-observer separation, present cosmic geometry, and present propagation regime may be extrapolated unchanged through the creation boundary so that the runtime quantity inferred from present distance necessarily equals the actual historical duration of initialization.

This is not permission to change constants observation by observation. A viable DFM cosmology must supply a **single, independently motivated and generally applicable creation-boundary model**. It must preserve real causal ordering and information while accounting for the establishment of cosmic geometry and propagation relationships. An ad hoc adjustment introduced only when an observation conflicts with a young chronology would count against the model.

Thus the relevant alternatives must not be collapsed:

1. **Fabricated chronicle:** information represents an event that never occurred.
2. **Functional/causal initialization:** the event, its temporal causal sequence, and its physically relevant consequences are real and integrated into the initialized created system; the disputed issue is the propagation duration assigned by backward extrapolation through the boundary.
3. **Runtime reconstruction:** both the event and the entire source-observer propagation history occurred under the ordinary post-initialization geometry and propagation regime inferred from present conditions.

DFM rejects (1). It proposes that (2) is a legitimate creation-boundary category and denies that (3) may simply be assumed.

### Disposition

**Answered as a philosophical/category objection; technical burden remains.**

The "trickster" conclusion does not follow merely from functional initialization. It requires the additional premise that a mature initialized state falsely represents events that never occurred, or that God was obligated to initialize creation only through states reachable by ordinary runtime development.

However, distant starlight and other history-shaped observations remain real research constraints. DFM owes a physically coherent account of how real astronomical events, their temporally ordered causal processes, their products, and their information-bearing radiation are integrated across the creation boundary. "Initialization" by itself is not that account.

The resulting constraint is explicit: **real temporal sequence does not by itself establish the full retrodicted propagation duration, but DFM must explain that propagation without fictional events or observation-specific changes to physical law.**

### Loss condition

If DFM can preserve a young chronology only by repeatedly assigning otherwise contrary observations to unconstrained fabricated chronicle, creating information-bearing photons for events that did not occur, or changing physical constants or propagation rules ad hoc for individual observations, then this objection succeeds against that use of DFM. A viable boundary model must be coherent, generally applicable, independently motivated, and capable of discriminating tests.
