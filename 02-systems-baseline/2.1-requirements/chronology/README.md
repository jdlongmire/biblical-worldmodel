# Chronology Requirements

Purpose: canonical BWM constraints governing chronological claims, age interpretation, mortality chronology, and related historical dating rules.

This surface owns accepted normative chronology constraints. Exploratory chronology models, evidence ledgers, and research mechanisms remain in work packages or Integration until disposition.

## Canonical chronology terminology

BWM uses **retrodictive age** for a past time or elapsed duration inferred by applying a chronological model to present measurements. **Extrapolated age** is the preferred plain-language gloss.

BWM distinguishes:
- measured present state;
- retrodictive age (`t_R`);
- actual elapsed age (`t_H`);
- inherited/system-state age;
- initialization state (`S_0`).

A reported model-derived age is not itself a direct observation. The evidential chain from measurement through model transformation to historical interpretation must remain visible.

The phrase **apparent age** is deprecated as the preferred formal term for model-derived chronology. It may remain in quotations, literature-specific terminology, or non-chronological uses such as apparent maturity.

WP-BWM-0014 is the current candidate source for human mortality chronology and chronological-contamination requirements. WP-BWM-0022 records the terminology decision and repository cleanup.
