# BWM Epistemic Governance Requirements

Status: Canonical requirements
Source: `WP-BWM-0009`

## BWM-EPI-001 — Dependency propagation

Evidence shall bear first on the exact claim tested. Upward consequence requires demonstrated dependency.

## BWM-EPI-002 — No post-hoc demotion

A claim previously treated as indispensable or materially load-bearing shall not be reclassified as dispensable only after failure.

## BWM-EPI-003 — Symmetry

Competing frameworks shall receive equivalent evidential treatment at equivalent claim levels unless a documented dependency justifies asymmetry.

## BWM-EPI-004 — Precommitment

Where practical, severe or discriminating tests shall record expected results, dependencies, consequences of failure, and escalation thresholds before results are interpreted.

## BWM-EPI-005 — Cumulative pressure

Repeated lower-level failures may escalate when a precommitted threshold is crossed, viable alternatives are exhausted, indispensable claims fail, or rescue assumptions accumulate without explanatory gain.

## BWM-EPI-006 — Observation/inference separation

Observation, operational measurement, model interpretation, historical retrodiction, and actual historical event shall remain distinct categories.

## BWM-EPI-007 — Unresolved means unresolved

An unexplained anomaly shall not be represented as a solved mechanism.

## BWM-EPI-008 — Human disposition authority

Only an authorized human Operator may confer or change canonical BWM status within the Operator's recorded scope.
