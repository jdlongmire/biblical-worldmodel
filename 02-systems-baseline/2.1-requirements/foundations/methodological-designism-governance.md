# BWM Methodological Designism Governance Requirements

Status: Candidate requirements  
Source: `WP-BWM-0030`  
Canonical effect: none until Principal Operator disposition

## BWM-MD-001 — Layer separation
BWM shall distinguish ontology, epistemology, hermeneutics, methodology, research programmes/frameworks, models, hypotheses, and observations.

## BWM-MD-002 — Scripture and interpretation
BWM shall distinguish the authority of Scripture from fallible human interpretation of Scripture.

## BWM-MD-003 — Creation as evidence
Creation shall be treated as genuinely evidential. Nature shall not be treated as self-interpreting.

## BWM-MD-004 — Word and perceived counsel
A claimed inward counsel shall not override Scripture. Apparent conflict shall trigger reevaluation of discernment or interpretation.

## BWM-MD-005 — Hermeneutical admissibility
A reinterpretation proposed under empirical pressure shall be independently defensible from grammatical, historical, literary, canonical, and theological evidence. Empirical convenience alone shall not establish an admissible reading.

## BWM-MD-006 — Design constraint
Design may be an admissible explanatory category, but shall not function as an unconstrained explanation. Design-based proposals shall state the constraints, expectations, or discriminators they contribute.

## BWM-MD-007 — Methodological naturalism
BWM shall not treat methodological naturalism as a definitional requirement of scientific method or as a worldview-neutral falsification criterion.

## BWM-MD-008 — Protective-belt discipline
Local failure shall bear first on the claim generating the failed expectation. Protective-belt revision shall comply with BWM-EPI dependency, no-post-hoc-demotion, and cumulative-pressure requirements.

## BWM-MD-009 — Positive research yield
Retained models shall be evaluated for research yield through novel constraint, discriminating empirical performance, explanatory integration, and demonstrated error correction.

## BWM-MD-010 — Degeneration
A model or programme shall be marked DEGENERATING when auxiliary repair accumulates primarily to preserve the conclusion without corresponding predictive, explanatory, integrative, or constraining gain.

## BWM-MD-011 — Research states
Applicable model and hypothesis ledgers shall distinguish SUPPORTED, LIVE, DEGENERATING, and REJECTED research states from canonical Operator dispositions.

## BWM-MD-012 — Loss conditions
Before severe discriminating tests where practical, model records shall state what result would materially weaken, reject, or force revision of the tested claim and identify affected parent dependencies.

## BWM-MD-013 — Intra-programme Bayes
Bayesian comparison within a governing frame may condition on that frame, but priors and likelihoods for admissible subordinate models shall remain exposed to evidential correction.

## BWM-MD-014 — Inter-programme comparison
Whole-programme comparison shall not establish victory by assigning the preferred core certainty and rivals impossibility by definition.

## BWM-MD-015 — Framework disagreement
A framework-level exclusion rule alone shall not be represented as empirical falsification of a rival model.

## BWM-MD-016 — Symmetry
Materially equivalent evidential and methodological standards shall be applied to BWM and competing programmes unless an independently documented reason justifies asymmetry.

## BWM-MD-017 — Noetic effects
BWM shall distinguish noetic impairment from individual culpability and shall not infer dishonesty, rebellion, or culpable suppression solely from disagreement with BWM.

## BWM-MD-018 — Unresolved mechanisms
Invoking design, providence, catastrophe, initialization, or another high-level explanatory category shall not permit an unknown subordinate mechanism to be reported as solved.

## BWM-MD-019 — Initial and boundary conditions
BWM shall identify material initial and boundary conditions in both its own models and rival models and shall not treat initialized state as uniquely objectionable without a criterion applied symmetrically.

## BWM-MD-020 — External programme authority
TRT, LRT, CHFM, and other externally authoritative programmes shall be consumed through BWM interfaces. BWM methodology may impose interface/conformance requirements without silently forking their source-of-truth research.

## BWM-MD-021 — Human canonical authority
Research-state classification may be proposed analytically; canonical acceptance, rejection, retirement, or supersession remains subject to authorized human Operator disposition under BWM-EPI-008.
