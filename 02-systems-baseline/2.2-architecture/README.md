# Publication and canonical architecture

The intended implementation is MkDocs Material under `03-solutions-baseline/site/`, built by GitHub-hosted Actions and published through GitHub Pages. Static-site content, responsive assets and future media metadata are product-owned. Canonical research and Atlas ownership remain external where explicitly designated.

## Canonical BWM architecture artifacts

- [`bwm-component-architecture.md`](bwm-component-architecture.md) — governing BWM component architecture: Foundations, historical intervals/boundaries/hypotheses, World Domains, and Integration.
- [`integration/framework-domain-matrix.md`](integration/framework-domain-matrix.md) — cross-cutting matrix separating source type from BWM role across programme interfaces, hypotheses, comparison families, and world domains.
- [`BWM-CANON-0001-worldmodel-interpretive-families.md`](BWM-CANON-0001-worldmodel-interpretive-families.md) — canonical definitions and distinctions for Designed Functional Maturity (DFM), Designed Time and Emergence (DTE), and Undirected Time and Emergence (UTE). Accepted by Principal Operator disposition on 2026-09-12. Logically classified as Integration; retained at its stable path during the initial restructure.

## Taxonomy surfaces

- [`foundations/`](foundations/) — ontology/metaphysics, epistemology, hermeneutics, and pinned BWM interfaces to external foundational programmes.
- [`historical-frameworks/`](historical-frameworks/) — BWM historical intervals, boundaries, and hypotheses, with external programme research consumed through interfaces.
- [`interfaces/`](interfaces/) — pinned gateway registry for externally authoritative research programmes.
- [`world-domains/`](world-domains/) — subject-matter domains to which foundations and frameworks are applied.
- [`integration/`](integration/) — cross-framework/domain synthesis, chronology, evidence ledgers, model comparison, open problems, predictions and falsifiers.

## Source-of-truth discipline

TRT, LRT, Semantic Actualism, DFM, CHFM, FCD, and CAC remain authoritative in their designated source repositories. BWM consumes them through pinned interface artifacts and does not silently fork their research programmes.

Active hypotheses and unfinished research remain in `04-work-packages/` until explicit Operator disposition and promotion.

Community/contact remains a separate dependency on the public site. External form providers and Giscus remain design inputs, not selected or activated services. ThinxHome is not the deployment backend or CI runner.
