## BWM jurisdiction

LRT supplies the Logos/logic grounding interface used by BWM Foundations. It constrains questions of intelligibility, rational order, logical admissibility, and the relation between foundational logic and actualized reality.

## BWM-adopted interface propositions

BWM may consume LRT for:

- logic as a real constraint on admissible states rather than merely a descriptive human convention;
- the requirement that BWM models remain logically coherent across domains;
- grounding relationships between Logos, intelligibility, and rational order where explicitly sustained by the authoritative LRT source;
- formal results only to the extent actually verified in the pinned LRT formalization.

## Boundary

BWM shall distinguish:

- formally verified LRT results;
- philosophical/theological interpretation of those results;
- TRT/LRT research conjectures;
- BWM-specific theological applications.

No category is promoted into another merely by appearing in this interface.
