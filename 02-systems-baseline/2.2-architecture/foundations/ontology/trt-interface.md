## BWM jurisdiction

TRT supplies foundational ontological structure for BWM, including the roles of logical constraint, information, action/actualization, and actualized reality. BWM consumes TRT as a Foundation rather than treating it as a historical or empirical World Domain.

## BWM-adopted interface propositions

At the interface level, BWM may rely on TRT for:

- reality being intelligible in terms of rational constraint, informational content, and actualization/action;
- distinction between possibility/admissibility and actuality;
- the need to keep foundational ontology distinct from domain-specific physical reconstruction;
- state-transition reasoning relevant to BWM Integration.

These interface statements do not promote every TRT conjecture into BWM's hard core.

## Open/non-canonical material

TRT physics-facing conjectures, gravity reduction, Born-rule work, and other protective-belt developments retain their source-repository confidence/status. BWM shall not silently promote them by reference.

## Dependent BWM surfaces

- Foundations / epistemology;
- DFM state-initialization reasoning;
- Integration / state-transition and explanatory-closure analysis;
- anthropology/consciousness where ontology becomes relevant.
