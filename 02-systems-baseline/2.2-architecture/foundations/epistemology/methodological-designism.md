# Methodological Designism Charter

Status: Candidate canonical methodology  
Source work package: `WP-BWM-0030`  
Disposition required: Principal Operator

## Purpose

Methodological Designism defines the common method by which Biblical WorldModel forms, compares, tests, revises, rejects, and integrates explanatory models.

It sits beneath BWM's ontology, epistemology, and hermeneutics and above domain-specific models:

```text
Christian ontology
  -> revelational epistemology
  -> canonical hermeneutics
  -> Methodological Designism
  -> research-programme governance
  -> frameworks/domains
  -> models/hypotheses
  -> predictions/tests
```

It is a governance layer, not an additional Historical Framework or World Domain.

## Ontological warrant

BWM expects reality to be intelligible because creation is ordered by God through the Logos. Logical order, informational determinacy, causal structure, and purpose are therefore legitimate objects of inquiry.

Design is not introduced only when a naturalistic explanation fails. Design is part of the programme's governing ontology and must itself generate constraints rather than function as an unconstrained explanation.

## Epistemic order

Scripture is the uniquely God-breathed authoritative propositional norm for BWM's biblical-historical claims. Creation supplies genuine evidence. Observation, testimony, instruments, historical records, and valid inference can yield genuine knowledge.

Human interpretation remains fallible. BWM therefore distinguishes the authority of Scripture from an interpreter's claim about Scripture, and the reality of creation from an investigator's model of creation.

The Holy Spirit does not contradict the Word He gave. Human attribution of inward counsel to the Spirit remains fallible and must be tested. A perceived conflict with Scripture is treated as a failure of discernment or interpretation, not a Word-versus-Spirit contradiction.

## Method

BWM permits and expects disciplined use of:

- grammatical, historical, literary, canonical, and theological exegesis;
- observation and operational measurement;
- deductive, inductive, abductive, and historical inference;
- model construction and comparison;
- quantitative analysis and Bayesian reasoning where applicable;
- prediction and risky retrodictive discrimination;
- adversarial testing;
- dependency analysis and precommitment;
- revision, rejection, and explicit retention of unresolved problems.

Methodological naturalism is not a definitional requirement of this method. A model is evaluated by the constraints it accepts, expectations it generates, evidence it explains, risks it takes, and corrections it survives.

## Hermeneutical constraint

BWM distinguishes:

`TEXT -> INTERPRETATION -> MODEL`

The canonical text is not revised by empirical pressure. Interpretations are corrigible where an admissible reading exists.

A reinterpretation proposed under evidential pressure must be independently defensible from grammatical, historical, literary, canonical, and theological properties of the text. Empirical convenience alone does not create an admissible interpretation.

## Research-programme structure

BWM uses a Lakatosian-style distinction between governing commitments and revisable model content.

The hard core establishes the programme under inquiry. The protective belt contains models, auxiliary hypotheses, mechanisms, parameters, historical reconstructions, and other corrigible claims.

The negative heuristic prevents a local anomaly from being promoted automatically into rejection of the whole programme.

The positive heuristic requires the programme to do constructive work: generate constraints, explanations, discriminating expectations, integrations, and tests.

Neither heuristic authorizes unlimited rescue behavior.

## Progress and degeneration

A programme or model is strengthened when it produces novel constraint, survives discriminating tests, increases explanatory integration, or demonstrates meaningful error correction.

It degenerates when auxiliary repair accumulates principally to protect a preferred conclusion without corresponding predictive, explanatory, integrative, or constraining gain.

These are evaluative dimensions rather than a single numerical score.

## Model states

Where applicable, BWM research shall distinguish:

- **SUPPORTED**: presently accounts for relevant evidence and has survived applicable tests.
- **LIVE**: admissible and motivated for investigation, with material questions unresolved.
- **DEGENERATING**: increasingly dependent on rescue assumptions without compensating research yield.
- **REJECTED**: defeated by evidence, incoherence, violation of an established higher constraint, or a superior model under precommitted criteria.

These research states do not replace Operator canonical-disposition states defined by WP-BWM-0009.

## Bayesian discipline

Within a programme, a governing frame may be conditioned upon while competing subordinate models update against evidence:

`P(M_i | E,C) proportional to P(E | M_i,C) P(M_i | C)`.

At the inter-programme level, BWM shall not claim comparative victory by stipulating its own core probability as one or a rival core probability as zero. Whole programmes may instead be compared through coherence, explanatory scope, empirical performance, risky expectations, dependency behavior, correction behavior, and presuppositional/transcendental critique.

## Framework disagreement

Framework disagreement is not model falsification.

Failure must be shown against observation, logical consequence, internal constraint, demonstrated dependency, or a mutually applicable evaluative criterion. A rival's hard-core exclusion rule cannot by itself falsify BWM, and BWM's hard-core commitments cannot by themselves falsify a rival model.

## Noetic effects and culpability

The Fall affects human knowing, but BWM does not infer individual moral culpability from scientific or interpretive disagreement alone.

Finite cognition, incomplete evidence, presuppositional commitments, noetic impairment, and ordinary error can produce false conclusions. Claims of dishonesty, rebellion, or culpable suppression require independent warrant.

## Symmetry

BWM shall expose its models to standards materially equivalent to those applied to rivals. Claimed asymmetries require independent justification.

A rival need not accept BWM's worldview to demonstrate internal contradiction, failed prediction, evidential mismatch, ad hoc repair, or dependency failure within BWM.

## Exemplar discipline

BWM may draw methodological lessons from historical exemplars without treating any exemplar other than Scripture's normative revelational pattern as an independent authority over the programme.

Gödel has two explicitly distinguished methodological interfaces:

- **Incompleteness:** a formal warning against conflating derivability within a sufficiently expressive formal system with exhaustive truth. BWM shall not inflate this result into a generic proof of transcendence or Christianity.
- **Modal ontological argument:** an exemplar of rigorous metaphysical formalization in which axioms, definitions, modal commitments, and inferential dependencies are made explicit and therefore exposed to analysis. Formal validity does not by itself establish the independent truth of the axioms.

BWM shall use Gödel comparatively with LRT and the Logos Transcendental Argument where useful, while keeping formal result, philosophical interpretation, metaphysical premise, and revealed theological claim distinct.
