# BWM Epistemic Hierarchy

Status: Canonical methodology
Authority: Principal Operator disposition, 2026-09-13
Source: `WP-BWM-0009`

## Purpose

Classify claims by epistemic level, identify the actual target of objections and tests, and propagate evidential consequences only through demonstrated dependency.

This hierarchy does not replace the BWM component architecture. Foundations, Historical Frameworks, World Domains, and Integration remain architectural categories.

## Levels

- L0 — Observation
- L1 — Prediction / Test
- L2 — Auxiliary Hypothesis
- L3 — Model / Theory
- L4 — Research Programme / Framework-level explanatory programme
- L5 — WorldModel
- L6 — Worldview

TRT/LRT remain Foundations architecturally. DFM, PFH, and CHFM remain Historical Frameworks architecturally. Particular claims within any component may occupy different L0-L6 levels.

## Dependency rule

An observation, test result, or argument bears first upon the specific claim from which the tested expectation follows. Consequences propagate upward only according to demonstrated dependency.

A failed auxiliary does not automatically falsify its parent model, framework, WorldModel, or worldview. A failure also cannot be quarantined at a lower level if the parent was previously committed to, or materially dependent upon, that claim.

## Symmetry rule

The same level of failure receives the same level of consequence across competing frameworks unless a demonstrated dependency justifies a different result.

## Precommitment rule

Before a severe or discriminating test, where practical, record:

1. exact claim;
2. hierarchy level;
3. expected result;
4. parent dependencies;
5. consequence of failure;
6. competing-framework expectations;
7. escalation threshold, if applicable.

Claims may not be demoted after failure merely to preserve a parent model or programme.

## Cumulative-pressure rule

Repeated lower-level failures may create higher-level evidential pressure. Escalation requires an explicit dependency record and at least one of:

- failure of a previously identified indispensable claim;
- exhaustion of viable alternatives;
- repeated ad hoc auxiliary replacement without increased constraint or explanatory gain;
- crossing a precommitted failure threshold;
- direct contradiction at the parent level.

Thresholds shall not be invented only after adverse evidence appears.

## Two-direction flow

Downward flow is constraint:

`Worldview -> WorldModel -> programmes/frameworks -> models -> hypotheses -> tests`

Upward flow is evidential pressure:

`Observations -> tests -> hypotheses/models -> programmes/frameworks -> WorldModel -> worldview`

## Observation and inference

BWM shall distinguish observation, operational measurement, prediction/test result, model interpretation, historical retrodiction, actual historical event, and higher-level interpretive claim.

Observation is not self-interpreting. This does not weaken observation; it identifies where inference enters the chain.

## Architectural mapping

- TRT/LRT: Foundation; specific claims may occur at L2-L4.
- DFM: Historical Framework; generic framework claims near L4, specific models at L2-L3.
- PFH: Historical Framework; duration/demographic scenarios remain hypotheses/models beneath the framework.
- CHFM: Historical Framework; specific hydrotectonic mechanisms and parameterizations sit at L2-L3.
- World Domains can contain claims at any appropriate epistemic level.

## Objections-ledger minimum fields

- target level;
- exact target claim;
- evidence/argument;
- parent dependency;
- severity;
- current disposition;
- upward consequence;
- precommitment;
- competing-framework comparison;
- Operator disposition when canonical status changes.

## Operator governance

An Operator is an authorized human steward with disposition authority within an explicitly assigned scope. J. D. Longmire is the Principal Operator for BWM.

AI systems may research, draft, classify, critique, test, and recommend. They do not independently confer canonical status.

Disposition states:

- ACCEPT
- ACCEPT-WITH-CONDITIONS
- REVISE
- HOLD
- REJECT
- RETIRE
- SUPERSEDE

## Canonicalization flow

`working artifact -> candidate -> cross-programme review -> symmetry test -> adversarial review -> Operator disposition -> canonical methodology`

## Safeguards

BWM shall not demote failed indispensable claims after the fact, treat convergent methods as independent without testing shared assumptions, label unresolved anomalies as solved mechanisms, protect frameworks with unconstrained rescue assumptions, or apply asymmetric evidential standards without an explicit reason.
