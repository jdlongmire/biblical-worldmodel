# Christian Methodological Designism

Status: Canonical methodology
Authority: Principal Operator disposition, 2026-09-26  
Source: `WP-BWM-0028`

## Purpose

Christian Methodological Designism (CMD) is the BWM methodological posture for disciplined inquiry within an intelligible created order. It governs the relation among authority, evidence, reason, operational investigation, historical reconstruction, and research-programme testing.

CMD does not replace the canonical BWM epistemic hierarchy, hermeneutics, ontology, or domain evidence.

## Commitments

1. Scripture is primary for biblical-historical claims. Christ the Logos grounds truth and intelligibility within the Christian WorldModel.
2. Nature is real evidence and must be investigated honestly. Natural evidence is interpreted evidence rather than a self-interpreting authority.
3. Logic, categorization, mathematics, model construction, and probabilistic reasoning are instruments of inquiry.
4. Stable secondary causes and repeatable operations warrant empirical investigation of the running created order.
5. Operational regularity does not by itself establish that the same dynamics generated an initialization state or were historically traversed across a boundary event.
6. Historical reconstructions must expose hard-core commitments, revisable auxiliaries, expectations, accommodations, failure conditions, and degeneration risk.
7. Competing reconstructions receive symmetric evidential and auxiliary-hypothesis scrutiny.
8. Scientific and historical reconstructions remain revisable where Scripture does not require them.

## Methodological distinctions

CMD distinguishes regularism, secondary-cause inquiry, and methodological naturalism as an admissibility rule.

BWM accepts regularism: nature is sufficiently stable for disciplined investigation.

BWM accepts secondary-cause inquiry: ordinary mechanisms are genuine objects of investigation within the created order.

BWM does not grant a rule excluding purposive agency or revelation from origins explanation worldview-neutral authority merely by methodological definition.

This methodological distinction does not by itself establish any particular historical reconstruction.

## Integration with epistemic governance

CMD consumes the canonical L0-L6 epistemic hierarchy. It adds no parallel hierarchy.

For substantive claims, identify:

`authority/observation -> model + assumptions + interpretation -> expectation/test -> disposition`

Consequences of failure propagate through demonstrated dependency.

## Research-programme discipline

CMD forbids universal explanatory solvents. In particular, BWM shall not use:

- functional maturity to absorb any inconvenient datum;
- emergence to stand in for an unspecified mechanism;
- retrospective auxiliaries without increased constraint;
- post-failure demotion of indispensable claims;
- asymmetric evidential pricing;
- measurement as though it were identical to historical reconstruction;
- teleology as a substitute for unresolved physical or historical explanation.

Canonical methodological maxim:

> Model, assumptions, and interpretation generate expectations; observations test those expectations at the level from which they follow.

## Exemplars

BWM may draw methodological tools from Christ and apostolic Scripture, Van Til, Aristotle, Socrates, Aquinas, Reid, Plantinga, Gödel, Lakatos, Bayes, and other relevant thinkers according to explicit jurisdiction.

Their use does not imply that they independently entail BWM's origins reconstruction or agree with one another on BWM's theological commitments.

Specific historical claims about these thinkers require source verification in the owning research or publication package.
