## Purpose

This canonical artifact defines the distinctions among **Designed Functional Maturity (DFM)**, **Designed Time and Emergence (DTE)**, and **Undirected Time and Emergence (UTE)**.

These are **WorldModel-level interpretive families** concerning:

- the degree of functional maturity present at the relevant starting boundary;
- the amount of subsequent temporal development required to reach the observed world;
- the role of purposive agency and teleology;
- the degree to which calculated developmental histories are taken to correspond to actual elapsed history.

They are broader than individual scientific theories. Each family may contain multiple research programmes, physical models, theories, and auxiliary hypotheses.

## Canonical dimensions

### Initial functional maturity

**Initial functional maturity** is the degree of organized, functioning structure present at the relevant starting boundary.

No family is defined as beginning from absolute metaphysical nothing. Each begins its physical reconstruction with some specified state possessing at least some functional capacity.

### Developmental duration

**Developmental duration** is the amount of actual subsequent history required by a model to reach the observed present state from its starting condition.

### Agency

**Agency** concerns whether purposive mind or intentional direction belongs within the explanatory account of origins and development.

### Historical entailment

**Historical entailment** concerns the degree to which a calculated process history is taken to correspond to actual elapsed history.

A process can be measured accurately without that fact alone establishing that the process produced the entire state being measured.

# 1. Designed Functional Maturity (DFM)

## Definition

**Designed Functional Maturity is the WorldModel interpretation that God may create systems at a high degree of functional maturity, such that their initial physical state need not have been produced by the ordinary developmental processes that would generate a similar state after creation.**

DFM therefore permits a created starting state represented conceptually as:

```text
S0 = G(Φ, L, B)
```

where:

- `S0` = the created starting state;
- `G` = intentional creation / specification;
- `Φ` = appointed functions or intended ends;
- `L` = the lawful structure under which the system subsequently operates;
- `B` = relevant boundary conditions or constraints.

The essential DFM claim is that the created starting state need not lie on the backward trajectory produced by extrapolating ordinary post-creation processes.

Conceptually:

```text
S0 ∉ Range(F_L^(t<0))
```

in the relevant sense, where `F_L` represents lawful post-creation evolution under `L`.

## Core implication

Working backward from a created mature state may calculate a developmental history that did not actually occur.

DFM therefore permits a substantial distinction between:

- **calculated developmental history**, and
- **actual elapsed history**.

## What DFM does not imply

DFM does not imply that everything observed today was present at creation.

After the created starting state, real history occurs under lawful processes. Creation establishes a starting state; it does not erase subsequent physical history.

DFM also does not authorize unrestricted appeal to `God made it that way` for every difficult observation. Candidate initialized features remain subject to scriptural coherence, lawful consistency, explanatory defensibility, and model-level evidential testing.

## Characteristic emphasis

DFM places comparatively greater explanatory weight on **initial specification and functional maturity**, with subsequent history remaining real but carrying less of the total generative burden required to reach the present state.

# 2. Designed Time and Emergence (DTE)

## Definition

**Designed Time and Emergence is the WorldModel interpretation that God intentionally establishes a sufficiently ordered and capable starting state and ordinarily realizes later complexity through genuine temporal development governed, sustained, or providentially directed toward intended outcomes.**

Its characteristic structure is:

```text
S0_DTE → designed/providential development through time → S_present
```

or conceptually:

```text
S0_DTE --F_L,t,agency--> S_present
```

## Core implication

Under DTE, much of the developmental history inferred from present processes is expected to correspond to actual elapsed history.

Stars may actually form through time. Planets may actually form through time. Biological systems may actually develop through time, to the extent specified by the particular DTE model.

DTE therefore assigns greater explanatory work to real temporal development than DFM does.

## Distinction from DFM

The primary distinction between DTE and DFM is **how much functional maturity is present at the starting boundary versus how much is realized through actual subsequent development**.

DFM allows a comparatively rich mature created state.

DTE allows a designed starting state with substantial potential or ordered capacity that becomes progressively actualized through real history.

## Distinction from UTE

The primary distinction between DTE and UTE is **agency and teleology**.

DTE includes purposive agency in the explanatory account. The existence, laws, capacities, initial state, developmental processes, and intended outcomes are ultimately grounded in God.

UTE does not require purposive agency within its explanatory account.

## Scope

DTE is an interpretive family rather than a single denominational or scientific position.

Positions such as old-earth progressive creation or some forms of evolutionary creation may be treated as species of DTE when they fit the defining criteria, but they do not define DTE exhaustively.

## Characteristic emphasis

DTE places explanatory weight on **design expressed through actual temporal development and emergence**.

# 3. Undirected Time and Emergence (UTE)

## Definition

**Undirected Time and Emergence is the WorldModel interpretation that the complexity observed today arises from a comparatively less mature physical starting state through sufficient time and undirected physical processes, without purposive agency being required within the explanatory account.**

Its characteristic structure is:

```text
S0_UTE → undirected physical development through time → S_present
```

or conceptually:

```text
S0_UTE --F_L,t--> S_present
```

without purposive agency as an explanatory primitive within the model.

## Core implication

UTE places substantial explanatory weight on actual elapsed developmental history.

Stars, galaxies, planets, chemistry, biological life, biodiversity, and other complex structures are explained principally through antecedent physical states plus lawful processes operating over sufficient time.

## Starting-state clarification

UTE must not be described as beginning with absolute nothing or literally zero maturity.

Any UTE model begins with some degree of functional capacity, including whatever combination of the following its model requires:

- lawful regularities;
- fields or matter-energy content;
- physical constants and parameters;
- boundary or initial conditions;
- information-bearing physical degrees of freedom;
- an ordered early state sufficient for later structure formation.

The relevant distinction is therefore one of **degree and kind of initial maturity**, not presence versus absence of any starting structure.

## Methodological clarification

UTE should not be used as a synonym for `methodological naturalism`.

Operational scientific work may use methodologically natural procedures under DFM, DTE, or UTE.

UTE names the historical explanatory architecture in which purposive agency is unnecessary or excluded from the account of how later complexity arises from the chosen physical starting state.

## Characteristic emphasis

UTE places explanatory weight on **time plus undirected emergence from a comparatively less mature physical starting state**.

# 4. Canonical comparison

| Dimension | DFM | DTE | UTE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial functional maturity | High | Intermediate / model-dependent | Lower / model-dependent |
| Subsequent development | Real; less generative work required | Substantial | Extensive |
| Purposive agency | Yes | Yes | Not required within the explanatory model |
| Teleology | Intrinsic | Intrinsic / providential | Not required |
| Deep elapsed time | Generally unnecessary to explain initial mature state | Usually accepted in many forms | Generally required |
| Calculated process history = actual history | Often open across the creation boundary | Usually substantially yes | Generally yes, subject to ordinary model qualifications |
| Starting boundary | Rich created state | Designed generative state | Physical initial state |
| Main explanatory emphasis | Initial specification + subsequent history | Design + temporal development | Time + undirected emergence |

The table is comparative rather than exhaustive. Individual models may occupy different positions within each family.

# 5. The families are not a one-dimensional spectrum

DFM, DTE, and UTE should not be treated simply as three points on one line.

Two distinctions are especially important.

## Axis A — Degree of initial functional maturity

DFM and DTE primarily differ over how much functional maturity is instantiated initially versus realized through actual subsequent history.

## Axis B — Agency / teleology

DTE and UTE primarily differ over whether purposive agency and intended ends belong within the explanation.

A simplified conceptual map is:

```text
                 HIGH INITIAL FUNCTIONAL MATURITY
                              │
                              │    DFM
                              │
              DESIGNED ───────┼──────── UNDIRECTED
                              │
                         DTE  │
                              │                 UTE
                              │
                 LOWER INITIAL FUNCTIONAL MATURITY
```

This diagram is illustrative rather than metrically exact.

# 6. Canonical umbrella formulation

> **All three interpretive families begin with some degree of functional capacity. They differ over how much functional maturity is present at the starting boundary, how much is realized through subsequent history, and whether purposive agency belongs in the explanation.**

This formulation should govern public comparisons unless a more specific lower-level model is being discussed.

# 7. Relationship to the BWM epistemic hierarchy

DFM, DTE, and UTE belong at the **WorldModel / interpretive-family level**, above specific research programmes, models, theories, and auxiliary hypotheses.

A particular physical model can fail without automatically falsifying the interpretive family containing it.

Evidence and objections should be classified according to the BWM epistemic hierarchy:

```text
L0 Observation
L1 Prediction / Test
L2 Auxiliary Hypothesis
L3 Model / Theory
L4 Research Programme
L5 WorldModel
L6 Worldview
```

DFM, DTE, and UTE are primarily L5 interpretive families, though particular commitments within them may generate L4–L2 descendants.

Failure propagates upward only according to demonstrated dependency.
