# Earth History and Geology

Type: World Domain

## Scope

This domain owns BWM claims concerning terrestrial physical history and the interpretation of the geological record.

Primary topics include:

- tectonics and crustal reorganization;
- sedimentation and stratigraphy;
- fossilization and paleontological context;
- geochemistry and hydrothermal alteration;
- radiometric systems and geological clocks;
- catastrophic restructuring;
- pre-Flood and post-Flood terrestrial state;
- post-catastrophe normalization.

## Primary framework interfaces

- DFM, for initialized terrestrial state and inherited retrodictive indicators;
- CHFM, for Flood catastrophe mechanisms and immediate terrestrial consequences;
- Integration, for chronology, evidence, and competing reconstruction models.

## Boundary

Earth History is a domain. CHFM is a cross-domain historical framework applied to it. Geological observations and historical reconstructions must remain explicitly distinguished.
