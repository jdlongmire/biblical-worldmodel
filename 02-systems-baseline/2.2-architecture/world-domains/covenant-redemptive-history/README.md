# Covenant and Redemptive History

Type: World Domain

## Scope

This domain owns BWM claims concerning the biblical-historical narrative of covenant, judgment, redemption, kingdom, and consummation.

Primary topics include:

- the Fall and covenantal curse;
- Noahic, Abrahamic, Mosaic, Davidic, and New Covenant structures;
- Israel and the nations;
- Christ and fulfillment;
- Church and mission;
- kingdom and reign;
- resurrection;
- eschatology and consummation.

## Primary framework interfaces

- PFH at the Fall boundary;
- CHFM at the Flood/Noahic transition;
- anthropology for human identity and vocation;
- archaeology/chronology where historical material evidence is relevant;
- Foundations / hermeneutics and epistemology.

## Boundary

This domain ensures BWM remains a full biblical worldmodel rather than only an origins programme. Eschatology remains within this domain unless future content volume justifies separation.
