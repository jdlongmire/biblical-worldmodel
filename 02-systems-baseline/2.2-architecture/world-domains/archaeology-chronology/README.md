# Archaeology and Chronology

Type: World Domain

## Scope

This domain owns BWM claims concerning material human traces, dating systems, chronological reconstruction, and the relationship between retrodictive age and actual elapsed history.

Primary topics include:

- human antiquity;
- tools, structures, habitation traces, symbolic artifacts, and settlements;
- human bodily remains and burial contexts;
- radiocarbon and other dating systems;
- chronological contamination and disturbance;
- archaeological association and provenance;
- distinction between human activity evidence and human mortality evidence;
- distinction between calculated/retrodictive age and actual elapsed chronology.

## Primary framework interfaces

- DFM, for retrodictive limits across initialized states;
- PFH, for potential pre-Fall human activity;
- CHFM, for catastrophic disturbance and terrestrial context;
- WP-BWM-0014-derived mortality chronology requirements;
- Integration, for chronology models and evidence ledgers.

## Boundary

This domain does not independently set scriptural chronology. It evaluates material and dating evidence under BWM's foundational and historical-framework constraints.
