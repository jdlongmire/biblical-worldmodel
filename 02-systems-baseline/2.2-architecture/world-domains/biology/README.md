# Biology and Life Systems

Type: World Domain

## Scope

This domain owns BWM claims concerning living systems, biological organization, and biological history.

Primary topics include:

- created kinds;
- biological information and coding;
- systems biology;
- genomic function and regulation;
- adaptation and variation;
- organismal development;
- abiogenesis and state-transition problems;
- biological maturity and initialization;
- ecology where directly relevant to historical frameworks.

## Primary framework interfaces

- DFM, for initialized biological maturity and retrodictive interpretation;
- PFH, where biology/ecology bears on pre-Fall human history;
- CHFM, where Flood history bears on paleontology, ecology, or biogeography;
- Foundations, especially TRT/LRT and state-transition reasoning.

## Boundary

Biology is a World Domain. It does not independently adjudicate biblical chronology or historical framework boundaries.
