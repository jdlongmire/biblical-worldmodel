## Double-counting control

COS-001 and COS-002 are correlated if the same special-boundary-state explanation is doing the work. They must not be treated as independent full-strength evidence without a demonstrated conditional separation.

COS-003 and COS-005 are primarily indicators of model incompleteness. They do not independently support DFM unless a DFM-specific prediction or discriminator is established.
