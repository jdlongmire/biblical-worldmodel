# DFM vs UTE Biology Subledger

Status: Live Integration subledger
Parent: `dfm-ute-evidence-ledger.md`

## Scope

Compare DFM and UTE on biological functional organization, information-bearing systems, apparent residue, and generative sufficiency.

| ID | Evidence / burden | DFM interpretation | UTE interpretation | Initial status | Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BIO-001 | Pervasive functional organization in living systems | Expected under functionally mature initialization | Expected to varying degrees under selection, constraint, drift, self-organization, and historical accumulation | UNDERDETERMINED | FND-004, FND-005 |
| BIO-002 | Function discovered in features previously labeled nonfunctional | Directionally consistent with DFM's function-first expectation | Often accommodatable through revised evolutionary function/history | WEAK, CASE-SPECIFIC | BIO-001 |
| BIO-003 | Biological information / coding-like organization | Compatible with informationally specified functional systems | UTE must explain emergence and maintenance through physical/biological mechanisms | OPEN EXPLANATORY BURDEN | FND-004 |
| BIO-004 | Abiogenesis state-transition problem | DFM treats life initialization as an actualization boundary rather than a required undirected chemical trajectory | UTE requires a physically sufficient path from nonliving chemistry to self-maintaining evolvable systems | OPEN DISCRIMINATOR PROGRAMME | FND-004, FND-005 |
| BIO-005 | Demonstrably functionless historical residue | Requires explanation under DFM; large systematic classes would pressure naive function-first applications | Naturally compatible with historical contingency and incomplete purging | POTENTIAL UTE DISCRIMINATOR | BIO-001 |

## Double-counting control

BIO-001, BIO-002, and BIO-003 are correlated. A single discovery of function must not be counted separately as evidence for organization, information, and function unless the evidential channels are demonstrated independent.

BIO-004 depends partly on foundational actualization and boundary-state issues. It should not duplicate FND-004/FND-005 as independent evidence.

## Research obligations

1. Predefine biological target classes before claiming predictive success.
2. Distinguish generic compatibility from DFM-specific risk exposure.
3. Specify observations that would favor UTE, including validated residue classes or empirically sufficient abiogenesis pathways.
