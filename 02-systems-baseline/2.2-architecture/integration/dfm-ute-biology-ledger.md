## Interpretation

BIO-002 is provisionally `OE-1` only because repeated discovery of function is directionally consonant with DFM while still broadly accommodatable under UTE. It cannot carry strong weight while highly correlated with BIO-001 and without preregistered target classes.

BIO-005 is deliberately retained as an exposed UTE-favoring discriminator candidate. If mature primary evidence establishes large, well-characterized classes of genuinely nonfunctional historical residue, its ordinal standing could rise materially against DFM biological applications.

No biology item currently qualifies for `OE-2` or `OE-3` because the ledger lacks sufficiently source-grounded likelihood contrasts and independence.

## Double-counting control

BIO-001, BIO-002, and BIO-003 are correlated. BIO-004 partly inherits foundational actualization/boundary burdens and cannot duplicate FND-004/FND-005 as independent evidence.
