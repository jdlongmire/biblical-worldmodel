## Interpretation

No Earth-history entry currently warrants directional `OE-2` or `OE-3` treatment. The dominant reason is that each item still requires case-level primary-source characterization of measurement, closure, event identity, and alternative state histories.

ERH-003 is the most important adverse discriminator candidate for DFM. If systems can be independently established as pristine, closed, and historically continuous, and their old ages converge through genuinely independent methods, the evidence may rise to `OE-2` or stronger in favor of UTE against the relevant DFM application.

ERH-004 can favor CHFM/DFM only when disturbance matches a specific preregistered mechanism or pattern. Generic alteration remains `OE-0`.

## Double-counting control

ERH-001 and ERH-003 may be the same evidential chain when concordance is used to infer pristine history. ERH-002 and ERH-004 cannot both receive full weight when they track the same disturbance event.
