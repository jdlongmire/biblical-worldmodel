# DFM vs UTE Earth-History Subledger

Status: Live Integration subledger
Parent: `dfm-ute-evidence-ledger.md`

## Scope

Compare DFM and UTE on terrestrial initialization, geological retrodiction, inherited chronological state, and disturbance/reset interpretations. CHFM-specific Flood mechanisms remain owned by CHFM and are referenced rather than silently imported into DFM.

| ID | Evidence / burden | DFM interpretation | UTE interpretation | Initial status | Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ERH-001 | Concordant radiometric ages | May reflect real elapsed history, initialized state, or later disturbance depending on system evidence; concordance alone does not settle which | Normally supports shared elapsed history where closure and starting-state assumptions are warranted | OPEN / METHOD-DEPENDENT | FND-005 |
| ERH-002 | Discordance correlated with alteration | Supports investigation of disturbance/reset/open-system history rather than naive elapsed-age reading | Also expected under recognized metamorphic, hydrothermal, weathering, or open-system processes | GENERALLY NEUTRAL | none independent yet |
| ERH-003 | Pristine systems with internally coherent old retrodictive ages | DFM must supply principled initialized-state justification if elapsed history is denied | Directly compatible with long elapsed history under continuity assumptions | POTENTIAL UTE DISCRIMINATOR | FND-005, FND-006 |
| ERH-004 | Catastrophe-correlated chronological disturbance | DFM permits inherited/reset state; CHFM may supply a specific Flood-related mechanism where independently supported | UTE allows local/regional disturbance from conventional geological processes | OPEN DISCRIMINATOR | CHFM mechanism dependency |
| ERH-005 | Geological structures requiring long ordinary-rate formation under a specified model | DFM can challenge the extrapolative assumption only where an alternative initialization/catastrophic account is constrained | UTE favors ordinary or variable-rate historical formation if mechanistically sufficient | CASE-SPECIFIC | FND-006 |

## Double-counting control

ERH-001 and ERH-003 may be the same evidential chain when concordance is the basis for calling a system pristine. They must not receive separate full weight without independent evidence of closure and state history.

ERH-002 and ERH-004 are disturbance evidence. CHFM-specific claims receive no additional credit unless the observed disturbance matches a preregistered CHFM mechanism or pattern.

## Research obligations

1. Separate DFM initialization claims from CHFM catastrophe claims.
2. For every dating case, identify the measured quantity, event purportedly dated, closure assumptions, and candidate state-history alternatives.
3. Predefine what pristine-system observations would materially pressure DFM initialized-state interpretations.
