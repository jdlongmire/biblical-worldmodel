# BWM Predictions and Falsifiers Register

Status: Integration governance artifact under WP-BWM-0013
Type: Integration

## Purpose

Define how BWM distinguishes explanatory accommodation from genuinely exposed predictions, discriminators, and falsifiers.

## Required classification

Each entry should be labeled as one of:

- retrodiction;
- prediction;
- discriminator;
- exclusion;
- falsifier;
- severe test;
- exploratory expectation.

## Minimum fields

Each entry should record:

1. claim ID;
2. owning framework/domain;
3. exact predicted or excluded observation;
4. pre-registration date where applicable;
5. measurement/data source;
6. pass/fail criterion;
7. competing-model expectation;
8. result;
9. confidence impact;
10. disposition and provenance.

## Governing rules

- A post hoc fit shall not be relabeled as a prediction.
- A discriminator should separate at least two live models.
- A falsifier must identify an outcome that would materially damage or force revision of the relevant protective-belt proposition.
- Hard-core theological commitments shall not be disguised as empirical hypotheses.
- Failure conditions should be stated before data inspection whenever practicable.

## Cross-framework use

This register may contain entries from DFM, PFH, CHFM, TRT/LRT-facing interfaces, or World Domains, provided ownership and epistemic category remain explicit.
