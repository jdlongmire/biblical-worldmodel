# BWM Predictions and Falsifiers Register

Status: Live Integration register
Type: Integration

## Purpose

Track exposed predictions, discriminators, exclusions, falsifiers, severe tests, and exploratory expectations without relabeling post hoc accommodation as prediction.

## Governing rules

- A post hoc fit shall not be relabeled as a prediction.
- A discriminator must separate at least two live interpretations.
- Failure propagates according to the canonical epistemic hierarchy and demonstrated dependency.
- Hard-core theological commitments are not disguised as empirical hypotheses.
- Failure conditions should be stated before data inspection whenever practicable.
- Unresolved means unresolved.

## Live entries

### BWM-PRED-001 — Functional discovery in apparently nonfunctional biology

Owner: DFM / Biology
Class: exploratory expectation moving toward discriminator
Claim: DFM expects continued discovery of coherent function, regulation, constraint, or system-level role in biological features previously classified primarily as nonfunctional residue, where such function is experimentally accessible.
Competing expectation: UTE can also accommodate discovered function, so individual discoveries are weak discriminators; systematic directional enrichment beyond prior expectations would be more significant.
Failure/revision trigger: a mature evidence base showing that large classes of such features are demonstrably functionless historical residue in ways positively predicted by UTE and difficult to reconcile with functional initialization would pressure the DFM biological application.
Status: OPEN. Requires preregistered target classes and quantitative baseline before strong evidential credit.

### BWM-PRED-002 — Early cosmic structural maturity

Owner: DFM / Cosmology
Class: retrodiction plus forward discriminator candidate
Claim: DFM is compatible with high structural maturity at the earliest observationally accessible cosmic epochs and does not require a long antecedent generative path to produce mature structure.
Competing expectation: UTE cosmologies require sufficient generative closure from antecedent states under their physical models, although model revisions can alter expected formation rates.
Failure/revision trigger: future observations showing a robust monotonic absence of mature structures where DFM-derived cosmological realizations specifically require them would damage those realizations, not automatically DFM as a whole.
Status: OPEN. WP-BWM-0011 owns detailed cosmology harvest and precommitment work.

### BWM-PRED-003 — PFH non-mortality archaeological signatures

Owner: PFH / Anthropology / Archaeology
Class: discriminator candidate
Claim: if an extended PFH interval with substantial human development occurred, evidence may exist of human agency that is chronologically separated from demonstrably post-Fall human death/burial evidence.
Potential signatures: tools, structures, engineered environments, symbolic artifacts, or habitation traces without associated human mortality contexts.
Failure/revision trigger: after a defined archaeological sampling programme, persistent coupling of the earliest credible human-agency signatures with human mortality evidence under independently robust chronology would pressure extended-development PFH hypotheses.
Status: OPEN. Requires operational criteria for human agency and chronology independence.

### BWM-PRED-004 — Human mortality chronology discrepancy mechanism

Owner: WP-BWM-0014 / Archaeology-Chronology
Class: severe-test programme
Claim: for deep-retrodictive dates assigned to genuinely human death/remains that exceed BWM post-Fall mortality chronology, at least one measurable inherited, introduced, altered, mixed, reset, or mis-modelled state variable must account for the excess if BWM-CHR-001 is historically correct.
Failure/revision trigger: repeated high-quality cases in which genuinely human mortality events are independently tied to deep elapsed chronology, with relevant systems demonstrated closed/appropriate and plausible old-bias mechanisms quantitatively excluded, create escalating evidential pressure under BWM-EPI-005.
Status: OPEN. Sima de los Huesos is the initial case.

### BWM-PRED-005 — CHFM correlated disturbance

Owner: CHFM / Earth History / Chronology
Class: discriminator candidate
Claim: where CHFM invokes Flood-related chronological disturbance, disturbance indicators should correlate with independently measurable catastrophic thermal, hydrothermal, geochemical, depositional, or structural effects rather than appearing as unconstrained age exceptions.
Competing expectation: conventional models may predict local disturbance from ordinary geological histories; discrimination requires a CHFM-specific spatial, stratigraphic, or process correlation.
Failure/revision trigger: absence of predicted disturbance signatures, or pristine system behavior where a specific CHFM mechanism requires disturbance, falsifies that protective-belt mechanism.
Status: OPEN. Requires mechanism-specific preregistration from the authoritative CHFM programme.

### BWM-PRED-006 — DFM initialized-state coherence

Owner: DFM / Integration
Class: severe-test principle
Claim: proposed initialized states must form a coherent functional system and cannot be populated ad hoc solely to absorb each contrary observation.
Failure/revision trigger: if DFM applications require mutually incompatible initialized states, arbitrary pseudo-history without functional justification, or unconstrained state assignments that eliminate discriminating risk, those applications should be rejected and cumulative pressure assessed against the framework.
Status: ACTIVE methodological falsifier.
