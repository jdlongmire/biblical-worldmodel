## Framework boundaries

### DFM

Primary historical jurisdiction:

`creation / initialization boundary -> operational created order`

Key domain applications: cosmology, Earth history, biology, archaeology/chronology, and selected anthropology questions concerning initialized human maturity.

### PFH

Primary historical jurisdiction:

`human creation -> Fall boundary`

Key domain applications: anthropology; archaeology and chronology; selected biology/ecology questions bearing directly on pre-Fall human history; covenant/redemptive history at the Fall boundary.

### CHFM

Primary historical jurisdiction:

`pre-Flood terrestrial order -> Flood catastrophe -> immediate post-Flood terrestrial state`

Key domain applications: Earth history; archaeology/chronology; paleontology and biology; anthropology where survival/dispersal intersects the Flood; covenant/redemptive history at the Noahic transition.

## Foundational reach

TRT, LRT, Semantic Actualism, epistemology, and hermeneutics are not domain models. Their matrix entries indicate constraint or grounding relevance, not empirical explanatory ownership.

## Domain independence

A world domain does not belong to one framework exclusively.

Examples:

- Earth history may require both DFM and CHFM.
- Archaeology/chronology may require DFM, PFH, CHFM, and post-Fall mortality constraints.
- Anthropology may require DFM for created maturity, PFH for pre-Fall development, CHFM for Flood-era survival/dispersal, and covenant history for post-Fall theological identity.

## Integration obligations

For each substantial BWM claim, integration should be able to answer:

1. Which foundational commitments constrain the claim?
2. Which Historical Framework owns the relevant interval or transition?
3. Which World Domain supplies the empirical or disciplinary evidence?
4. Is the claim explicit Scripture, necessary inference, permissible inference, model hypothesis, empirical proposition, or observation?
5. What competing models exist?
6. What evidence would count against the claim or framework?
7. Does the claim create unresolved tension elsewhere in BWM?

## Architectural relationship

`Foundations -> Historical Frameworks -> World Domains -> Integration`

This expresses dependency of interpretation, not a simple temporal sequence.

Temporal backbone:

`DFM -> PFH -> Fall -> post-Fall/pre-Flood history -> CHFM -> post-Flood history -> covenant/redemptive history`
