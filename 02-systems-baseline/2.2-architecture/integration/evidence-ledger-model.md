# BWM Evidence Ledger Model

Status: Integration governance artifact under WP-BWM-0013
Type: Integration

## Purpose

Define how BWM records evidence without conflating observation, interpretation, theological constraint, or model inference.

## Required claim classes

Each ledger entry should be classified as one of:

- explicit Scripture;
- necessary scriptural inference;
- permissible scriptural inference;
- foundational proposition;
- framework proposition;
- domain hypothesis;
- empirical observation;
- empirical measurement;
- model-derived inference;
- unresolved tension.

## Minimum ledger fields

Each entry should record:

1. claim ID;
2. claim text;
3. claim class;
4. owning Foundation, Framework, or World Domain;
5. primary source;
6. secondary source if used;
7. direct observation or derived inference;
8. assumptions/dependencies;
9. competing interpretations;
10. evidence supporting;
11. evidence challenging;
12. confidence label;
13. falsifier or revision trigger where applicable;
14. disposition;
15. provenance commit/source.

## Source hierarchy

For biblical claims, Scripture is primary. For empirical claims, primary technical sources and direct data are preferred. Secondary sources may summarize or route to primary evidence but must not silently replace it.

## Negative evidence

Absence of expected evidence may count only when the model establishes that the evidence should be preserved, observable, and searched for with sufficient sensitivity.

## Cross-domain rule

Evidence imported from one World Domain into another must preserve its original disciplinary meaning, uncertainty, and assumptions.

## Ledger use

The ledger supports:

- framework comparison;
- Bayesian/model-comparison work;
- contradiction detection;
- open-problem tracking;
- promotion decisions;
- public claims traceability.
