# DFM vs UTE Foundational Subledger

Status: Live Integration subledger
Parent: `dfm-ute-evidence-ledger.md`
Scale: `ordinal-evidence-scale.md`

## Scope

Track foundational explanatory burdens beneath empirical domain comparisons. These are not independent empirical evidence items unless a valid bridge to observation is demonstrated.

| ID | Foundational issue | DFM / BWM-side burden | UTE-side burden | Provisional ordinal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FND-001 | Why anything exists | Depends on BWM foundational ontology and Logos grounding | Physical UTE models presuppose an existing reality and do not by themselves explain why anything exists | `OE-NC / I0 / P0 / S3` |
| FND-002 | Why logic is binding | TRT/LRT provide the intended grounding interface | UTE physical models presuppose logical consistency and inferential validity | `OE-NC / I0 / P0 / S3` |
| FND-003 | Why stable laws obtain | BWM grounds lawfulness in created rational order | UTE models use lawful regularity but require a separate account of why regularities obtain | `OE-NC / I1 / P0 / S2` |
| FND-004 | Why information-bearing states are actualizable | TRT/LRT supplies logic-information-action/actualization structure | UTE accounts must specify how admissible physical states become actual through lawful dynamics | `OE-NC / I1 / P0 / S2` |
| FND-005 | Boundary/initial-state justification | DFM must specify a coherent initialized state with principled functional constraints | UTE must justify or explain special initial/boundary conditions not generated by the model | `OE-U / I2 / P1 / S2` |
| FND-006 | Auxiliary-rescue discipline | DFM cannot assign initialized state ad hoc after every challenge | UTE cannot invoke unconstrained unseen mechanisms/parameters solely to rescue fit | `OE-0 / I3 / P3 / S3` |

## Interpretation

FND-001 through FND-004 are foundational comparisons rather than empirical discriminators. They therefore receive `OE-NC` as empirical evidence items. They remain legitimate worldview-level explanatory burdens and may be evaluated philosophically, but they must not be smuggled into empirical totals.

FND-005 is genuinely comparative but remains `OE-U` until the competing models specify what constitutes adequate boundary-state explanation.

FND-006 is a symmetric methodological rule and therefore `OE-0`: both sides are equally exposed to auxiliary-rescue penalties.

## Dependency rule

FND-001 through FND-005 are parent premises for multiple domain arguments. Any domain comparison that depends on them shall reference these IDs rather than count the same foundational burden again as new independent evidence.
