# DFM vs UTE Foundational Subledger

Status: Live Integration subledger
Parent: `dfm-ute-evidence-ledger.md`

## Scope

Track foundational explanatory burdens that sit beneath empirical domain comparisons. These items must not be double-counted as independent cosmological, biological, or geological evidence.

| ID | Foundational issue | DFM / BWM-side burden | UTE-side burden | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FND-001 | Why anything exists | Ultimately depends on BWM foundational ontology and Logos grounding | Physical UTE models presuppose an existing reality and do not by themselves explain why anything exists | FOUNDATIONAL, NON-EMPIRICAL |
| FND-002 | Why logic is binding | TRT/LRT provide the intended grounding interface | UTE physical models presuppose logical consistency and inferential validity | FOUNDATIONAL, NON-EMPIRICAL |
| FND-003 | Why stable laws obtain | BWM grounds lawfulness in the created rational order | UTE models use lawful regularity but require a separate account of why those regularities obtain | OPEN FOUNDATIONAL BURDEN |
| FND-004 | Why information-bearing states are actualizable | TRT/LRT interface supplies logic-information-action/actualization structure | UTE accounts must specify how admissible physical states become actual through lawful dynamics | OPEN FOUNDATIONAL BURDEN |
| FND-005 | Boundary/initial-state justification | DFM must specify a coherent initialized state with principled functional constraints | UTE must justify or explain any special initial/boundary condition not generated by the model | SYMMETRIC BURDEN |
| FND-006 | Auxiliary-rescue discipline | DFM cannot assign initialized state ad hoc after every challenge | UTE cannot invoke unconstrained unseen antecedent mechanisms/parameters solely to rescue fit | SYMMETRIC METHODOLOGICAL RISK |

## Dependency rule

FND-001 through FND-005 are parent premises for multiple domain arguments. Any evidential comparison that depends on them shall reference these IDs rather than count the same foundational burden again as new independent evidence.
