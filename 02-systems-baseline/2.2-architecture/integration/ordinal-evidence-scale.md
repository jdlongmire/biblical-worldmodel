# BWM Ordinal Evidence Scale

Status: Canonical Integration methodology
Type: Integration / model comparison

## Purpose

Provide a disciplined non-numerical evidence scale for comparing live models and frameworks when justified Bayesian likelihoods, priors, or independence assumptions are not yet available.

## Governing principle

Evidence strength depends on discriminatory power, independence, preregistration, measurement quality, and dependency structure. Repetition of correlated evidence does not increase strength as though each item were independent.

## Scale

### OE-0 — Neutral / non-discriminating

Use when:

- both live models expect or readily accommodate the evidence;
- the evidence is shared operational regularity;
- the evidence is merely a restatement of the phenomenon to be explained;
- no likelihood contrast has been established.

Interpretation: no comparative evidential credit.

### OE-1 — Weakly favors

Use when:

- the evidence is directionally more natural under one model;
- competing models can accommodate it without major revision;
- prediction was post hoc or weakly constrained;
- independence or preservation assumptions are only partially established.

Interpretation: modest directional pressure, insufficient to alter framework standing by itself.

### OE-2 — Favors

Use when:

- a clear expectation difference exists;
- the favored model exposed itself to the result before or independently of observation;
- the alternative requires added auxiliary assumptions, parameter movement, or reduced constraint;
- evidence quality is strong and major dependencies are identified.

Interpretation: material comparative evidence.

### OE-3 — Strongly favors

Use when:

- the evidence was preregistered or strongly entailed by one model;
- the competing model assigns substantially lower plausibility absent significant revision;
- measurement and source quality are high;
- the item is largely independent of other counted evidence or correlation is explicitly discounted;
- plausible rescue mechanisms have been tested rather than merely asserted.

Interpretation: substantial pressure on competing model or protective belt.

### OE-X — Exclusion / severe contradiction

Use when:

- an outcome directly violates a precommitted necessary condition, exclusion, or falsifier for the relevant model level;
- measurement/model linkage is strong enough that failure cannot reasonably be moved to a lower dispensable claim;
- dependency is explicit.

Interpretation: local falsification or forced revision at the identified epistemic level. Upward consequence follows BWM-EPI dependency rules and is not automatic.

### OE-U — Underdetermined

Use when:

- evidence is relevant but model expectations are too vague;
- primary-source characterization is incomplete;
- multiple dependencies/confounders remain unresolved;
- alternative explanations are not sufficiently constrained.

Interpretation: no directional score authorized yet.

### OE-NC — Non-commensurable / category mismatch

Use when:

- the compared claims operate at different epistemic levels without a valid bridge;
- a metaphysical claim is being treated as though it were an independent empirical datum;
- the proposed comparison mixes theological authority with operational measurement without an explicit inference chain.

Interpretation: comparison invalid as framed.

## Independence modifier

Every scored item must also receive one independence tag:

- `I3` — substantially independent evidential chain;
- `I2` — partly independent with known shared premises;
- `I1` — strongly correlated/derived from another counted item;
- `I0` — duplicate or derivative; do not count separately.

No ordinal aggregation may treat I0 or I1 items as fully additive.

## Prediction-status modifier

Each item should be tagged:

- `P3` — preregistered/severe prediction;
- `P2` — independently documented prior expectation;
- `P1` — broad prior compatibility/directional expectation;
- `P0` — post hoc accommodation.

Strong claims of evidential force require both prediction exposure and discriminatory contrast. P0 items cannot exceed OE-1 without exceptional independent justification.

## Source-quality modifier

- `S3` — verified primary source/data or direct canonical source;
- `S2` — strong secondary with precise primary traceability;
- `S1` — tertiary/orientation only;
- `S0` — unverified or anecdotal.

S0/S1 evidence should not support OE-2 or OE-3 dispositions.

## Scoring record

Each ledger entry should therefore record at minimum:

`Ordinal judgment + independence + prediction status + source quality`

Example:

`OE-2 / I2 / P2 / S3`

This means: material evidence favoring one model, partly independent, independently predicted before the present evaluation, supported by primary evidence.

## Aggregation rule

The ordinal scale is not arithmetic. Do not add OE values as numbers.

Whole-ledger assessment should consider:

- number of genuinely independent discriminators;
- severity of contradictions;
- direction consistency;
- auxiliary cost;
- dependency structure;
- source quality;
- prediction exposure;
- unresolved counterevidence.

## Symmetry rule

Identical criteria apply to DFM, UTE, DTE, CHFM, PFH hypotheses, and competing conventional models. No model receives a higher ordinal judgment merely because its foundational commitments align with BWM.
