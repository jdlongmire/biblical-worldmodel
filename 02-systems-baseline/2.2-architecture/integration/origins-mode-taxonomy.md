# Origins-Mode Taxonomy

Status: Canonical integration
Authority: Principal Operator disposition, 2026-09-26  
Source: `WP-BWM-0028`

## Purpose

Classify the explanatory role assigned to purposive agency, initialized state, elapsed process, and undirected dynamics at a specified origins boundary.

DFM, DTE, and UTE are **origin modes**, not three peer BWM research programmes.

## DFM — Designed Functional Maturity

A purposive agent initializes a system in a state sufficient for its commissioned operation. Genuine elapsed history follows initialization. Dynamics observed after deployment need not be the process that generated the initialized state.

Within BWM, DFM is instantiated as the creation/initialization Historical Framework.

DFM does not license arbitrary initialized history. Record-like initialized states carry an additional explanatory burden.

## DTE — Designed Time and Emergence

A purposive agent intends an outcome and uses genuine elapsed processes and intermediate stages as means by which the relevant function or form develops.

DTE is a comparative origin mode. It is not an active BWM research programme.

## UTE — Undirected Time and Emergence

The causal reconstruction under comparison includes no directing purposive agency. The target system is explained through admitted initial/boundary conditions, lawful dynamics, stochastic processes, selection effects, and other non-purposive mechanisms.

UTE classifies the causal account under comparison. It does not by itself classify the researcher's total worldview.

UTE is a comparative origin mode. It is not an active BWM research programme.

## Initialization symmetry

Within a specified explanatory domain, dynamics operate on admitted state, law, boundary conditions, or other structure. The comparative question is what structure is admitted, how its presence is explained, what history is claimed to have been traversed, and what explanatory work is assigned to agency, dynamics, and elapsed time.

BWM shall not redescribe every admitted initial condition in a rival model as DFM.

## Hybrid application

Models may combine modes across boundaries, domains, or intervals. Each comparison shall identify:

- target system and domain;
- boundary under examination;
- mode applied at that boundary;
- subsequent historical mode;
- auxiliary hypotheses.

## Comparison

| Axis | DFM | DTE | UTE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginning | designed deployment/initialization | designed commencement of developmental process | admitted boundary/initial state plus non-purposive dynamics |
| Agency | constitutive | constitutive | absent from causal reconstruction |
| Intermediate stages | optional for production of initialized target | constitutive | constitutive where target is dynamically derived |
| Elapsed process | genuine after initialization | means of making | means of making |
| Primary burden | justify initialized content, especially record-like content | justify developmental history and claimed mode of guidance | derive target organization from admitted conditions and dynamics |

Admissibility is not evidential success. A proposed DFM discontinuity must identify the boundary, initialized state, warrant, integration burden, and discriminating consequences.

## Discrimination

Comparison should seek observations for which pre-specified expectations materially diverge. Evidence expected under multiple modes can strongly constrain all of them while having low discriminatory force between them.
