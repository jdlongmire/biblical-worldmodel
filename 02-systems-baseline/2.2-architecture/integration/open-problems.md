## Purpose

Maintain an explicit register of unresolved tensions, missing mechanisms, evidentiary gaps, and cross-framework dependencies.

## BWM-OP-001 — PFH duration and Genesis 5 age accounting

- Affected components: PFH; Anthropology; Archaeology and Chronology
- Problem: Does Genesis permit an extended interval between human creation and the Fall, and does Genesis 5 count Adam's total existence or specifically mortal/post-Fall age?
- Why it matters: This controls whether deep pre-Fall human history is biblically admissible.
- Current evidence: Genesis does not explicitly state a creation-to-Fall duration; Genesis 5:3–5 gives Adam's age at Seth's birth and death.
- Competing interpretations: total-lifespan reading versus post-Fall/mortal-zero-point hypothesis.
- Resolution condition: focused canonical/exegetical analysis sufficient to classify the age-accounting zero-point.
- Responsible source: WP-BWM-0012 / PFH
- Status: OPEN

## BWM-OP-002 — Pre-Fall reproduction and demographic expansion

- Affected components: PFH; Anthropology; Biology
- Problem: Does the pre-Fall Genesis 1:28 command imply actual reproduction and population expansion before the Fall, or only capacity and commission?
- Why it matters: Extended PFH archaeology requires more than Adam and Eve if pre-Fall cultural expansion is proposed.
- Current evidence: Genesis 1:28 command is pre-Fall; explicit named offspring appear in the post-Fall narrative.
- Resolution condition: exegetical case distinguishing command, capability, and recorded fulfillment.
- Responsible source: WP-BWM-0012 / PFH
- Status: OPEN

## BWM-OP-003 — Radiocarbon horizon and PFH correlation

- Affected components: PFH; Archaeology and Chronology
- Problem: Is the approximately 50–60 ka practical radiocarbon horizon meaningfully related to the open PFH interval, or is the similarity merely coincidental?
- Why it matters: It is the principal proposed empirical anchor for the current extended-PFH hypothesis.
- Current evidence: radiocarbon dating loses practical resolving power in this range; BWM does not treat the method horizon itself as elapsed chronology.
- Resolution condition: primary-source method characterization plus a non-circular reason to connect the horizon to a biblical-historical transition.
- Responsible source: WP-BWM-0012
- Status: OPEN; correlation confidence LOW

## BWM-OP-004 — Human remains with deep conventional ages

- Affected components: Archaeology and Chronology; PFH; BWM-CHR-001/002
- Problem: Conventionally deep-dated human remains, including Sima de los Huesos, conflict with the BWM human-mortality chronology constraint if the specimens are genuinely human.
- Why it matters: This is a direct empirical pressure point on BWM chronology.
- Current evidence: multiple dating methods and stratigraphic arguments produce ages far beyond current post-Fall chronology.
- Competing interpretations: conventional elapsed age; inherited/disturbed state; method-specific chronological contamination; taxonomic dispute.
- Resolution condition: method-by-method decomposition with independently testable mechanisms or a revision of the relevant BWM classification/constraint.
- Responsible source: WP-BWM-0014
- Status: OPEN / UNRESOLVED CHRONOLOGICAL CONTAMINATION

## BWM-OP-005 — CHFM chronology disturbance mechanisms

- Affected components: CHFM; Earth History; Archaeology and Chronology
- Problem: Which dating systems, if any, can CHFM quantitatively demonstrate were reset, disturbed, inherited, or mixed by Flood processes?
- Why it matters: CHFM cannot function as a generic explanation for discordant ages without mechanism-specific evidence.
- Resolution condition: system-specific physical models with measurable consequences and falsifiable bounds.
- Responsible source: authoritative CHFM repository
- Status: OPEN

## BWM-OP-006 — DFM initialized-state constraints

- Affected components: DFM; Cosmology; Earth History; Biology; Chronology
- Problem: What principled constraints distinguish functionally necessary initialized maturity from arbitrary pseudo-history?
- Why it matters: Without constraints, DFM risks becoming explanatorily unconstrained.
- Current rule: initialized state must be coherent with commissioned function, lawful operation, and independent explanatory constraints.
- Resolution condition: stronger formal criteria plus discriminating predictions or exclusions.
- Responsible source: authoritative DFM repository
- Status: OPEN

## BWM-OP-007 — Framework boundary interactions

- Affected components: DFM; PFH; CHFM; Integration
- Problem: Some observations may encode effects from initialization, ordinary pre-Fall history, Fall-related change, Flood catastrophe, and later normalization simultaneously.
- Why it matters: Misassigning a discrepancy to the wrong boundary creates circular explanations.
- Resolution condition: explicit event/boundary provenance in chronology and evidence ledgers.
- Responsible source: Integration
- Status: ACTIVE governance problem
