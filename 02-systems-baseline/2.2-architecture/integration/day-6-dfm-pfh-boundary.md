# Day 6: Deployment, Commissioning, and the Human Runtime Boundary

## Purpose

This note defines the BWM interface between the Sixth Day material, Designed Functional Maturity (DFM), and Pre-Fall History (PFH).

## Canonical relationship

Genesis 1:24–31 gives the Day 6 creation sequence at high resolution relative to the creation week as a whole. Genesis 2:7–25 gives increased resolution on the human events within that same canonical setting.

Within BWM, the controlling sequence is therefore:

```text

        |
        v

        |
        +-- terrestrial animal population
        +-- Adam formed and given life
        +-- vocation and moral boundary
        +-- creaturely encounter and naming

        +-- marriage
        +-- joint human blessing and dominion commission
        |

Completed Day 6 state: "very good"
        |
        v

        +-- Fall / covenantal breach
```

## DFM representation

DFM WP-022 represents the sixth phase as the cumulative transition:

```text
S_5 --G_6--> S_6
```

`S_5` is the inherited state after Day 5. `G_6` is the supernatural Day 6 creative/deployment phase. `S_6` is the completed creation state after Day 6.

Genesis 2 can be represented analytically as a decomposition of the human portion of `G_6`. This is an analytical abstraction. Genesis itself does not use systems-engineering vocabulary.

After commissioning, ordinary history proceeds from the initialized state:

```text
S_6 --F_L^t--> S_6(t)
```

This expresses the DFM distinction between provenance and subsequent operation. It does not reduce human agency, image-bearing, moral responsibility, or divine providence to physical dynamics.

## Three boundaries

### 1. Creation / deployment

God creates humanity. Adam's formation from dust and receipt of the breath of life, followed by Eve's derivation from Adam, belong to divine creative action.

### 2. Commissioning

Humanity is placed into an immediately meaningful order and given a vocation. Adam receives work and prohibition. The naming episode exercises differentiated cognition and language. Eve is brought to Adam. Marriage is constituted. Male and female receive blessing, reproduction, stewardship, and dominion.

Day 6 therefore functions as the **commissioning boundary of the human office**.

### 3. Historical exercise

The commands to be fruitful, multiply, fill, subdue, cultivate, keep, and exercise dominion are prospective. Their fulfillment occurs after commissioning.

PFH concerns this post-commissioning, pre-Fall historical interval. Scripture does not state its duration in Genesis 1–2. The existence of a commissioned vocation establishes an interval of possible exercise; it does not establish how long that interval lasted.

## Human functional maturity

The Day 6 account constrains DFM's concept of human functional maturity more strongly than a generic claim that Adam possessed an adult body.

Adam is immediately represented as capable of divine-human communication, propositional comprehension, moral responsibility, purposeful work, classification, naming, relational recognition, and covenantally significant obedience or disobedience.

Accordingly, a DFM model of Adam must include sufficient initialized capacity for those functions.

A further distinction remains open:

```text
initialized capacity != exhaustive initialized knowledge
```

Genesis permits genuine experience and learning within Day 6. The naming episode itself appears to move from God's prior declaration that Adam's solitude is not good toward Adam's experiential recognition that no corresponding helper is found among the creatures. The text therefore supports both designed functional readiness and genuine creaturely discovery.

## Relation to the naming chronology

The quantitative model in `sixth-day-naming-model.md` addresses a narrow objection: whether the narrated Genesis 2 events are chronologically impossible within an ordinary Day 6.

Its five-hour naming interval and kind counts are model parameters. They demonstrate feasibility under stated assumptions. They do not define the internal schedule of `G_6`.

The architecture therefore preserves the result while preventing the model from hardening into revealed chronology.

## Claim boundary

BWM affirms the textual sequence and treats the DFM representation as a subordinate analytical model. It does not claim that:

- Genesis is an engineering run book;
- systems terminology appears in the Hebrew text;
- the text specifies the physical mechanism of supernatural initialization;
- Adam possessed exhaustive propositional knowledge at creation;
- the naming episode establishes an exact duration;
- Day 6 itself establishes the duration of pre-Fall history.

## Cross-programme rule

BWM owns the canonical scriptural and world-model boundary. DFM owns the technical research programme concerning phased supernatural initialization, functional state depth, ordinary operation, and provenance. PFH owns hypotheses concerning human history after commissioning and before the Fall.

The interface is:

```text
BWM textual boundary
      |
      v
DFM Day 6 deployment model
      |
      v
Day 6 human commissioning boundary
      |

```

This ordering prevents circularity. PFH cannot be used to rewrite the Day 6 textual boundary, and DFM cannot use its own deployment analogy as independent proof of the scriptural history it is designed to model.
