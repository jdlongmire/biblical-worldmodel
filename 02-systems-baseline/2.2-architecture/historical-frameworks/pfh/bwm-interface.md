# Pre-Fall History (PFH) BWM Interface

Status: BWM interface candidate under WP-BWM-0013
Type: Historical Framework
Source work package: `WP-BWM-0012`

## Historical jurisdiction

`human creation -> pre-Fall human history -> Fall boundary`

PFH is the BWM framework for questions belonging to the human-creation-to-Fall interval. The extended pre-Fall development model is an initial PFH hypothesis, not the definition of PFH.

The Day 6 starting condition is developed in [Day 6 Commissioning Boundary](day-6-commissioning-boundary.md) under `WP-BWM-0018`. That note treats Day 6 as the inauguration of the human office while leaving the elapsed duration before the Fall open.

## Governing epistemic boundary

Scripture is primary. Nature supplies secondary evidence that may constrain open variables without overriding explicit scriptural constraints.

## Current BWM interface propositions

PFH currently carries these architectural propositions:

- Scripture does not explicitly state an elapsed duration between human creation and the Fall;
- Day 6 establishes humanity's vocation, moral boundary, marriage relation, blessing, and joint commission, providing the starting condition for PFH;
- the Genesis 1:28 commission is pre-Fall and therefore creates a legitimate research question concerning pre-Fall reproduction, expansion, stewardship, and development;
- total human antiquity and post-Fall human mortality chronology are distinct variables;
- human death belongs to the post-Fall order under the BWM mortality constraint;
- archaeological evidence of human activity must be distinguished from evidence of human death/burial.

## Open hypotheses

The following remain under investigation in WP-BWM-0012 and are not promoted as settled BWM requirements by this interface:

- an extended pre-Fall duration;
- pre-Fall population expansion;
- substantial pre-Fall migration, settlement, cultural, symbolic, architectural, or technological development;
- Adam's 930 years as specifically post-Fall/mortal age accounting;
- a meaningful relationship between the open PFH interval and the approximately 50–60 ka practical radiocarbon horizon.

## Domain interfaces

Primary:

- anthropology;
- archaeology and chronology.

Supporting:

- biology/ecology where pre-Fall human history depends on them;
- covenant/redemptive history at the Fall boundary;
- Earth history/geography.

## Relationship to mortality chronology

`WP-BWM-0014` governs human mortality chronology and dating conflicts involving human death, bodily remains produced by death, and burial contexts.

PFH governs when/how humans may have lived before the Fall. WP-BWM-0014 governs when humans may have died.
