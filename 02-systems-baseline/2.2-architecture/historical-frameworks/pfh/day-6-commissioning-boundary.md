# Day 6 Commissioning Boundary

Status: candidate integration under `WP-BWM-0018`
Type: PFH integration note
Parent interface: `historical-frameworks/pfh/bwm-interface.md`

## Purpose

Define the relationship between the sixth creation day and the jurisdiction of Pre-Fall History (PFH) without assigning an elapsed duration to the human-creation-to-Fall interval.

## Canonical datum

Genesis 1–2 places the creation of man and woman within the sixth day and associates humanity with vocation, moral boundary, marriage, blessing, reproduction, dominion, and stewardship. Genesis 1:28 commissions the man and woman to be fruitful, multiply, fill the earth, subdue it, and exercise dominion over living creatures.

Genesis 2 develops the human focus: the man is formed, placed in the garden to work and keep it, receives the prohibition concerning the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, encounters the creaturely order brought to him for naming, receives the woman as his corresponding helper, and enters the one-flesh marriage relation.

These propositions are textual claims. A precise intra-day clock schedule is not.

## Architectural proposition

For BWM purposes, Day 6 functions as the **inauguration of the human office**.

The commission establishes activities whose execution is intrinsically subsequent to the giving of the commission: reproduction, filling, subduing, dominion, cultivation, and stewardship. Scripture does not state how much elapsed time occurs between this commissioning and the Fall.

Accordingly:

`Day 6 commissioning -> exercise of the human office -> Fall boundary`

PFH owns investigation of the middle interval.

This formulation does not require an extended pre-Fall interval. It establishes only that the commission precedes its historical exercise and that the duration remains an open variable unless constrained by Scripture or warranted secondary evidence.

## Naming episode and human vocation

Genesis 2:18–23 may be read as a purposeful sequence in which God declares that the man's solitude is not good, the creatures are brought to the man and named, no corresponding helper is found for him, and the woman is then brought to him and recognized as bone of his bones and flesh of his flesh.

A useful narrative model is:

`God knows -> God declares -> Adam experiences -> God provides -> Adam recognizes`

The sequence is an interpretive synthesis, not an inspired formula. Its value is that it explains why the naming episode belongs naturally between the declaration of Adam's aloneness and the presentation of the woman without requiring the text to specify how long the naming took.

## Relationship to PFH hypotheses

This note strengthens one existing PFH interface proposition: the Genesis 1:28 commission creates a legitimate research question concerning pre-Fall reproduction, expansion, stewardship, and development.

It does not promote the following open hypotheses to settled BWM requirements:

- an extended pre-Fall duration;
- a particular pre-Fall population;
- substantial settlement or technological development;
- a 50–60 ka pre-Fall boundary;
- Adam's 930 years as exclusively post-Fall age accounting.

Those remain governed by `WP-BWM-0012` and the PFH interface.

## Relationship to the Fall

The Fall terminates the unfallen administration of the human office and introduces the covenantal breach addressed by Genesis 3. Questions concerning the consequences of Adam's federal headship and the post-Fall mortality order interface with covenant/redemptive history and `WP-BWM-0014`.

The Day 6 commissioning boundary therefore supplies PFH with a defined starting condition while leaving the duration and historical development of the pre-Fall interval open to investigation.
